## 0. Executive decision

The first public website should be a **small, substantive research-and-systems-engineering website**, not a full ecosystem portal and not a product/consultancy funnel.

It must let a first-time visitor:
1. understand the distinctive question TGCV investigates;
2. grasp why the accessible transformations of a system matter;
3. distinguish the research programme from Transformational Intelligence, systems engineering and AI;
4. inspect the current scientific position and its limits;
5. see how the analytical perspective may be explored across different system domains;
6. find a real next step to explore, ask a question or follow the work.

The germinal site must be useful without the future Playground, without a new interactive simulator, without a community platform and without an unvalidated product proposition.

**Recommended first-release scope:** six substantive destinations plus a compact About / Governance and Contact / Participate route. Do not publish a navigation item merely because it appears in the long-term ecosystem map.

## 2. Page-by-page content specifications

### P01 — Home

**Purpose:** deliver the Efecto Ajá, orient the visitor and route them into substantive content.

**Core question:** “How does a change in a system alter what that system can do next?”

**Required content blocks:**
1. Headline and short supporting statement.
2. One clear explanatory visual showing system conditions and a changed set of accessible transformations.
3. The central insight in plain language.
4. A short analytical sequence: system/state and conditions → candidate transformations → accessibility → realised transition → subsequent state/possibilities → trajectory → outcome; value is a separate contextual evaluation.
5. A clear, accessible conceptual ecosystem map distinguishing TGCV, Systems Thinking, Systems Engineering, Transformational Intelligence, Value Construction/VALUE, Labs & Applications and proposed ValueLabOS; show labelled relationships, not a proven causal chain.
6. Direct visitor routes to Explore, Research, Systems Engineering, Transformational Intelligence, Value Construction and Labs & Applications.
7. Two or three carefully selected candidate application contexts where they add explanatory value, plus a real contact/participation route.

**Draft headline for testing:** “Understanding and engineering how systems transform.”

**Supporting copy direction:** “Researching how system conditions shape the transformations available, how those possibilities change, and what follows.”

**Do not:** lead with value creation, AI automation, consulting services, a generic innovation promise, or a Playground that does not yet exist.

**Source basis:** Home Experience v1.5 and Home Copy v1.2, aligned with the current Web Master Brief and Information Architecture. All final copy remains subject to external comprehension testing.

### P02 — Explore

**Purpose:** make the core idea understandable without requiring prior knowledge or mathematical notation.

**Required content:**
- A simple system example with a clearly bounded system and context.
- A before/after comparison where a condition changes.
- Candidate transformations versus accessible transformations.
- A visible opening, closing or reconfiguration example (not expansion only).
- A short explanation of what execution adds to accessibility.
- A successor-state/trajectory illustration.
- A clear warning that accessibility does not guarantee execution, favourable outcomes or value.
- Explain that **Accessibility → Execution → Outcome → Value** is an analytical distinction, not a complete causal model; represent trajectory across states and transformations, not as another stage.
- Links to the formal Research page for visitors who want detail.

**Recommended explanatory sequence:**
1. A system has a current state and operates under conditions.
2. Those conditions affect which candidate transformations are accessible.
3. A change in state/context/constraints may change that accessible set.
4. A transformation may then be realised, changing the system and its future possibilities.
5. Outcomes and value require additional definitions and evidence.

**Visual asset:** one static, accessible diagram or a small set of annotated diagrams. No bespoke interactive simulator in the germinal release.

**Candidate examples:** software agent/tool permissions or a small network configuration are intuitive, but the final example must be checked for simplicity, boundedness and scientific fidelity.

### P03 — Research / TGCV

**Purpose:** present the scientific programme accurately and credibly.

**Required content:**
- TGCV's full name and status as an integrative, falsifiable research programme.
- The current minimal ontological Core (S) and analytical representation of accessible transformations (T_acc) in accessible prose.
- The central phenomenon: changes in the structure of accessible transformations, which may expand, contract, reconfigure or substitute.
- The analytical sequence and distinction between T_acc, ΔT_acc, Reach, Trajectory, Outcome and Value.
- Research questions: identifiability/non-circularity; dynamics; reachability/trajectory; cross-domain translation; outcome/value linkage; limits and falsifiers.
- A short evidence-status summary with links to controlled external assets where approved.
- A selected bilingual evidence portfolio summarising representative empirical, industrial, computational and synthetic cases recorded in the current Evidence-to-Claim Matrix; it must distinguish evidence types and preserve each case's claim boundary.
- Explicit open questions, operational falsifiers and no-claims; explain what observations would support, weaken, restrict or refute specific propositions.

**Recommended source assets:**

- 05_ASSETS/TCP/TGCV-EXT-TCP-001_v0.4.md — current controlled Testable Concept Paper.

### P04 — Systems Engineering

**Purpose:** explain the engineering discipline and practices through which the ecosystem can represent, analyse and work with systems.

**Required content:**
- Systems and subsystems, boundaries, context and interfaces.
- Components, dependencies, constraints, resources and configurations.
- Modelling, architecture, simulation, experimentation and systems integration.
- How system engineering can make conditions and transformation constraints explicit.
- Relationship to TGCV: TGCV's research question can inform what is modelled and compared; systems engineering provides methods/practices for working with real engineered systems.
- A bounded example or diagram, preferably drawn from a candidate application context and clearly labelled.

**Do not claim:** TGCV replaces systems engineering, provides a complete systems-engineering lifecycle, or has a validated proprietary engineering method.

**Source basis:** Master Brief and Information Architecture. Additional authoritative systems-engineering references may be added in a later research/editorial pass; do not invent a methodology from generic descriptions.

### P05 — Transformational Intelligence

**Authoritative working reference:** `06_WEB/TGCV_WEB_TRANSFORMATIONAL_INTELLIGENCE_EXPERIENCE_v0.1.md`.

**Purpose:** explain TI as a developing capability concept and open research hypothesis concerning the use of information about accessible transformations and transformation-space evolution. It is not a validated general capability, established discipline, product or synonym for AI.

**Required narrative and content:**
- Distinguish Transformation-Space Dynamics (observable evolution of accessible transformations), transformation handling (discrimination, selection, coordination and realisation), and the TI capability hypothesis (representing, evaluating, anticipating or adapting behaviour using information about transformation-space structure/evolution).
- Use the visitor-facing working definition in the Experience document. Do not define TI through performance, realised value or successful outcomes.
- Present five candidate dimensions under investigation: representation, evaluation, selection, anticipation and adaptation. They are not validated components or necessary-and-sufficient criteria.
- Explain the illustrative software-configuration migration example without implying execution, implementation or demonstrated TI.
- Distinguish TI from AI, Systems Thinking and Systems Engineering. AI may support some TI-related functions but is not equivalent to TI; TI need not require generative AI.
- Explain the evidence progression from observing transformation-space dynamics to testing sensitivity, actual use, anticipatory/adaptive handling and possible generalisation.
- State open questions about construct differentiation, incremental explanatory contribution, independent measurement and generalisation.
- Preserve the independence of outcome and value assessment. Do not imply TI necessarily produces value.

**Required caveat:** the manifesto is a canonical working manifesto, not proof of a generally validated capability. The Experience's more cautious three-layer distinction and five candidate dimensions govern public copy; earlier four-dimension formulations are not current page authority.

**Source basis:** `00_GOVERNANCE/TGCV_TRANSFORMATIONAL_DYNAMICS_INTELLIGENCE_MANIFESTO_001.md`, `03_EXPERIMENTS/TR-131/execution/TR131_TRANSFORMATIONAL_CAPABILITY_INTELLIGENCE_CONCEPTUAL_SPEC_001.md` and `03_EXPERIMENTS/TR-131/execution/TR131_FORMAL_MAP_TRANSFORMATION_SPACE_DYNAMICS_TO_TRANSFORMATIONAL_INTELLIGENCE_001.md`.

### P07 — About / Governance

**Purpose:** establish identity, openness and credibility without a long institutional biography or exaggerated organisational claims.

**Required content:**

- Interdisciplinary orientation: systems thinking, systems engineering, transformation-space dynamics, modelling and experimentation.

**Purpose:** make a genuine low-friction route available for questions and carefully scoped contributions.

**Minimum launch requirement:**

- A short statement of what is welcome: questions, corrections, candidate cases, methodological discussion and potential collaboration.
- No forced registration.
- Minimal data collection and clear privacy notice.

## 4. First-release evidence and claim controls

### 4.1 Public evidence labels

Use a compact, consistent vocabulary:
- **Concept / working definition** — a defined conceptual proposal; not empirically established by definition.
- **Hypothesis** — a proposition awaiting an appropriate test.
- **Synthetic demonstration** — behaviour shown in a constructed model/fixture.
- **Executed** — a specified operation was run; execution alone does not establish scientific validity.
- **Audited** — a stated artifact or operation was checked against an identified contract; specify what was audited.
- **Empirical evidence** — evidence from an identified real-world dataset/system, with scope and limitations.
- **Pilot** — bounded real-world trial; not automatically generalisable.
- **Deployed** — operating in a real environment; not synonymous with effective or validated.
- **Open question** — unresolved scientific or practical issue.

Do not show a badge without a short explanation and a link/source where appropriate.

### 4.2 Claims safe to communicate, subject to current source verification

- TGCV investigates how the structure of transformations accessible to a system changes under relevant conditions.
- The change can involve expansion, contraction, reconfiguration or substitution.
- The analytical programme studies possible relations to reachability and trajectories.
- Different domains can be examined using a bounded common translation protocol while retaining their native semantics.
- A synthetic TSTC v004 demonstration was executed and audited across three synthetic fixtures, with the recorded comparisons classified as equivalent representation.

### 4.3 Claims not to communicate as established

- TGCV has demonstrated that increasing accessibility causes value creation.
- TGCV is superior to conventional engineering, optimisation, workflow or control models.
- TGCV is universally applicable across all systems/domains.
- Transformational Intelligence is a generally validated form of intelligence or a proven product capability.
- The Juguete Colectivo is a scientific validation or production-ready engine.
- The TSTC synthetic result establishes empirical causal effects or industrial utility.
- Any candidate application is a validated customer case or deployment.
- A Playground, community, platform or service is available before it exists and is maintained.

### 4.4 Evidence status must be freshly checked

The repository STATUS file available during this blueprint review is dated 2026-11-06. Before any public release, refresh the current canonical STATUS, RMA and Evidence→Claim Matrix pointers and verify that no relevant state has changed. This blueprint does not freeze the scientific status.

## 6. Editorial workflow and acceptance gates

Every page should pass:

**Gate A — Scientific fidelity**
- Is the wording consistent with current canonical sources?
- Are state, candidate transformations, accessibility, execution, trajectory, outcome and value kept distinct?
- Are non-canonical proposals and historical material labelled?

**Gate B — Evidence and provenance**
- Does every substantive scientific claim have an identifiable source?
- Is the evidence label accurate and bounded?
- Are execution, audit and synthetic demonstration not overstated?

**Gate C — Visitor comprehension**
- Can a first-time visitor explain the page's central idea?
- Is jargon introduced only after plain-language explanation?
- Is there a useful next step?

**Gate D — Publication readiness**
- Are links, licenses, citations, privacy and accessibility checked?
- Does every call to action work?
- Is the page maintainable by the available capacity?

**Gate E — Scope discipline**
- Does the page avoid creating a new product/service/community promise?
- Does it avoid requiring the Playground?
- Does it fit the first-release architecture?

A page that fails a gate remains draft or is excluded from release.

## 7. Efecto Ajá — proposed test protocol

Run a small qualitative test with people unfamiliar with TGCV and from different backgrounds. It is a content-design test, not a scientific experiment.

After they browse Home for up to two minutes without coaching, ask:
1. What do you think this project is trying to understand?
2. What changes when a system's conditions change?
3. What is the difference between a system changing state and its available transformations changing?
4. What would you explore next?
5. What remains unclear or sounds too abstract?

Record:
- participant background (at a broad, non-identifying level);
- time spent;
- explanation in their own words;
- key misunderstanding or missing concept;
- next action they chose;
- suggested copy/visual correction.

Do not collect unnecessary personal data. Do not treat a small qualitative sample as statistically representative. Iterate Home and Explore if visitors repeatedly confuse accessibility with execution or interpret TGCV as generic AI/consultancy.

## 12. Integrated editorial decision lock (2026-11-24)

This section reconciles the Content Blueprint with the approved Home and ecosystem decisions. It supersedes incompatible first-release assumptions elsewhere in this document. Parent: Web Master Brief §21; page architecture: Information Architecture §16; Home experience: Home Experience §14.

### 12.1 Approved page set and navigation

First-release navigation: **Explore, Research, Systems Engineering, Transformational Intelligence, Value Construction, Labs & Applications, About, Contact**. Systems Thinking is transversal (Home, Explore and Research), not a separate first-release page. Do not publish Community as a top-level destination. No empty or placeholder page is permitted.

### 12.2 Home content contract

The Home sequence is opening question → discovery → second visitor question with a concise answer → tangible example → conceptual explanation → ecosystem → exploration. Use the approved opening question «¿Qué tendría que cambiar para que un sistema pudiera hacer algo que hoy no puede hacer?» and the second question «¿Qué nuevas opciones aparecen cuando un sistema puede hacer algo que antes no estaba a su alcance?». Its short answer must explain that newly accessible transformations create new options and possible paths that may open opportunities for value, without implying execution or guaranteed value. Retain the working headline «Comprende cómo cambian las posibilidades de un sistema» and the conceptual depth for Explore.

The tangible example is migration of a software configuration between two different environments. It must be newly specified as an illustrative bounded scenario. It is not to be represented as an existing execution, a validated application, a feature of Juguete Colectivo or evidence from C10-S.

### 12.3 Page readiness and evidence controls

- **Home:** ready for editorial rewrite; new illustration and bilingual adaptation required.
- **Explore:** use the current TGCV distinctions and build the bounded migration example; explain candidate vs accessible transformations and distinguish accessibility, execution, trajectory, outcome and value.
- **Research:** source from current Core and controlled research assets, but refresh evidence claims against current STATUS, RMA and Evidence→Claim Matrix immediately before publication. Current public status must not be copied from stale snapshots.
