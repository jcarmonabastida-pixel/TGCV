## 3. Integrated editorial findings

### 3.1 Public identity and programme status

The pages consistently identify TGCV as **TGCV — Transformaciones Generativas y Construcción de Valor / TGCV — Generative Transformations and Value Construction**, described as a programme of applied research and innovation.

The copy does not present TGCV as a consolidated theory, a universal explanation or a validated product. The conditional relationship is preserved: changes in accessible transformations may contribute to trajectories, outcomes and value, but do not guarantee execution, favourable outcomes or value creation.

**Disposition:** coherent.

### 3.2 Core conceptual distinctions

The following distinctions are maintained across the page set:

- candidate transformation ≠ accessible transformation;
- accessibility ≠ execution or realisation;
- execution ≠ favourable outcome;
- outcome ≠ value;
- trajectory describes development through states and transitions, not an additional universal stage in a linear chain;
- changes in transformation accessibility do not imply that the system autonomously initiated those changes;
- TGCV research programme ≠ Transformational Intelligence capability concept;
- Systems Thinking perspective ≠ Systems Engineering discipline ≠ Transformational Intelligence capability hypothesis;
- TGCV ≠ VALUE applied framework ≠ ValueLabOS proposed enabling ecosystem.

**Disposition:** coherent. No scientific definitions or claims were changed in this review.

### 3.3 Home ecosystem map — bilingual asymmetry corrected

The Spanish Home copy previously introduced Research and Value Construction as two related spaces, then separately described Labs & Applications and ValueLabOS, while the English copy explicitly listed TGCV as an ecosystem element. This made the two versions structurally different and left the Spanish map without the same explicit TGCV node.

The Spanish map has been revised to align with the English version. Both now distinguish:
- TGCV as the research and innovation programme;
- Research as the communication and development space for its questions, models, methods, experiments and evidence;
- Value Construction as the complementary applied VALUE framework;
- Labs & Applications as bounded experimentation and application exploration;
- ValueLabOS as a proposed digital and collaborative ecosystem.

The distinction between complementary relationship and proven causal chain is retained. A duplicated English Home heading was also corrected.

**Disposition:** corrected in Home Copy v1.4.

### 3.4 Research evidence summary — bilingual equivalence corrected

The Spanish and English evidence-portfolio summaries listed different sets of representative domains and study types. The English summary has been aligned with the Spanish one to include urban infrastructure, electrical systems and decision experiments alongside the other named evidence areas.

This is a correction of editorial coverage, not an addition of evidence or a change in scientific claim status. The bounded scope and limitations remain explicit.

**Disposition:** corrected in Research Copy v0.9.

### 3.5 Transformational Intelligence references refreshed

The Transformational Intelligence copy's editorial notes referred to older Content Blueprint versions and described the integrated cross-page terminology check as still pending. Those references have been updated to Content Blueprint v2.9 and the current page-copy set reviewed here.

The five dimensions remain candidate dimensions under investigation, not a validated architecture or necessary-and-sufficient criteria. The distinctions between transformation-space dynamics, transformation handling and the TI capability hypothesis remain intact.

**Disposition:** corrected in Transformational Intelligence Copy v0.2.

### 3.6 Systems Engineering and VALUE relationship

Systems Engineering is described as an established engineering discipline with practices for defining, modelling, designing, integrating and evaluating systems. It is not presented as a TGCV invention or as a method superseded by TI.

VALUE is presented as a complementary applied framework with its own methodological foundations and evaluation. The fictional clinic example is not represented as a real intervention, empirical validation or evidence that VALUE is effective. Selection of the preferred option is explicitly separated from execution authorisation.

**Disposition:** coherent; no change to scientific or methodological source assets.

### 3.7 Value Construction terminology — first-mention and contextualisation correction

The previous Value Construction Copy used the name VALUE in the system-model explanation before defining what VALUE was. It also introduced CCV, IVS and the Value Discovery Sprint in a short cautionary passage without first explaining their distinct purposes. This was a genuine sequencing defect that the first integrated review did not catch; the review's earlier broad coherence conclusion must not be read as evidence that this detail was correct.

The page has been revised so that:
- VALUE is defined at its first substantive mention as the working name of the applied framework, with its Transformational Value Framework descriptor, before VSM or later sections refer to it as an established subject.
- CCV has a dedicated section as the capability objective the framework aims to develop, not a validated score or maturity scale. The unresolved alternatives for maturity levels remain open.
- IVS has a dedicated section as an exploratory measurement proposal, with no canonical dimensions, formula, weights or validated scale. It is not a ready KPI or the value produced.
- The Value Discovery Sprint has its own section as a bounded pilot-intervention design intended to improve the quality and traceability of a decision. Its five-day duration is a planning hypothesis; it has not been tested in a real case and is not an available commercial offer.
- CCV and IVS are not required for the Sprint; the Sprint does not include implementation or proof of realised value, and any subsequent execution requires separate authorisation.
- The canonical acronyms CCV and IVS are retained in both language versions, with the English expansions explained rather than silently changing the identifiers.

**Disposition:** corrected in Value Construction Experience v0.3 and bilingual Copy v0.2. These are editorial clarifications grounded in the existing methodological and Sprint source documents; they do not change those source definitions or their maturity status.

## 4. Cross-page narrative coherence

The first-release copy forms a coherent visitor progression:

1. **Home** introduces the question of how a system's possibilities can change and offers routes into the ecosystem.
2. **Explore** explains candidate transformations, accessibility, conditions and the distinction between possibility, execution, outcome and value.
3. **Research** explains what TGCV investigates, how it is examined, what the evidence supports and what remains open.
4. **Systems Engineering** explains the established engineering discipline and connects system modelling/intervention to the TGCV research question without conflating their roles.
5. **Transformational Intelligence** explores a developing capability hypothesis about using information on accessible transformations to reason and guide behaviour.
6. **Value Construction** defines VALUE before using it, explains the applied framework and its cycle, then separately contextualises CCV/IVS, the Value Discovery Sprint and the fictional clinic case while preserving its independent status relative to TGCV.

The page set avoids presenting these elements as a proven causal pipeline. Cross-links distinguish conceptual/research deepening from the complementary applied VALUE route.

**Disposition:** coherent at the level of current copy. The review does not claim that visitor comprehension has been empirically tested.
