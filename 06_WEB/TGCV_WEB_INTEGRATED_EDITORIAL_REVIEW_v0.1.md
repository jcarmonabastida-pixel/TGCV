## 1. Executive disposition

The seven substantive first-release sections form a coherent editorial progression:

1. **Home** introduces the central question, accessible transformations and routes into the ecosystem.
2. **Explore** explains candidate transformations, accessibility, execution, outcome, value and trajectory.
3. **Research** presents TGCV's scientific object, questions, methods, evidence and unresolved claims.
4. **Systems Engineering** explains established engineering practice and its complementary relationship to TGCV.
5. **Transformational Intelligence** presents a developing capability concept, distinct from AI, Systems Thinking and Systems Engineering.
6. **Value Construction** presents VALUE as an applied framework with independent foundations and evaluation.
7. **Labs & Applications** presents bounded demonstrators, proposed visitor experiences, candidate application contexts and broad opportunity families.

The conceptual architecture is coherent at the level of role and epistemic status. The website must not imply a linear causal story from TGCV through TI and VALUE to value creation. The sections are related, but they are not equivalent and are not stages in a validated process.

**Disposition:** the seven-section editorial set is suitable for continued content refinement and later design preparation. It is **not yet ready for publication approval**. About and Contact remain first-release destinations in the Content Blueprint but do not yet have a completed Experience/Copy pair in the reviewed set.

## 2. Integrated narrative and boundary review

### 2.1 Home → Explore → Research

- Home introduces the central question and software-configuration migration illustration without presenting it as an executed experiment or existing demonstrator.
- Explore deepens the explanation: candidate transformations, accessibility relative to system boundaries and conditions, and the distinction from execution, outcome and value.
- Research moves from accessible explanation to scientific definitions, analytical representations, research questions, evidence and falsifiability.

**Disposition:** coherent progression. Reuse of the software-migration example is intentional, provided each page retains its distinct explanatory role and the example remains explicitly illustrative.

### 2.2 Systems Engineering and Transformational Intelligence

- Systems Engineering is presented as an established discipline and practice for defining, modelling, designing, integrating and evaluating engineered systems.
- Transformational Intelligence is presented as a developing capability concept/hypothesis, with candidate dimensions and evidence requirements.
- Systems Thinking remains a transversal perspective, not an additional first-release page or a synonym for either of the above.

**Disposition:** roles are sufficiently differentiated. Avoid copy that makes TI sound like an established discipline, a validated product capability or a replacement for engineering practice.

### 2.3 Research and Value Construction

- TGCV investigates whether and under what conditions changes in accessible transformations may contribute to reachability, trajectories, outcomes and value.
- VALUE structures applied work around opportunity, system understanding, alternatives, decisions, action and learning.
- Neither validates the other automatically. The causal contribution of accessible-transformation changes to value remains open.

The Value Construction Copy gives dedicated explanations to:
- **CCV:** a capability-development objective, not a validated score or maturity scale;
- **IVS:** an exploratory measurement proposal, not a ready-to-use or validated indicator;
- **Value Discovery Sprint:** an untested pilot-intervention design, not an available service or commercial offer.

The clinic case is fictional and its figures/outcomes are simulated.

**Disposition:** the principal epistemic boundaries are present. Keep CCV, IVS and the Sprint in their dedicated explanatory sections; do not introduce them earlier as if visitors already knew them.

### 2.4 Labs & Applications

The page distinguishes four content classes:
- existing software/methodological assets;
- proposed visitor-facing experiences;
- Application-Fit candidate contexts;
- broad Business Translation opportunity families.

The featured and additional contexts remain exploratory candidates. The five Business Translation families are not validated markets, a service portfolio or evidence of application fit. The invitation to discover other problems is not a formal participation process.

The Copy v0.4 removes specific claims about TGCV Full Chain Demo's distributable functionality until the exact package and interface have been verified together. The local manual acceptance record remains qualified and separate from scientific validity, package identity and public availability.

the cross-domain mapping demonstrator remains bounded synthetic methodological evidence. energy/mobility minimum demonstrator remains distinct from the energy scenario in the cross-domain mapping demonstrator, and its reference reconstruction was not an independent baseline implementation.

**Disposition:** editorial boundaries are materially clearer. Public links, downloads and functionality claims remain gated by asset and release verification.
