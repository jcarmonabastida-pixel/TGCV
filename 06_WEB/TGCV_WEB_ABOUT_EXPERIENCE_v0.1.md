## 1. Role in the visitor journey

**About** gives visitors a concise, trustworthy account of what TGCV is, why the programme exists, how its work is approached and how its claims are kept within the limits of available evidence.

It should not repeat the scientific explanation in Research, the conceptual introduction in Explore, or the applied framework explanation in Value Construction. Its role is to establish identity, purpose, working principles, transparency and organisational boundaries.

**Primary visitor question:**

> What is TGCV, why does this programme exist, and how does it approach its research responsibly?

**Primary visitor outcome:** understand TGCV as an evolving applied-research and innovation programme, its central research concern, its interdisciplinary orientation and the principles used to distinguish research questions, methods, evidence, prototypes and applications.

**Secondary visitor outcome:** understand what the programme does not claim about its institutional status, scientific maturity or practical results.

## 2. Audience and depth

The page serves:
1. Visitors who want to understand the initiative behind the website.
2. Researchers and technical practitioners assessing the programme's purpose and working discipline.
3. Potential interlocutors who want to understand the current scope and maturity before considering a conversation.

Keep the page concise and accessible. It is not an institutional biography, a detailed scientific paper, a governance manual or a catalogue of services.

## 3. Narrative architecture

### Block 1 — What is TGCV?

**Visitor question:** What kind of initiative is this?

Introduce the approved identity:

**TGCV — Transformaciones Generativas y Construcción de Valor**

*Un programa de investigación aplicada e innovación sobre cómo la ampliación de las posibilidades de transformación de los sistemas puede contribuir a la construcción de valor.*

In English, preserve the approved public rendering:

**TGCV — Generative Transformations and Value Construction**

*A programme of applied research and innovation exploring how expanding the transformations available to systems may contribute to value construction.*

State plainly that TGCV is a research and innovation programme, not a consolidated theory, a consultancy firm, an AI agency or a product platform.

### Block 2 — Why the programme exists

**Visitor question:** What problem motivates the work?

Explain that systems can be studied not only in terms of their current state or performance, but also in terms of which transformations are accessible under their conditions, how that set changes and what may follow from those changes.

The programme investigates whether this perspective can be represented and examined across different domains while preserving the meaning and established methods of each domain.

Do not claim ownership of the general idea that systems change or that capabilities and possibilities evolve. Do not claim that the analytical architecture is universally valid or wholly original.

### Block 3 — The question being pursued

**Visitor question:** What is the central research question?

Use one concise formulation:

**How do changes in the transformations accessible to a system relate to the possibilities it can reach, the trajectories that may follow and, ultimately, outcomes and value?**

Explain that the contribution of changes in accessible transformations to later outcomes and value remains an open research question. Expanding possibilities does not guarantee execution, favourable outcomes or value creation.

### Block 4 — How the programme approaches the work

**Visitor question:** How does TGCV investigate the question?

Present a short set of working principles:
- Define the system, its relevant conditions and the question being examined.
- Make candidate transformations and accessibility conditions explicit where the domain and evidence allow.
- Preserve domain-native concepts and compare with established representations rather than replacing them by default.
- Separate accessibility from execution, reachability, trajectory, outcome and value.
- Use modelling, synthetic experiments and empirical investigation according to the question and the available evidence.
- Report limits, uncertainty, negative and indeterminate findings rather than converting them into positive claims.

Describe the interdisciplinary orientation through the approved names **Systems Thinking** and **Systems Engineering**, together with transformation-space analysis, modelling and experimentation. Do not imply that these are all parts of a new unified discipline or that TGCV replaces established practice.

### Block 5 — How to read the work and its evidence

**Visitor question:** How can a visitor tell what has actually been established?

Explain the distinctions that govern the programme's public claims:
- A research question is not a finding.
- A conceptual or methodological proposal is not empirical validation.
- A synthetic experiment can test a bounded method or representation; it is not, by itself, evidence of real-world causal effects.
- A prototype's existence or functional acceptance does not establish scientific validity or public availability.
- An exploratory application context is not a validated deployment.
- An observed outcome is not automatically value; value requires an explicit, context-sensitive evaluation.

Where materials are made public, identify their scope and status accurately. Do not promise that all internal research records, datasets or software are openly available.

### Block 6 — Transparency, traceability and correction

**Visitor question:** What makes the programme's claims inspectable and correctable?

Describe public-facing commitments, not internal workflow detail:
- Keep the identity and scope of each research asset clear.
- Distinguish current decisions from working proposals and historical material.
- Preserve provenance and version information for materials released publicly.
- Make the basis and limits of claims explicit.
- Correct material errors and update public descriptions when the underlying status changes.
- Do not use marketing, application development or prototype demonstrations to upgrade scientific claims.

Do not expose internal repository paths, decision IDs, execution identifiers or governance machinery in visitor copy. GitHub may be used as the canonical working record internally, but this page should explain the public principles rather than reproduce repository operations.

### Block 7 — An evolving programme

**Visitor question:** Is TGCV a finished framework or a programme still being developed?

State that TGCV is evolving. Its concepts, methods and evidence are subject to examination, comparison and revision. Some questions remain open, and findings may narrow the programme's claims as well as support them.

Invite thoughtful engagement without implying a formal membership, contribution, funding, collaboration or participation process. Any practical route to make contact belongs to the separate Contact destination and must be published only when a real, monitored channel is ready.

## 4. Relationship to the rest of the website

About should provide context and orientation, then direct visitors to the substantive pages:
- **Research** — scientific questions, methods, evidence and open limitations.
- **Explore** — the accessible explanation of transformations, accessibility, execution, outcome and value.
- **Systems Engineering** — established engineering practices and their relationship to complex systems.
- **Transformational Intelligence** — a developing capability concept and its open evidence questions.
- **Value Construction** — the complementary applied framework and its independent foundations.
- **Labs & Applications** — bounded demonstrators, proposed experiences and exploratory application contexts.

Do not present these destinations as departments, organisational units or stages of a validated delivery process.

## 5. Editorial and evidence controls

- Preserve the official names **About**, **TGCV**, **Systems Thinking**, **Systems Engineering**, **Transformational Intelligence**, **Value Construction**, **VALUE**, **ValueLabOS**, **Research**, **Explore** and **Labs & Applications** exactly as approved. Descriptive prose may be translated; proper names must not be.
- Use the approved TGCV identity and descriptor in both languages.
- Do not describe TGCV as a consolidated theory or claim universal validity, complete originality, causal efficacy, predictive superiority or demonstrated value creation.
- Do not imply a staffed institute, formal company, research centre, external scientific board, institutional affiliation, consortium, active community or formal participation programme unless independently established and authorised.
- Do not invent founder biographies, team members, credentials, partners, funders, institutional endorsements or contact details.
- Do not promise open access to every research asset or a public software release.
- Keep the page distinct from Research; avoid reproducing detailed scientific evidence inventories.
- Keep the Spanish and English versions equivalent in meaning, scope, maturity and caveats.
- Contact remains conditional on a real monitored route.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

The About Experience is acceptable when:
1. A first-time visitor can identify TGCV as an applied-research and innovation programme.
2. The programme's purpose and central question are understandable without technical prerequisites.
3. The open status of the causal contribution to outcomes and value is explicit.
4. The interdisciplinary orientation is described without claiming a new discipline or replacing established practices.
5. The distinctions among research, methods, synthetic experiments, prototypes, exploratory contexts and validated results are clear.
6. Transparency and correction principles are concrete but do not expose internal governance machinery.
7. No unsupported organisational, institutional, team, partnership or community claim appears.
8. The page complements rather than duplicates Research, Explore and Value Construction.
9. The Spanish and English copy preserve the same claims and limits.
10. No unavailable Contact or participation route is implied.
