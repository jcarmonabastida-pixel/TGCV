The recovered continuity package states that SLR-1 must attempt to falsify TGCV's architectural originality. It must distinguish:

- terminology similarity;
- construct equivalence;
- structural equivalence;
- full architectural absorption.

A literary or terminological coincidence alone does not modify the Core. fileciteturn72file1

`SIP → SLR task → evidence → SLR decision → PMO/SMO review → RMA`

with architecture review between evidence and any substantive Core change.
