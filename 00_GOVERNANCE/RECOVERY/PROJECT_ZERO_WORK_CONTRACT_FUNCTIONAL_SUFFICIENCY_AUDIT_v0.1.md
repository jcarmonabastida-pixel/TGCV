## 1. Objective

Determine whether the currently recovered evidence is sufficient to define a safe **current functional role** for Project Zero and Work Contract without reconstructing either missing historical original.

This audit does not attempt to recover missing clauses, historical terminology or complete specifications.

The assessment is based on:

## 3. Project Zero interface assessment

### Recovered functional evidence

The recovered interface record states that Project Zero is referenced upstream of programme governance/PMO-SMO and that it **appears to define the programme-level frame within which the research programme operates**. Its exact rules, scope and terminology remain unrecovered.

The Programme Operating System independently defines a programme-wide operating baseline and separates continuity/governance, scientific architecture, research execution, applied methodology and external transfer.

### Sufficiency finding

**Interface-level sufficiency: PASS.**

The evidence is sufficient to preserve a bounded functional role:

`Project Zero → programme-level framing/context → PMO/SMO`

It is **not** sufficient to define a full Project Zero specification, historical objectives, exact governance clauses, scope, milestones, terminology or decision rights.

### Safe derivative boundary

A future derivative may define only the minimum current programme function necessary to represent the upstream framing interface. It must not claim historical fidelity.

## 4. Work Contract interface assessment

### Recovered functional evidence

The recovered interface record states that Work Contract is referenced upstream of PMO/SMO and is **treated as the governing agreement for how the research collaboration/workflow is conducted**. Exact clauses must not be invented.

The current PMO/SMO specification independently establishes operation-specific authorization, dependencies, evidence requirements, provenance, closure and change-control boundaries.

### Sufficiency finding

**Interface-level sufficiency: PASS.**

The evidence is sufficient to preserve a bounded functional role:

`Work Contract → agreed operating/collaboration conditions → PMO/SMO`

It is **not** sufficient to define a complete contractual/workflow specification, historical obligations, roles, deliverables, acceptance terms, escalation rules or commercial/legal conditions.

### Safe derivative boundary

A future derivative may define only a minimal programme-governance interface concerning agreed working conditions and coordination expectations. It must not invent contractual clauses or legal/commercial commitments.

## 6. Governance consequence

This result permits, but does not require, creation of minimal current functional derivatives for Project Zero and Work Contract.

Any such derivative must:

- be labelled `CURRENT / CONTROLLED — FUNCTIONAL DERIVATIVE`;
- state explicitly that the historical original was not recovered;
- contain only supported functional interfaces;
- preserve unresolved historical details as open recovery targets;
- not modify Core, RMA scientific state, claim status or closed tests;
- not create execution authorization;
- not create industrial utility, superiority, causality or value claims.

The derivative must remain subordinate to the Programme OS, SIP, RMA and PMO/SMO specification.

## 7. What remains prohibited

The following are not authorized by this audit:

- fabrication of historical Project Zero content;
- fabrication of Work Contract clauses;
- legal/contractual interpretation beyond recovered evidence;
- promotion to `FOUNDATIONAL`;
- reopening historical scientific decisions;
- treating the derivatives as evidence of the historical programme architecture;
- automatic authorization of future research execution.
