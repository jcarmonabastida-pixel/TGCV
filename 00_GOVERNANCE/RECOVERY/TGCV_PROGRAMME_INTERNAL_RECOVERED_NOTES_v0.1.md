## Recovered programme logic

The historical conversation repeatedly emphasises:

- turning experience into reusable intellectual assets rather than relying only on time-based consulting;
- separating high-value services from scalable methodological/product assets;
- using workflows, templates, knowledge repositories and AI-assisted processes to make the method reproducible;
- connecting research, practical application and social/industrial impact.

These are programme-design observations, not scientific evidence for TGCV.

## Boundary with current TGCV

The current scientific Core must remain authoritative. Historical applied constructs can inform ARM/RII/MOI and methodological work only through explicit traceability.

No historical commercial or professional concept is to be promoted into the TGCV scientific ontology merely because it uses related vocabulary such as value, capability, flow, adaptation or intelligence.
