## 1. Constitutional role

The Programme Contract is the **second constitutional document of the Programme**.

- **Project Zero:** answers what the Programme is.
- **Programme Contract:** answers how the Programme decides and works so that scientific and strategic integrity are preserved.

It is **not a legal contract**. It is an intellectual and governance contract.

## 2. Mission

**Establecer los principios, compromisos y reglas de comportamiento que garantizan la coherencia científica, metodológica y estratégica del Programa durante toda su evolución.**

## 3. Problem solved

Without the Contract, the Programme would be exposed to:

- opportunistic changes of direction;
- loss of focus;
- incorporation of activities that do not contribute to the Programme purpose;
- conflicts between scientific and industrial interests;
- uncontrolled growth.

Its function is therefore to protect Programme coherence over time.

## 4. Capital

**Primary:** Methodological — it establishes working discipline.

**Secondary:** Strategic; relational — it generates trust among people joining or collaborating with the Programme.

## 5. System

**Sistema de Dirección y Gobernanza.**

It is the bridge between Programme vision and execution.

## 6. Primary users

- Programme Director;
- Scientific Committee;
- doctoral researchers;
- collaborating researchers;
- industrial partners;
- project coordinators.

It is not primarily an external persuasion document. Its function is alignment among Programme participants.

## 7. Dependency

**Depends only on Project Zero.**

This establishes the constitutional hierarchy:

`Project Zero → Programme identity`

`Programme Contract → rules for preserving that identity through work and decisions`

## 8. Assets it feeds

The Contract governs downstream operational assets, including:

- PMO;
- ACTI;
- SIP;
- ARM;
- TGCV;
- TCP;
- RII.

The list is not exhaustive: downstream assets must respect the Contract's applicable principles and rules.

## 9. Maturity

**A5 — Consolidated.**

The conceptual structure is mature; validation is expected to come through sustained use.

It is not A6 because long-term operational validation has not yet been established.

## 10. Risks and mitigations

### Risk 1 — Becoming a declaration of intent

**Mitigation:** every important strategic decision should be justifiable against the Contract.

### Risk 2 — Becoming excessively rigid

**Mitigation:** distinguish permanent principles/invariants from revisable rules.

### Risk 3 — New collaborators do not read it

**Mitigation:** make it a welcome document and mandatory reference for people joining the Programme.

## 11. Indicators

The Contract is functioning correctly when:

- important decisions remain mutually coherent;
- strategic deviations are detected early;
- the Programme retains a stable identity as it grows.

These are governance indicators, not scientific validity measures.

## 12. Permanent invariants

### Invariant 1 — Scientific integrity

**La integridad científica tiene prioridad sobre la rapidez.**

### Invariant 2 — Purpose alignment

**Toda decisión debe poder justificarse por su contribución al propósito del Programa.**

### Invariant 3 — Evidence / theory / methodology / transfer sequence

**La evidencia precede a la teoría. La teoría precede a la metodología. La metodología precede a la transferencia.**

This is an ordering principle for programme development, not a claim that all applied activity must wait for a final theory in every operational circumstance; exceptions, if ever needed, require explicit governance.

### Invariant 4 — Rigour and applicability

**La utilidad práctica nunca justificará reducir el rigor científico. Y el rigor científico nunca justificará ignorar la aplicabilidad.**

### Invariant 5 — Accumulative intellectual heritage

**El patrimonio intelectual del Programa es acumulativo. Nada se elimina sin una justificación explícita y trazable.**

## 13. Constitutional relationship with Project Zero

Together, the two assets constitute the Programme's current constitutional layer:

```text
PROJECT ZERO
What is the Programme?
        │
        ▼
PROGRAMME CONTRACT
How do we work and decide so the Programme preserves its integrity?
        │
        ▼
GOVERNANCE / OPERATING ASSETS
PMO · SIP · RMA · ACTI · …
        │
        ▼
RESEARCH · METHODOLOGY · TRANSFER
```

The Contract cannot redefine the Programme's purpose independently of Project Zero; equally, downstream assets cannot override the Contract's invariants without explicit constitutional governance.

## 14. Boundary with scientific Core

The Contract protects scientific integrity but does not itself determine scientific truth. Scientific Core changes remain subject to evidence, testing, explicit decision and architecture review.

Therefore the constitutional layer governs **how the Programme protects scientific work**, while the scientific layer governs **what the evidence supports**.

## 15. Evaluation

With Project Zero and the Programme Contract certified/recovered, the Programme has its two constitutional reference assets:

1. **Project Zero → identity, purpose, scope and permanent principles.**
2. **Programme Contract → principles, commitments and rules of behaviour that preserve integrity through evolution.**

From this point onward, subsequent assets should normally be understood as **narrative, operational, methodological, scientific or transfer assets**, rather than additional constitutional documents, unless an explicit governance decision establishes otherwise.

## 16. Continuity rule

The Contract is permanent but version-controlled. Its permanent invariants should be distinguished from revisable operating rules. Material changes require explicit provenance and governance review.
