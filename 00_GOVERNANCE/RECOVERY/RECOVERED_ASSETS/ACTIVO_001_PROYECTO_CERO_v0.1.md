## 1. Mission

**Definir la identidad, el propósito, el alcance y los principios permanentes del Programa de I+D+i, constituyendo la referencia fundacional sobre la que se apoyan todos los demás activos.**

The Project Zero defines the **Programme**, not TGCV as a theory. The absence of TGCV from this mission is deliberate and is a governing distinction.

## 2. Problem solved

Project Zero resolves the programme identity problem:

- future collaborators could otherwise understand the Programme differently;
- documents could lose coherence;
- it would become difficult to determine whether a decision contradicts the original programme vision.

Its function is therefore institutional identity and continuity.

## 3. Capital

**Primary:** Strategic.

**Secondary:** Methodological; relational.

## 5. Primary users

Project Zero is primarily intended for people joining, evaluating or governing the Programme, including:

- future researchers;
- doctoral supervisors;
- industrial partners;
- funders;
- new Programme members.

It answers the question:

> **¿A qué me estoy uniendo exactamente?**

## 6. Dependencies

**None.** Project Zero is the origin-level programme asset.

This does not mean it is independent of reality or evidence; it means that no other TGCV programme asset is its conceptual prerequisite.

## 7. Assets it feeds

Project Zero provides the programme-level frame for downstream assets, especially:

- Vision Paper;
- Research Prospectus;
- PMO;
- SIP;
- ARM;
- TCP;
- TGCV.

The list is intentionally non-exhaustive: Project Zero is an upstream programme identity reference and can therefore inform any subsequent programme asset where appropriate.

## 8. Maturity

**A5 — Consolidated.**

It is deliberately not classified A6 because A6 would imply that the programme identity had been demonstrated over a sufficiently long operational history. That condition has not yet been met.

## 9. Horizon

**Permanent.**

## 10. Risks and mitigations

### Risk 1 — Becoming a historical document

**Mitigation:** Project Zero must continue to be actively used as a programme reference.

### Risk 2 — Continuous modification

**Mitigation:** strict version control.

### Risk 3 — Replacement by downstream documents

**Mitigation:** maintain the principle that no downstream document can redefine the purpose of the Programme.

## 11. Indicators

Qualitative programme indicators:

- all subsequent assets remain coherent with Project Zero;
- new collaborators can understand the Programme after reading it;
- no strategic decision contradicts its principles.

These should be treated as governance indicators rather than as evidence of scientific validity.

## 12. Governance interpretation

Project Zero occupies the highest programme-level position in the current architecture:

`PROJECT ZERO`
`     ↓`
`Programme identity / purpose / scope / permanent principles`
`     ↓`
`Programme governance and operating assets`
`     ↓`
`Scientific + methodological + external-transfer assets`

It therefore constrains the **purpose and identity** of downstream work, but does not directly determine scientific truth. Scientific claims remain subject to the scientific evidence and architecture governance system.

## 13. Boundary with TGCV

A key recovered principle is:

> **Project Zero defines the Programme; it does not define the theory.**

Therefore:

- Project Zero may establish why the Programme exists and what kind of value it seeks to construct and deliver;
- it may establish scope and permanent principles;
- it may frame TGCV as a research/theoretical asset;
- it must not silently dictate the scientific content of TGCV;
- TGCV's scientific Core remains governed by scientific evidence, tests, freezes and architecture review.

## 14. Continuity rule

Project Zero should evolve only through controlled versions. A later version may refine or clarify the programme identity, but its replacement or material change requires explicit programme governance.

The current recovery establishes Project Zero as the origin-level programme asset. Its exact relationship to the historical **Work Contract**, **PMO/SMO**, **SIP**, **ACTII** and **Flows/MOP** will be reconciled as those assets are recovered.

Until then, no historical dependency beyond the explicit recovered statement of independence should be invented.
