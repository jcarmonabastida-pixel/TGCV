**Role:** operational flow / scientific workflow

> **Naming warning:** MOI-Operativo is distinct from the **Modelo Ontológico Inicial (MOI)**. The former describes how the Programme conducts research; the latter is an ontological/theoretical artefact.

## 1. Role

MOI-Operativo answers one question:

> **¿Cómo funciona el Programa cuando empieza a investigar de verdad?**

It is not an additional conceptual architecture. It is the operational description of the Programme's scientific workflow.

## 2. Position relative to SIP

The recovered ordering is:

`Proyecto Cero → Contrato → Vision Paper → Research Prospectus → MOI-Operativo → SIP`

The rationale is that the SIP is a complex control system and should be certified only after the process it governs has been made explicit.

Thus MOI-Operativo is a **process specification**, while SIP is the governance/control system that governs that process.

## 4. Principle of Flow Primacy

### Canonical principle

> **El Programa no está organizado alrededor de documentos, sino alrededor del flujo de transformación del conocimiento. Los activos existen únicamente para soportar ese flujo.**

This is a programme-governance principle, not a claim about the scientific content of TGCV.

## 5. Consequence for SIP

SIP is not best understood as simply another document in the asset inventory. It is the **system that governs the operational flow**.

Therefore:

`MOI-Operativo = description of the workflow`

`SIP = governance/control system for that workflow`

The distinction should be preserved when SIP is reconstructed and certified.

## 7. Four-cycle model

The operational flow is not ultimately linear. It can be represented as four connected cycles.

### Cycle I — Discovery

**Question:** ¿Qué está ocurriendo realmente?

**Inputs:**

- reality;
- observations;
- literature;
- cases.

**Transformations:**

- search;
- evidence;
- dossiers/expedients;
- synthesis.

**Output:** Banco de Hechos.

**Primary domain:** SIP-governed discovery process.

### Cycle II — Explanation

**Question:** ¿Cómo podemos explicar lo descubierto?

**Input:** Banco de Hechos.

**Transformations:**

- patterns;
- concepts;
- relations;
- principles.

**Output:** TGCV / theory construction.

**Domain:** MOI and theoretical construction.

### Cycle III — Transformation

**Question:** ¿Cómo utilizamos ese conocimiento?

**Input:** TGCV.

**Transformations:**

- ARM;
- methodology;
- pilots;
- transfer.

**Output:** Value.

**Domain:** application, industrial opportunities, projects, pilots and innovation.

### Cycle IV — Programme Learning

**Question:** ¿Qué hemos aprendido sobre nuestra propia forma de investigar?

**Inputs:** outputs and experience from the preceding cycles.

**Output:** improvements to:

- SIP;
- PMO;
- methodology;
- governance;
- discovery capability.

The methodology of discovery therefore may emerge as a result of Programme learning rather than being a pre-specified end in itself.

## 9. Strategic interpretation

The Programme should not be defined solely as an effort to produce:

- a theory;
- a methodology;
- a PMO.

Its deeper operational identity is a **permanent capability to produce scientifically sound knowledge and convert that knowledge into impact**.

Within that capability:

- TGCV is a major scientific result;
- the discovery methodology can become a programme legacy;
- ACTI is infrastructure enabling the capability;
- transfer demonstrates the practical transformation potential of generated knowledge.

This interpretation must remain subordinate to Project Zero and the Programme Contract.

## 10. Strategic hypothesis — future research potential

A hypothesis is registered for future evaluation:

> The four-cycle knowledge-transformation architecture may not be unique to TGCV and may describe a general operating pattern for serious R&D&I programmes.

This **must not currently be promoted to a scientific claim**. It is a strategic/research hypothesis to be tested later if evidence and programme priorities justify doing so.

## 11. Proposed Programme Map

Before SIP certification, one controlled session should produce a **Mapa Operativo del Programa**: a single-page visual representation of the complete knowledge-generation flow from problem formulation through transfer.

The map is intended as a visual integration layer connecting already-certified assets, not as a new constitutional or scientific asset.

Its purposes are:

- provide a common mental model;
- connect assets to the flow they support;
- explain Programme operation to researchers, industrial actors and funders;
- serve as a stable interface for SIP certification.

It should not reopen the general architecture after certification unless a material contradiction is discovered.

## 12. Strategic/operational governance mode

The recovered proposal introduces two complementary governance levels:

**Strategic level:** certify assets.

**Operational level:** certify flows.

This prevents the Programme from becoming well documented but poorly connected.

## 14. Important boundary: MOI-Operativo vs Modelo Ontológico Inicial

These names must never be conflated.

- **MOI-Operativo:** programme workflow / operational process.
- **Modelo Ontológico Inicial (MOI):** ontological/theoretical artefact in the scientific architecture.

The operational flow may contain a transition into MOI, but it does not make MOI-Operativo itself an ontological model.

## 15. Continuity and change rule

MOI-Operativo is an operational baseline. Changes should be made only through controlled versions and explicit reconciliation with SIP, PMO, ACTI and the constitutional layer.

The **Principle of Flow Primacy** should be treated as a programme governance principle unless later evidence shows that it belongs at constitutional level.
