# TGCV — Internal Recovery Method

## Objective

Recover the programme's internal architecture from prior TGCV conversations and Library artefacts without converting conversational density into false canonical history.

## Method

1. Search Library by exact programme vocabulary and semantic equivalents.
2. Identify candidate source artefacts.
3. Read the relevant content and surrounding context.
4. Distinguish original/recovered artefact from later summaries.
5. Assign status: FOUNDATIONAL, CURRENT, WORKING, HISTORICAL, SUPERSEDED or UNVERIFIED.
6. Record provenance and dependencies.
7. Only then copy/adapt into the canonical repository.
8. If a source is absent, create a recovery-status record rather than fabricate it.
9. Reconcile recovered assets against the latest valid research state before declaring them current.

## Priority vocabulary

Proyecto Cero; Contrato de Trabajo; PMO; SMO; SIP; ACTII; Flujos; SDM; SLR; Evidence Bank; Fact Bank; RMA; freezes; gates; continuity; traceability.

## Integrity rule

Historical material is valuable even when superseded. It should remain recoverable because it may contain design rationale, rejected alternatives, process innovations and decision provenance. Superseded does not mean disposable.
