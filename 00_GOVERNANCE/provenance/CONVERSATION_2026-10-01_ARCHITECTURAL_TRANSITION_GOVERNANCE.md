# TGCV — Conversation Provenance Record: Architectural Transition Governance

**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Status:** PERSISTED / GOVERNED

## Context

This conversation established that TGCV should not proceed directly to new experiment design while the operative Core and Evidence→Claim Matrix remain formulated around the inherited architecture.

The discussion identified a governance problem: accumulated evidence relevant to Dynamic Transformation Space / Transformational Intelligence may eventually warrant architectural revision, but changing the Core or claim ontology before discriminating evidence would make the architecture conform to the expected result.

## Agreed resolution

Before designing new experiments, TGCV introduces an explicit **Architectural Transition / Evidence Assessment** layer.

The layer:

- keeps the inherited Core and Evidence→Claim Matrix operative;
- records the TSDI direction as an architectural hypothesis under empirical evaluation;
- allows evidence to be classified as supporting, challenging, non-discriminating or irrelevant to either architectural description;
- defines criteria for when a formal Core review is warranted;
- defines criteria for when the Evidence→Claim Matrix should subsequently be reviewed;
- preserves the traceability chain from old architecture/claim to evidence, detected limitation, new concept and revised architecture/claim;
- does not authorize scientific execution;
- does not upgrade scientific claims.
