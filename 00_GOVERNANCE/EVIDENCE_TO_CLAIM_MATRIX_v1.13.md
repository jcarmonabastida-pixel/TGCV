## Controlled candidate transversal methodology propagation

**Package:** `TGCV-TM-CPP-001-R1`  
**Scope:** M0–M4 and M7 as candidate methodological rules; M5–M6 as bounded candidate rules; M8 as a boundary rule. M9 remains open and is not propagated as an established rule.

The propagated methodology preserves the following chain:

`state / relevant conditions → Uτ → ex-ante Pτ → T_acc → ΔT_acc → executed transformation / subsequent state → trajectory → outcome → Value`

### Propagated methodological rules

- **M0:** reconstruct state/configuration and relevant conditions before defining candidate transformations.
- **M1:** define the candidate transformation universe `Uτ` before evaluating accessibility or observed outcomes.
- **M2:** define `Pτ` independently of subsequent outcome/value.
- **M3:** derive `T_acc` from candidate transformations satisfying `Pτ`; observed execution and outcome do not define `T_acc`.
- **M4:** reconstruct comparable `T_acc,0`, `T_acc,1` and `ΔT_acc` independently of outcome.
- **M5:** keep `ΔT_acc` analytically distinct from the transformation actually selected/executed/observed; bounded only.
- **M6:** reconstruct subsequent state/trajectory as a temporally distinct layer; bounded only.
- **M7:** reconstruct the conventional outcome independently after the state/trajectory layer.
- **M8:** do not identify conventional outcome with TGCV Value without a separate value construction/validation argument.

### M9 remains open

`ΔT_acc → ΔV` remains an OPEN HYPOTHESIS / RESEARCH QUESTION. No current evidence establishes a transversal causal value rule. It is not propagated as established methodology.

### Mandatory non-substitution controls

1. observed transformation ≠ accessible transformation;
2. eligibility/administrative condition ≠ `Pτ` unless ex-ante admissibility is established;
3. structural/treatment/take-up/adoption change ≠ `ΔT_acc` without explicit accessibility reconstruction;
4. longitudinal variable match ≠ TGCV trajectory definition;
5. outcome ≠ TGCV Value automatically;
6. temporal association ≠ causal effect;
7. domain-specific semantic variables ≠ TGCV primitives.

### Associated empirical controls

Ex-ante accessibility/admissibility; outcome-independent `T_acc`; temporal ordering; non-circularity; provenance/reproducibility; independent reconstruction where required; and explicit handling of interference/comparability and other design-specific threats where applicable.
