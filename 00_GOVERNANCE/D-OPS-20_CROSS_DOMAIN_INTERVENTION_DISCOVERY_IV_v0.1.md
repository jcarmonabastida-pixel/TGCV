## 1. Purpose

Continue C09 discovery after the negative Ethereum intervention-identification result, applying the strengthened intervention-first filter:

1. independently governed rule layer;
2. public longitudinal state archive;
3. stable object/state identity;
4. independently enumerable transformation universe;
5. pre-execution accessibility rule;
6. independently assigned or credibly quasi-exogenous accessibility intervention;
7. credible treatment/control or counterfactual trajectory;
8. no direct encoding of the target trajectory by the intervention;
9. reproducible provenance.

This is discovery only. No dataset acquisition, modelling or empirical execution is authorized.

## 2. Discovery screen

### Candidate A — Randomized local infrastructure investment

A large urban infrastructure investment experiment in Mexico randomly allocated USD 68 million across low-income neighbourhoods and reported improvements in infrastructure access and private investment. The randomized allocation is unusually strong for causal identification. citeturn0search9

**Strength:** independently assigned treatment and credible counterfactual.

**Failure at present:** the intervention is an investment programme rather than an independently governed formal transformation-rule system. A finite `U_tau` and non-circular `P_tau` for the underlying infrastructure transformations have not been demonstrated.

**Disposition:** `RETAIN AS CAUSAL-DESIGN REFERENCE; NOT C09 CANDIDATE`.

### Candidate B — New metro / light-rail natural experiments

Longitudinal natural experiments exist in which treatment is defined by proximity to newly opened stations, with pre/post treatment and control groups. Examples include a new metro system with 5,627 baseline participants and 1,770 follow-up participants, and a Portland light-rail intervention with 282 prospectively followed participants. citeturn0search0turn0search2

**Strength:** clear accessibility intervention, temporal ordering and longitudinal treatment/control structure.

**Failure at present:** the public studies demonstrate causal treatment designs, but not an independently governed formal rule layer defining `U_tau` and `P_tau` for the system state. The transport network is the intervention context rather than a formally versioned state-transition specification.

**Disposition:** `RETAIN AS METHODOLOGICAL REFERENCE; NOT ADMITTED`.

### Candidate C — High-speed rail quasi-natural experiments

Recent work uses the opening of China's high-speed rail network as a quasi-natural experiment with longitudinal CHARLS panel data from 2011–2020. citeturn0search1

**Strength:** longitudinal treatment timing and quasi-experimental identification.

**Failure:** same rule-layer deficiency as Candidate B; the infrastructure intervention is observable but a formal independently governed transformation system for `T_acc` has not been established.

**Disposition:** `NOT ADMITTED`.

### Candidate D — Broadband infrastructure policy

The Broadband China policy has been used as a staggered quasi-natural experiment with China Family Panel Studies data from 2010–2020. citeturn0search4turn0search5

**Strength:** longitudinal state/outcome data and externally determined policy timing.

**Failure:** no sufficiently formal, independently governed transformation-rule layer for defining the accessible transformation universe was identified in this screen. Policy exposure is not equivalent to a formally specified `P_tau`.

**Disposition:** `NOT ADMITTED`.

### Candidate E — Water infrastructure access

Recent longitudinal studies exploit programme timing and matched/control designs to estimate effects of water-access interventions. citeturn0search8

**Strength:** explicit accessibility treatment and counterfactual methodology.

**Failure:** no independent formal rule layer and finite transformation universe meeting the TGCV C09 gate were identified.

**Disposition:** `NOT ADMITTED`.

## 3. Discovery conclusion

The current discovery produces an important asymmetry:

- infrastructure/transport/public-policy domains provide strong causal intervention designs;
- Ethereum provides an unusually strong formal state-transition and longitudinal-state architecture;
- but no candidate found in this pass combines **both** properties strongly enough to satisfy the full C09 gate.

The missing combination remains:

`formal transition rule + longitudinal state archive + independently assigned accessibility intervention`.

This is now the principal bottleneck for C09 candidate discovery.

## 4. Scientific decision

**D-OPS-20 = CLOSED — NO NEW C09 EXECUTION CANDIDATE ADMITTED.**

The negative result is informative: causal identification cannot be obtained simply by selecting a domain with a strong natural experiment, nor simply by selecting a domain with a formal transition system. The two architectural requirements must coexist.

No claim-matrix upgrade follows.

## 5. Next controlled operation

The next discovery pass should therefore be **rule-layer-first**, not outcome-first: search for domains where the governing rule itself determines transformation admissibility and where an externally assigned policy/constraint change alters that admissibility while preserving an independent longitudinal state archive.

Priority families for the next pass:

1. machine-readable network/protocol standards with externally imposed policy changes;
2. regulated allocation/scheduling systems with versioned formal admissibility rules;
3. versioned computational or data-exchange protocols with externally imposed compatibility transitions;
4. other formal state-transition systems in which treatment changes admissibility rather than directly changing the outcome.
