# EXT-UPD-4.7 — I-01 Constructive Operationalization Decision v0.1

**Status:** CLOSED / GOVERNANCE DECISION — CONSTRUCTIVE I-01 ATTEMPT AUTHORIZED IN PRINCIPLE
**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Predecessor:** EXT-UPD-4.6 / I-01 Gate-C Translation Trace

## 1. Trigger

I-01, the independent infrastructure-network adaptation/reconfiguration domain, completed the documentary Translation Trace with **Gate C = INDETERMINATE**. The limiting boundary was explicit: the available native sources identify network configurations, candidate reconfiguration operations and pre-outcome feasibility constraints, but do not close `Uτ,D`, accessibility membership, `T_acc,D` and `ΔT_acc,D` without analyst-supplied completion.

This decision does not reopen the frozen Core, C-01, TR-130, TR-131, or the historical D-OPS-24 v0.4 route.

## 2. Decision

Authorize **one and only one bounded constructive operationalization attempt within I-01** before considering a third independent domain or broader downstream execution.

The attempt shall target only the following closure chain:

`Uτ,D (closed) → Pτ,D (pre-outcome) → T_acc,D → ΔT_acc,D`

No Reach, Trajectory, Outcome, Value, causal, predictive, optimization, universal-generalization or Core-revision claim is authorized by this decision.

## 3. Scientific purpose

The operation is designed to discriminate between two explanations for the repeated boundary observed in C-01 and I-01:

- **H1 — domain-specific boundary:** the difficulty is primarily a property of the selected native domains/sources.
- **H2 — deeper operationalization boundary:** reconstructing `T_acc,D` and `ΔT_acc,D` in naturally occurring domains requires a level of discretization, bounding or closure not supplied by the native representation, making the boundary methodological rather than merely domain-specific.

The result may be PASS, PARTIAL or INDETERMINATE. An INDETERMINATE result caused by analyst-supplied completion is itself a valid methodological result and shall not be bypassed by further discretionary completion.

## 4. Required constructive target

The next design must identify a bounded native I-01 subdomain/unit for which:

1. `S_D` is explicitly and reproducibly representable;
2. `C_D` is explicitly representable;
3. `Uτ,D` is finite, discrete or otherwise natively bounded without analyst-invented discretization;
4. `Pτ,D` is a native pre-outcome feasibility/accessibility rule;
5. `T_acc,D = {τ ∈ Uτ,D | Pτ,D=1}` can be reconstructed without analyst completion;
6. at least one ordered state pair permits a defensible directed comparison `ΔT_acc,D+` and, where applicable, `ΔT_acc,D−`;
7. every closure step has source-level provenance.

## 5. Hard-stop conditions

Execution shall stop and return INDETERMINATE if closure requires any of the following unless independently native to the selected source:

- analyst-supplied discretization of a continuous transformation space;
- analyst-supplied arbitrary bounds or thresholds;
- outcome-defined accessibility;
- inferred candidate transformations not explicitly supported by the source;
- filling missing membership decisions by analyst judgment;
- importing a downstream Reach/Trajectory/Outcome/Value construct to force closure;
- changing the frozen TGCV definitions to fit the domain.

## 6. Scope exclusions

This decision does **not** authorize:

- a third independent domain;
- a new D-OPS-24 discovery family or QF budget;
- dataset acquisition or empirical processing;
- Gate D / ETC downstream execution;
- causal inference or prediction;
- value linkage or optimization;
- changes to TGCV Core;
- changes to C-01 conclusions;
- external-asset refresh.

## 7. Controlled sequence

The authorized sequence is:

**Decision → Constructive Operationalization Design → Design Audit → Preflight → Execution Authorization → one controlled I-01 attempt → Evidence→Claim impact assessment → propagation → consistency closure.**

No execution may begin before the design, audit, preflight and explicit execution authorization are closed successfully.

## 8. Stop/continue rule

- If the constructive attempt closes `Uτ,D`, `Pτ,D`, `T_acc,D` and `ΔT_acc,D` without analyst-supplied completion, record bounded constructive support and reassess the need for downstream work under a separate governance decision.
- If it fails for the same methodological reason, record H2 as strengthened methodological evidence and do **not** automatically open a third domain merely to repeat the same boundary.
- Any other outcome remains bounded and requires explicit governance interpretation before further work.

## 9. Governance effect

This decision opens a **design stage only**. It does not itself upgrade any scientific claim or authorize execution.

GitHub remains the canonical continuity/provenance surface; local execution material remains non-canonical.
