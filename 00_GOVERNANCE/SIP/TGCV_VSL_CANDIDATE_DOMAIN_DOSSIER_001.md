# TGCV — VSL Candidate Domain Dossier 001

**Status:** DRAFT / CANDIDATE-DOMAIN REGISTRY  
**Protocol:** VSL-EXP-01 v0.1 — FROZEN  
**VSL-SPEC-01:** v0.1 — FROZEN  
**Purpose:** Establish a neutral candidate-domain set and evidence inventory before compatibility assessment

## 1. Purpose

This dossier establishes the candidate-domain registry to be assessed under the frozen VSL-EXP-01 protocol.

It does not select, rank, score or recommend a candidate.

No candidate is considered compatible merely because it has previously been used in TGCV work.

The dossier separates candidate-domain existence, available domain evidence, potential Value-identification material, unresolved requirements and compatibility assessment.

Compatibility is not determined by this registry alone.

## 2. Frozen methodological basis

The assessment will use only the frozen requirements of VSL-SPEC-01 v0.1 and VSL-EXP-01 v0.1.

No candidate may redefine those requirements.

The required sequence is:

candidate evidence inventory -> compatibility assessment -> readiness disposition -> domain-specific VSL

## 3. Candidate registry

The initial registry contains four heterogeneous candidate domains because they already have distinct TGCV-related material in the programme and therefore permit evidence-led assessment rather than hypothetical domain construction.

### CD-01 — Aviation maintenance / regulatory alternative compliance

**Domain:** aircraft maintenance and regulatory alternative means of compliance (AMOC).

**Potential unit of analysis:** aircraft/component maintenance case, maintenance action or compliance episode.

**Potential Outcome material:** documented technical/compliance result associated with the maintenance case.

**Potential evaluative perspectives requiring explicit specification:** operator, regulator, maintenance organization or another declared beneficiary/decision perspective.

**Potential reference:** baseline compliance configuration, conventional compliance route, or other pre-specified alternative.

**Value status at registry stage:** NOT YET ASSESSED.

**Known methodological issue:** existing TGCV reconstruction material is not automatically a Value specification. A domain-specific Outcome-to-Value mapping must be independently established before execution.

**Evidence provenance:** existing IT-G1 / AMOC-related TGCV reconstruction material in the canonical repository.

### CD-02 — Electricity-system technical constraint / transformation domain

**Domain:** heterogeneous electricity-system operation and technical constraints.

**Potential unit of analysis:** defined electricity-system episode, configuration or transformation episode.

**Potential Outcome material:** domain-specific technical outcome associated with system configuration or transformation.

**Potential evaluative perspectives requiring explicit specification:** system operator, asset owner, consumer or other explicitly declared perspective.

**Potential reference:** pre-specified operating baseline, alternative configuration, benchmark or counterfactual.

**Value status at registry stage:** NOT YET ASSESSED.

**Known methodological issue:** technical performance is not automatically Value. Costs, benefits, trade-offs, perspective and reference must be independently specified.

**Evidence provenance:** existing MT4 / electricity-system TGCV material in the canonical repository.

### CD-03 — EV–Grid application-fit domain

**Domain:** EV–Grid synthetic application-fit demonstrator.

**Potential unit of analysis:** frozen synthetic EV–Grid scenario/episode.

**Potential Outcome material:** downstream state/trajectory result produced under the frozen demonstrator.

**Potential evaluative perspectives:** synthetic perspective only, if explicitly declared.

**Potential reference:** frozen synthetic baseline or other independently frozen comparator.

**Value status at registry stage:** NOT YET ASSESSED.

**Known methodological issue:** because the domain is synthetic, any Value construct would be synthetic and domain-bounded. It cannot be used to infer real-world Value without a separate empirical basis.

**Evidence provenance:** canonical C05 EV–Grid Minimum Demonstrator material.

### CD-04 — Empirical social/infrastructure outcome domain

**Domain:** empirical social/infrastructure intervention datasets previously examined in TGCV boundary cases.

**Potential unit of analysis:** pre-specified geographic, household, individual or intervention episode, depending on the dataset.

**Potential Outcome material:** observed downstream social/infrastructure outcome variables.

**Potential evaluative perspectives:** must be explicitly established; outcome variables cannot be assumed to constitute Value.

**Potential reference:** must be fixed before outcome inspection and must be justified from the domain evidence.

**Value status at registry stage:** NOT YET ASSESSED.

**Known methodological issue:** prior empirical outcome variables and treatment/take-up variables do not by themselves establish a domain-specific Value construct or TGCV accessibility predicate.

**Evidence provenance:** existing C10C boundary-case material in the canonical repository.

## 4. Registry neutrality

The four candidates are registry entries, not recommendations.

The dossier intentionally does not assign scores, ranks, tiers, winners, estimated experimental success or expected claim contribution.

A candidate may subsequently be excluded because VALUE_NOT_IDENTIFIED_BLOCKED or because information is insufficient. Such an exclusion is a methodological result, not a ranking.

## 5. Required evidence inventory before assessment

For each candidate, the following must be retrieved and documented from canonical repository evidence before C01-C12 are assessed:

1. exact domain and population/unit;
2. observable/reconstructable Outcome variables;
3. available evaluative perspectives;
4. candidate reference or counterfactual information;
5. time horizon;
6. cost/benefit/trade-off information;
7. aggregation requirements;
8. uncertainty and missingness;
9. provenance and reproducibility constraints;
10. evidence establishing that Value can be specified independently of TGCV transformation/accessibility variables.

A missing item remains unresolved until supported by evidence.

## 6. Assessment rule

No compatibility status is assigned in this registry.

For each candidate, the next assessment artifact must record C01-C12 exactly as defined by frozen VSL-EXP-01 and must distinguish observed domain fact, reconstruction, methodological inference, unresolved issue and non-claim.

No compatibility determination may be based on the proposition that an appropriate Value measure “should exist”.

## 7. Candidate-specific VSL prohibition

No domain-specific VSL is to be constructed yet.

Construction is authorized only after a candidate receives VALUE_IDENTIFIED_READY under VSL-EXP-01.

A VALUE_PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED_READY disposition does not authorize silently expanding the Value construct.
