# TGCV — Cross-Domain Map Evaluation and Persistence Decision 001

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** CLOSED — ANALYTICAL MAP RETAINED AS INDEPENDENT ARTIFACT  
**Related artifact:** `TGCV_CROSS_DOMAIN_END_TO_END_ACCESSIBILITY_OUTCOME_VALUE_MAP_001.md`

## Decision

The cross-domain map is evaluated as methodologically useful and sufficiently stable to retain as an independent analytical synthesis, but not yet as a normative governance methodology and not as evidence of general TGCV validation.

## Findings

1. The map consolidates distributed evidence for the chain `State / conditions → candidate transformations → admissibility Pτ → T_acc → ΔT_acc / execution → subsequent state / trajectory → outcome → ΔV / Value`.
2. Its principal contribution is separation and assembly of analytical layers, not a claim that every layer has been demonstrated end-to-end in one empirical case.
3. Operationalisation evidence is stronger and more mature than transversal causal validation.
4. C09 currently supplies bounded causal evidence for the accessibility → subsequent-trajectory link.
5. C10C-004 materially strengthens the methodological case for semantic-to-executable reconstruction in a non-software domain, but does not identify TGCV `T_acc`.
6. C10C-001 and C10C-003 are important boundary cases because structural change, treatment, take-up/adoption and longitudinal outcomes are not silently relabeled as accessibility.
7. Value linkage remains open: the evidence base does not establish `ΔT_acc → ΔV`.

## Persistence decision

Persist in GitHub:

- the independent cross-domain map itself;
- this evaluation/decision record;
- the fact that the map is an analytical synthesis layer between experimental evidence and future methodological consolidation;
- the explicit non-upgrade and open-Value boundaries.

Do not yet persist as a governance-level methodological rule:

- a new formal TGCV primitive;
- a new claim level;
- a transversal-validity closure;
- a `T_acc → Value` causal relation;
- a replacement or modification of TGCV Core.

## Canonical-state implication

The map is now a persisted independent analytical artifact. Canonical state should therefore record its existence and disposition, while the current RMA and Evidence-to-Claim Matrix remain unchanged in substance.

The current Matrix v1.11 already contains C10C-004 propagation and the relevant cross-domain boundary evidence. This analysis does not warrant another Matrix version by itself.

## Next analysis authorized

Proceed next to test whether a transversal methodology can be extracted from the map **without introducing concepts that are not already empirically or formally supported**. That analysis must distinguish:

- ontology inherited from TGCV Core;
- operational translation rules supported by evidence;
- empirical gates and boundary tests;
- optional methodological conventions;
- still-open hypotheses, especially downstream Value linkage.

Any resulting methodology proposal must remain separate from claim upgrades until independently reviewed.
