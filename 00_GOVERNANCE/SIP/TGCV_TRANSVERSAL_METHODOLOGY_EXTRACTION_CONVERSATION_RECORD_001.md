## 1. Starting point

The next authorized analytical direction after retaining the cross-domain accessibility → execution → outcome/value map is to test whether a transversal methodology can be extracted from that map while preserving strict separation between:

- ontology inherited from TGCV Core;
- evidence-supported operational translation rules;
- empirical gates and boundary tests;
- optional methodological conventions;
- open hypotheses, especially downstream Value linkage.

No modification of TGCV Core, RMA, Evidence-to-Claim Matrix, or claim status is authorized by this record.

## 2. Conversation decision

The working extraction identified eight candidate translation rules:

1. **State → candidate transformations (`Uτ`)**: reconstruct relevant state/conditions and derive a candidate transformation universe from it.
2. **Candidate transformations → admissibility (`Pτ`)**: define admissibility explicitly before identifying accessible transformations.
3. **Admissibility → `T_acc`**: construct accessible transformations from the admissible universe under an explicit operational definition, rather than from observed results.
4. **`T_acc` independent of Outcome/Value**: accessibility must not be constructed from the downstream outcome or value endpoint.
5. **`ΔT_acc` → execution/trajectory**: distinguish accessibility change from transformations actually executed and from subsequent trajectories.
6. **Trajectory → Outcome**: identify subsequent outcomes without treating the outcome itself as proof of accessibility.
7. **Outcome ≠ Value by default**: a conventional or domain-specific outcome must not automatically be relabeled as TGCV Value.
8. **`ΔT_acc` → `ΔV`**: downstream value linkage remains an open hypothesis; no transversal causal rule has been established.

## 4. Evidence boundary preserved

The cross-domain map establishes that the accumulated evidence can operationalize and audit parts of the chain

`State / conditions → candidate transformations → admissibility Pτ → T_acc → ΔT_acc / execution → subsequent state / trajectory → outcome → ΔV / Value`

but does not establish every link in every case. In particular, downstream `ΔT_acc → Value` remains open.

C10C-002 is a relevant anchor because its bounded structural reconstruction explicitly defines a 12-family transformation universe, reconstructs `T_acc,0*`, `T_acc,1*` and `ΔT_acc*`, and keeps value variables external to accessibility construction. Its causal test was bounded to `Z → ΔT_acc*` and was closed as a negative/non-detectable bounded causal result; it does not establish a value pathway.

C10C-004 contributes methodological reconstruction evidence from semantic instrument specification through executable historical transformation to reproducible outcome, but does not identify its domain-specific empowerment construct as TGCV `T_acc`.
