## Scope qualification carried into propagation

The inspection searched for a **bounded, prospectively defensible operational transformation space** derivable from the frozen replication package. It did not attempt exhaustive enumeration of the complete universe of transformations potentially conceivable for the experiment.

This must not be represented as proof that the experiment's complete transformation universe is intrinsically unbounded, intractable, or incompatible with TGCV.
