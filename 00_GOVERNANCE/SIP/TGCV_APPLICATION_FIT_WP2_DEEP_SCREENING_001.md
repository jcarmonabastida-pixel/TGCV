## 1. Purpose

This record starts the second WP2 pass: deep-screening a bounded subset of the eight initial opportunity hypotheses.

The selection is **not a ranking** and does not imply that selected candidates are better, more valuable, more feasible, or more likely to validate TGCV. The subset is selected for **structural diversity and information value for the multidomain hypothesis**.

The central discrimination test is:

`multidomain coexistence` vs `transformational coupling`

The deep-screening question is whether, after explicit reconstruction of states, candidate transformations and admissibility conditions, a transition in one domain changes the accessible transformation conditions of another domain and thereby changes a subsequent coupled trajectory.

## 4. Deep-screening reconstruction

For each selected candidate, the screening will attempt to identify, with the strongest available bounded evidence:

1. **Decision-time boundary** — what is frozen at the relevant decision point?
2. **State/context** — `S_t, C_t` and relevant exogenous conditions.
3. **Candidate transformations** — concrete actions or transitions that could occur from that state.
4. **Admissibility rule** — operational or reconstructable `P_τ(S_t,C_t,L)` independent of the later outcome where possible.
5. **Accessible transformation space** — `T_acc,t` or a bounded local/candidate transformation space when exhaustive enumeration is neither feasible nor necessary.
6. **Transition/intervention** — what changes between the two states?
7. **Post-transition accessibility** — `T_acc,t+1` and the specific transformations newly available, unavailable or reordered.
8. **Cross-domain propagation** — whether the change in one domain materially modifies the transformation conditions of another domain.
9. **Downstream trajectory** — whether subsequent actions/outcomes depend on the changed accessibility structure.
10. **Alternative explanation** — whether the same pattern is fully represented by ordinary coordination, dependency, capability, workflow, optimisation or control language without requiring a transformation-space layer.
11. **Maturation route** — scientific evidence, applied demonstration, deployment/integration, or independent value evaluation.
12. **Minimum next proof** — the smallest controlled artefact, simulation, reconstruction or empirical study capable of resolving the main remaining uncertainty.

## 5. Screening disposition vocabulary

Each candidate will receive a bounded disposition, not a score or ranking:

- **COUPLING-CANDIDATE** — cross-domain transformational coupling remains structurally plausible after initial reconstruction.
- **COEXISTENCE-CANDIDATE** — multiple domains are present, but the coupling currently reduces to coordination/context rather than changed accessibility.
- **INSUFFICIENT-RECONSTRUCTION** — the public or available evidence does not permit the required reconstruction.
- **DEMO-SUITABLE** — a controlled Applied Demonstration can resolve the main applicability uncertainty.
- **SCIENTIFIC-ROUTE-SUITABLE** — a bounded scientific study appears capable of testing a defined TGCV proposition.
- **DEFERRED** — retained without further deepening until a specific structural gap is identified.

These dispositions are exploratory WP2 states and are not TGCV scientific claims.

## 7. Evidence discipline

Public descriptions may establish that a problem is multidomain, but they do not by themselves establish `T_acc`, `ΔT_acc`, causal effects, TGCV differential value, or Value.

The deep-screen therefore distinguishes:

- documented architecture/problem structure;
- inferred candidate transformations;
- reconstructable accessibility conditions;
- demonstrated transitions;
- observed downstream trajectories;
- interpretations requiring further evidence.

No vendor claim, benchmark result, optimisation result, technical demonstration or reported operational benefit is silently converted into TGCV evidence.
