**Date:** 2026-09-17

## 1. Purpose

Provide a minimal, non-committal first-contact package derived from MT5-VSL-20 Q1–Q7 for four target types identified in MT5-VSL-21.

This package is designed to discover whether a concrete prospective context exists. It does not request participant data, ask the recipient to validate TGCV, or imply that a study has already been designed or approved.

## 2. Common scientific description

TGCV is being developed as a research programme studying whether a system's transformation conditions and subsequent trajectories can be analysed through changes in the transformations accessible to the system.

For this specific methodological route, the research team is exploring a prospective setting where two analytically separate components can be measured:

`system transformation → independently specified ΔT_acc → subsequent trajectory → pre-specified individual financial-well-being measurement`

The financial-well-being measurement would be frozen before observing treatment outcomes and would remain independent of the operationalization of system accessibility.

## 3. Common seven-question screen

1. **Concrete context:** Do you have a named prospective programme, deployment or applied-research study in which a system or service transformation will occur or be observed?
2. **Participants:** Will identifiable individual participants be recruited or enrolled through a prospective recruitment frame?
3. **Transformation:** Can the initial and resulting system/service states be specified independently of financial-well-being outcomes?
4. **Repeated measurement:** Can the same individuals be measured before and after the relevant transition within a pre-specified window?
5. **Financial well-being:** Would administration of the CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale, or another independently justified external financial-well-being instrument, be feasible before and after the transition?
6. **Reproducibility:** Could item-level responses and required scoring metadata be retained so that an independent analyst could reproduce the Value measurement, subject to ethics/privacy constraints?
7. **Governance:** Is there an identifiable ethics/privacy/data-access pathway and a responsible study lead with whom a detailed protocol could be discussed?

No participant-level data or treatment results are requested at first contact.

## 8. Screening response template

If a recipient expresses interest, request only:

- study/programme name;
- responsible organization and study lead;
- site/geography;
- target individual population and recruitment frame;
- system/service transformation;
- expected baseline/post timing;
- proposed language and administration mode;
- existing or planned financial-well-being instrument;
- data-access/reproduction feasibility;
- ethics/privacy status.

Do not request raw participant data at this stage.

## 9. Advancement rule

Responses are classified as:

- `OPPORTUNITY ONLY` — interest or methodological compatibility without a concrete context;
- `CONDITIONAL CONTEXT` — concrete prospective context exists, but one or more C1–C12 conditions remain unresolved;
- `QUALIFIED CONTEXT` — C1–C12 are evidenced sufficiently to construct a frozen material package;
- `INCOMPATIBLE` — the context requires retrospective endpoint construction, proxy scoring, outcome-dependent VSL modification, or lacks independently reconstructible `ΔT_acc`.

## 10. No-go communication rules

No outreach message may:

- state or imply that TGCV is an established universal theory;
- ask the recipient to choose outcomes because they are expected to improve;
- define financial wellbeing as Value merely because it is desirable;
- promise a causal `ΔT_acc → ΔV*` result;
- request participant data before a valid access pathway exists;
- imply that participation validates TGCV;
- imply that participation guarantees doctoral funding or publication.

## 11. Governance boundary

This package authorizes preparation of external first-contact communication only.

It does not authorize sending messages, recruitment, intervention, translation, pilot execution, confirmatory Value measurement, causal estimation, or modifications to Core/RMA/Evidence→Claim Matrix/STATUS/C09/M9.
