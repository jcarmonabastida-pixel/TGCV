# TGCV — MT5-VSL-19 External Partner / Study Target Search and Qualification 001

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Status:** `CLOSED — NO QUALIFIED EXTERNAL DEPLOYMENT TARGET IDENTIFIED`

## 1. Purpose

Apply the frozen MT5-VSL-18 C1–C12 target profile to current external opportunities and determine whether any concrete partner/study can be admitted as a prospective TGCV Value-measurement context.

This is an opportunity qualification audit, not recruitment or experimental execution.

## 2. Search boundary

Search focused on:
- CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale deployment/collaboration;
- prospective programme evaluation using the scale;
- financial-inclusion and financial-service interventions;
- European/Spanish research and doctoral infrastructure capable of establishing prospective field studies.

The audit distinguishes public methodological evidence from an actual accessible study context.

## 3. Candidate targets reviewed

### T1 — CFPB Project Catalyst / research collaboration route

CFPB documents that Project Catalyst has conducted research collaborations with organizations testing financial products/services and that organizations may apply to participate in research pilots. A documented Credit Karma collaboration used the CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale and related financial-information engagement data. citeturn0search1

**Qualification:** `OPPORTUNITY ONLY`.

The public material does not establish a currently available TGCV deployment with named prospective site, independently reconstructible `ΔT_acc`, recruitment frame, partner access agreement and all C1–C12 conditions simultaneously satisfied.

### T2 — CFPB programme-use ecosystem

CFPB documentation provides concrete precedents in which financial coaches administer the scale at intake and quarterly intervals, and organizations use individual scores to assess progress and programmes. CFPB explicitly recommends consistent administration and retaining responses/scores for longitudinal comparison. citeturn0search4turn0search9

**Qualification:** `SCIENTIFIC/DEPLOYMENT PRECEDENT — NOT A SUBMITTED TGCV CONTEXT`.

The public case material does not identify a currently accessible prospective study that independently specifies a TGCV transformation and `ΔT_acc`.

### T3 — CFPB scale infrastructure

The official CFPB guide provides the standardized scale, questionnaires and scoring materials, including Spanish materials, and describes the scale as an individual-level measure designed for consistent observation and comparison over time. citeturn0search0turn0search6

**Qualification:** `INSTRUMENT INFRASTRUCTURE — NOT A DEPLOYMENT TARGET`.

### T4 — CFPB National Financial Well-Being Survey

The CFPB public survey infrastructure contains individual scale scores and individual/household characteristics. citeturn0search15

**Qualification:** `REFERENCE/VALIDATION DATA — NOT A PROSPECTIVE TGCV DEPLOYMENT CONTEXT`.

It lacks the required prospective system transformation and treatment-independent `ΔT_acc` transition needed by MT5-VSL-18.

### T5 — MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

The 2026 MSCA Doctoral Networks call supports consortia involving higher-education institutions, research infrastructures, businesses and public-sector organizations. The call deadline is 24 November 2026. citeturn0search3turn0search5

**Qualification:** `HOSTING/FUNDING OPPORTUNITY — NOT A STUDY CONTEXT`.

The call provides an institutional route for constructing a future project but does not itself supply a named population, field site, transformation, Value instrument deployment or item-level data pathway.

## 5. Qualification result

No target satisfies all C1–C12.

The most mature routes are:

- CFPB collaboration/programme ecosystem: real prospective measurement precedent and collaboration mechanism;
- MSCA Doctoral Networks: real institutional mechanism for constructing a new prospective doctoral study.

Neither currently supplies the complete TGCV context.

## 6. Critical bottleneck

The unresolved requirement is not availability of a Value instrument. The CFPB scale is publicly specified, includes scoring materials and supports repeated individual measurement. citeturn0search0turn0search2

The unresolved requirement is the **joint prospective context** in which:

`concrete system transformation + independently reconstructible ΔT_acc + individual recruitment + frozen VSL + item-level longitudinal measurement + independent reproduction`

can all be established before treatment outcomes are observed.

## 7. Fail-closed disposition

No target is promoted to:
- pilot context;
- partner status;
- recruitment;
- measurement execution;
- causal Value experiment.

No retrospective C09 CFPB reconstruction is permitted.
