# TGCV MT4 — Evidence-to-Claim Propagation 001

## Status
**MATERIAL EVIDENCE PROPAGATION — NO CLAIM-LEVEL UPGRADE**

## Source result
`00_GOVERNANCE/SIP/TGCV_MT4_DOMAIN_TRANSFER_RESULT_001.md`

MT4 closes as a bounded methodological domain-transfer result in a heterogeneous historical electricity-system domain. The evidence demonstrates reproducible preservation of distinctions among candidate technical constraints, realized transformations, state/trajectory variables and economic/value-related variables. Full `P_tau` and a downstream TGCV value endpoint remain unresolved.

## Claim propagation

### C01 — State/context/conditions and constraints/resources
**Impact:** bounded methodological support.
MT4 demonstrates that the candidate methodology can identify and preserve a technical constraint/state layer in a non-software electricity-system domain. This does not establish a universal state representation or upgrade C01.

### C02 — Accessibility via independently defined admissibility
**Impact:** bounded qualification.
MT4 provides a partially formalized technical candidate admissibility layer (`CORE6`) with 92.58% complete country-technology-year coverage, but full `P_tau` remains undetermined. The result is evidence that the admissibility distinction can be operationalized and audited in a new domain, not evidence that the candidate rule is complete or general.

### C07 — Accessible transformation spaces change over time
**Impact:** boundary qualification only.
MT4 distinguishes realized technology changes from accessibility-space change and does not substitute `Actual_*` variables for `T_acc` or `Delta T_acc`. No positive `Delta T_acc` claim is propagated.

### C08 — Accessibility changes modify future trajectories
**Impact:** bounded methodological support only.
MT4 establishes downstream separation of candidate constraints, realized transformations and state/trajectory variables, but does not establish a trajectory causal estimand in this domain. No upgrade.

### C09 — Accessibility changes causally affect subsequent trajectories
**Impact:** no positive propagation.
MT4 contains no causal estimate of `Delta T_acc -> trajectory`; therefore C09 remains at its existing bounded empirical causal-support status without MT4 being used as positive causal evidence.

### C10 — Accessibility changes generate/predict value
**Impact:** material boundary qualification.
MT4-8 shows that `Inv`, `Fixed_OM_annual` and `Variable_OM` can be structurally isolated from the CORE6 candidate accessibility rule, but the audit does not establish these variables as downstream TGCV value outcomes. No `Delta T_acc -> Delta V` evidence is added. C10 remains open.

### C11 — Domain-independent / transversal
**Impact:** bounded methodological cross-domain evidence.
MT4 adds a genuinely heterogeneous electricity-system domain to the documented transfer evidence. It strengthens the empirical record of methodological transfer and boundary preservation but does not establish transversal validity. No upgrade.

### C16 — Transversal analytical translation protocol
**Impact:** material positive methodological evidence, bounded.
MT4 demonstrates that the candidate translation sequence can be applied while preserving separation among state, candidate transformations, admissibility/accessibility, realized transformation, trajectory and economic/value-related variables. This supports C16's methodological evidence base but does not upgrade its claim level.
