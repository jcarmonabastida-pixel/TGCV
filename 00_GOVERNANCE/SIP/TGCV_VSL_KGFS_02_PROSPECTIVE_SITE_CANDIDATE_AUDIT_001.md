# TGCV — VSL-KGFS-02 Prospective Site Candidate Audit 001

**Status:** CANDIDATE SITE IDENTIFIED — NOT A VSL FREEZE  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Candidate:** CD-05 / C09 KGFS Rural Banking

## 1. Purpose

Select a concrete prospective KGFS context for the remaining language and administration compatibility audit, independently of any Value or outcome result.

## 2. Candidate site

The proposed prospective context is:

**Tamil Nadu KGFS — Thanjavur / Thiruvarur area**

This is a methodological candidate, not a selected experimental winner.

## 3. Basis for candidate identification

Dvara Research documents Pudhuaaru KGFS in Thanjavur and Thiruvarur districts of Tamil Nadu as one of the original KGFS operating contexts.

Dvara KGFS's documented operational footprint also establishes Tamil Nadu as a major KGFS geography.

Independent Dvara research involving Dvara KGFS customers has documented interviews in Tamil Nadu and states that interviews were conducted in Tamil, Kannada, Hindi and Odia, with questionnaires translated from English into those languages. The Tamil Nadu locations in that study included Ariyalur, Thanjavur and surrounding locations.

These facts establish a concrete and relevant context in which Tamil-language administration is a plausible prospective configuration.

## 4. What this selection does and does not establish

It establishes:

- a concrete KGFS geography for compatibility analysis;
- a relevant Tamil-language context to audit;
- an existing KGFS research precedent in Tamil Nadu;
- an individual respondent precedent in Dvara KGFS customer research.

It does **not** establish:

- validity of the CFPB scale in Tamil;
- validated translation equivalence;
- validity of the scale specifically for KGFS customers;
- the exact future study site or branch;
- an administration mode;
- household aggregation;
- causal effects;
- Value evidence.

## 5. Language gate

For the candidate context, the required prospective language is provisionally:

**Tamil**

The existence of Tamil-language interviewing and questionnaire translation in prior Dvara KGFS research supports feasibility of Tamil-language research administration.

It does not by itself validate a Tamil CFPB scale.

**Result: CANDIDATE LANGUAGE = IDENTIFIED**

**Result: CFPB TAMIL EQUIVALENCE = NOT ESTABLISHED**

## 6. Respondent unit

The prospective Value construct remains individual-level.

A respondent must therefore be explicitly sampled and linked to a complete admissible instrument response vector.

No household Value score is authorized by this candidate selection.

**Result: INDIVIDUAL RESPONDENT = REQUIRED**

**Result: HOUSEHOLD AGGREGATION = NOT ESTABLISHED**

## 7. Administration gate

Prior Dvara KGFS research demonstrates interviewer-administered questionnaires in local languages, but that evidence does not automatically establish that the CFPB scale can use the same mode under its scoring rules.

The exact administration mode must therefore be selected and frozen only after checking the CFPB source requirements.

**Result: ADMINISTRATION = OPEN**

## 8. Translation/equivalence gate

The candidate context makes a translation audit concrete, but no validated Tamil CFPB version has yet been established in the canonical evidence base.

Therefore no translation may be frozen merely because Tamil-language Dvara research exists.

**Result: TRANSLATION/EQUIVALENCE = OPEN / CRITICAL**

## 9. Independence from outcomes

The candidate was selected solely from:

- documented KGFS geography;
- documented prior KGFS research;
- documented language context.

No treatment effect, accessibility change, trajectory or Value outcome was used.

**Result: SITE-SELECTION INDEPENDENCE = PASS**

## 10. Decision

The programme may now perform a **Tamil CFPB translation/equivalence and administration audit for the Thanjavur/Thiruvarur KGFS context**.

This does not freeze VSL-KGFS-02.

Current state:

`VSL-KGFS-02 = NOT_FROZEN`

`SITE_CANDIDATE = TAMIL_NADU / THANJAVUR-THIRUVARUR`

`COMPATIBILITY = NOT_ESTABLISHED`
