# TGCV — VSL-KGFS-02 Population / Administration Evidence Audit 002

**Status:** CLOSED — COMPATIBILITY NOT ESTABLISHED; PROSPECTIVE ROUTE REMAINS CONDITIONAL  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Candidate:** CD-05 / C09 KGFS Rural Banking  
**Specification:** TGCV_VSL_KGFS_02_PROSPECTIVE_EXTERNAL_STANDARD_COMPATIBILITY_SPECIFICATION_v0.1.md

## 1. Purpose

Determine whether currently available external evidence is sufficient to close the remaining population, language, administration and unit-of-analysis gates for prospective use of the CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale in a KGFS-compatible study.

## 2. Source-standard facts

The CFPB documentation establishes a 10-item Financial Well-Being Scale designed to measure individual financial well-being and provides a defined scoring procedure. Current CFPB guidance requires the questionnaire wording and response options to remain unchanged and uses respondent responses together with age group and administration mode in scoring.

**Result: SOURCE INSTRUMENT / SCORING = PASS.**

## 3. India evidence

Independent published work demonstrates use of the CFPB scale in India. One Indian study explicitly used the original five-item CFPB scale to measure perceived financial well-being. Another Indian study used the CFPB scale among healthcare professionals.

A 2025 India-wide study also used the CFPB scale as a benchmark against a newly developed consumer financial-well-being scale, with data representing regions of India.

This establishes Indian research use, not automatic validity for the KGFS rural-finance population.

**Result: INDIA USE / RESEARCH PRECEDENT = PASS.**

**Result: KGFS-SPECIFIC VALIDITY = NOT ESTABLISHED.**

## 4. Population compatibility

The available evidence does not establish measurement equivalence or validated performance specifically for KGFS rural-finance beneficiaries, the intended rural Indian linguistic population, the respondent recruitment frame, or the intended administration conditions.

The CFPB original scale-development evidence is rooted in U.S. survey samples, so external validity to KGFS cannot be assumed from the original validation alone.

**Result: POPULATION COMPATIBILITY = OPEN / CRITICAL.**

## 5. Language

The CFPB provides English and Spanish materials. The existence of those versions does not establish a validated version for the exact language(s) required by a KGFS prospective study.

A local-language translation/adaptation must therefore have an independently defensible equivalence procedure before it can enter the frozen VSL.

**Result: LANGUAGE COMPATIBILITY = OPEN.**

## 6. Administration mode

The CFPB scoring procedure incorporates administration mode. Therefore the prospective KGFS protocol must freeze the exact administration mode and ensure that it matches an admissible source-standard configuration.

Evidence that the instrument has been used in India does not, by itself, establish equivalence of administration conditions.

**Result: ADMINISTRATION COMPATIBILITY = OPEN.**

## 7. Individual versus household

The external score is an individual respondent measure. The CFPB public-use survey is documented at individual level.

Historical C09 household-level structures cannot be converted into individual CFPB scores.

A prospective design must therefore collect the instrument from identifiable individual respondents. Any later household aggregation would require a separate pre-specified rule and justification.

**Result: INDIVIDUAL RESPONDENT UNIT = SOURCE-COMPATIBLE.**

**Result: HOUSEHOLD AGGREGATION = NOT ESTABLISHED.**

## 8. Measurement fidelity

The CFPB explicitly requires unchanged questionnaire wording/response options for proper operation and comparability.

Therefore no C09 proxy substitution, item deletion, unvalidated local rewording, post-hoc recoding, or analyst-defined replacement score is permitted.

**Result: MEASUREMENT FIDELITY REQUIREMENT = PASS / FROZEN PRINCIPLE.**

## 9. Missingness

The CFPB guidance requires complete responses for its standard lookup scoring route and separately describes handling through IRT methods where missing responses occur.

A future KGFS protocol must choose and freeze one admissible scoring/missingness pathway before execution.

**Result: SOURCE MISSINGNESS RULE = PASS.**

**Result: KGFS IMPLEMENTATION = OPEN.**

## 10. Decision

The evidence is sufficient to establish a credible prospective measurement route, but not sufficient to freeze VSL-KGFS-02.

The unresolved issue is whether the instrument can be administered and interpreted as a valid measure in the specific KGFS target population and language/administration context.

**VSL-KGFS-02 = NOT FROZEN**

**Prospective route = CONDITIONALLY OPEN**

**Historical C09 route = CLOSED**

## 11. Required next gate

Before freeze, a prospective KGFS package must provide:

1. exact target respondent definition;
2. exact target language(s);
3. translation/equivalence protocol;
4. administration mode;
5. evidence/plan for population validity;
6. individual-level sampling and response linkage;
7. reference/time protocol;
8. missingness/scoring path;
9. independent reconstruction procedure.

No Value experiment is authorized before these conditions are frozen.

## 12. Governance

No change to C09, Core, RMA or Evidence-to-Claim Matrix.

Historical C09 remains VALUE_NOT_IDENTIFIED_BLOCKED.

The prospective route remains a methodological possibility only.
