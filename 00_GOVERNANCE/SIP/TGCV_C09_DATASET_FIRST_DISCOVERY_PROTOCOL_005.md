# TGCV C09 — Dataset-First Discovery Protocol 005

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Status:** FROZEN — MULTICAUSAL CAUSAL-CONTRIBUTION D5 ADOPTED
**Supersedes:** `TGCV_C09_DATASET_FIRST_DISCOVERY_PROTOCOL_004.md` for future C09 discovery
**Scope:** C09 real-world candidate discovery and causal-contribution interpretation

## 1. Purpose

Refine C09 after repeated candidate screens showed that treating `T_acc` as if it had to be the unique causal bridge to `Y` is unnecessarily restrictive for real-world systems.

Real outcomes/value normally depend on multiple simultaneous determinants, mechanisms and interactions. The C09 requirement is therefore not that `T_acc` explain the total outcome, but that the **causal contribution of a structural accessibility change `Delta T_acc` to a downstream trajectory/outcome can be identified, bounded or otherwise defensibly characterized within that multicausal system**.

This revision preserves the scientific requirement for causal identification while replacing the implicit monocausal interpretation of D5.

## 2. Strategic decision

The discovery route remains:

`public dataset → early public-data feasibility → stable unit → structural state transition → intervention → downstream trajectory → causal-contribution architecture → full provenance/admission`

The key causal target is now explicitly contribution-oriented:

`Delta T_acc -> Y | other relevant causal mechanisms`

rather than an exclusive pathway claim:

`Z -> Delta T_acc -> Y` as the sole explanation of `Y`.

The existence of other causal pathways is **not itself a D5 failure**. The failure condition is inability to identify, bound or otherwise defensibly characterize the contribution attributable to `Delta T_acc`.

## 3. Multicausal system representation

A realistic candidate may have:

`Y = f(Delta T_acc, X1, X2, ..., Xk, interactions, epsilon)`

and may contain concurrent paths such as:

`Z -> Delta T_acc -> Y`
`Z -> X1 -> Y`
`Z -> X2 -> Y`
`X3 -> Y`
`Delta T_acc <-> other system mechanisms`

TGCV does not require `Delta T_acc` to be the only determinant, dominant determinant, or sole mediator of `Y`.

The empirical question is whether its causal contribution can be separated from the other relevant mechanisms under an explicit estimand and defensible assumptions/design.

## 4. D0–D6 pipeline

Protocols 003 and 004 remain the operational basis for public-data feasibility and cross-source fusion. Their D6-E and D6-E-F gates remain in force.

D0 continues to prioritize mechanism-separation architectures, but ranking should now also favor designs that permit estimation of a **causal contribution / controlled or interventional effect of `Delta T_acc`** in a multicausal system.

High-priority signatures include:

1. factorial/separate randomization of accessibility-producing and complementary components;
2. randomized accessibility intensity;
3. encouragement/two-stage designs with explicit exclusion or bounded direct effects;
4. repeated mediator measurements plus rich baseline covariates supporting an interventional mediated estimand;
5. multiple mediators/pathways with sufficient measurement to estimate controlled or interventional contributions;
6. principal-stratification/compliance architectures;
7. informative partial-identification bounds for the accessibility contribution;
8. cross-source architectures where structural access and downstream value are generated by different institutions but the contribution estimand remains identifiable/bounded.

## 5. D1 — Structural transition

Unchanged from Protocol 004.

The same stable unit must support a defensible structural transition `S_0 -> S_1` and independently defined `T_acc,0`, `T_acc,1`, hence `Delta T_acc`.

`T_acc` must represent structural accessibility/capability and must not be defined by downstream behaviour, adoption, use, treatment status alone or outcome.

## 6. D2 — Intervention

Unchanged from Protocol 004.

`Z` must be identifiable and distinguishable from `T_acc,1`.

The protocol does not require `T_acc,1` to be unaffected by `Z`; indeed, the intervention may be the causal source of the structural accessibility change.

## 7. D3 — Stable unit

Unchanged from Protocol 004.

The unit must support:

`unit_i,t0 -> unit_i,t1 -> Y_i,t>1`.

## 8. D4 — Downstream trajectory

Unchanged from Protocol 004.

`Y` must be a subsequent trajectory/outcome/value variable independent of the mere existence of the accessibility transition.

The existence of other determinants of `Y` is expected and does not invalidate D4.

## 9. D5 — Causal Contribution / Mechanism-Identification Gate

D5 is now explicitly a **Causal Contribution / Mechanism-Identification Gate**.

It asks:

> Can the causal contribution of `Delta T_acc` to `Y` be identified, bounded or otherwise defensibly characterized while accounting for relevant competing mechanisms and pathways?

### D5.0 — Contribution estimand defined

Before evaluating identification, the candidate must define the target estimand. Examples include:

- controlled direct/mediated contribution;
- interventional indirect effect attributable to changing the accessibility component;
- incremental effect of changing `T_acc` while holding specified complementary components fixed;
- principal-stratum contribution under explicitly defined potential accessibility states;
- bounded accessibility-mediated contribution under partial identification.

A vague statement that accessibility “affects” `Y` is insufficient.

### D5.1 — First-stage identification

There must be a defensible causal estimate or bound for:

`Z -> Delta T_acc`.

The first stage must identify a structural accessibility change, not merely reproduce treatment assignment.

### D5.2 — Contribution identification

At least one defensible strategy must identify or bound the contribution of `Delta T_acc` to `Y` in the presence of other causal determinants.

Admissible architectures include:

- independently randomized accessibility component;
- factorial/separate-randomization design;
- valid encouragement/instrument with explicit exclusion or bounded direct effects;
- controlled/interventional mediated estimand under explicitly stated assumptions;
- principal-stratum estimand with identifiable/bounded interpretation;
- measured competing mechanisms with appropriate path-specific or controlled-effect identification;
- informative partial-identification bounds.

A simple regression of `Y` on observed `Delta T_acc` is not sufficient by itself.

### D5.3 — Multicausal pathway audit

The candidate must enumerate material determinants/pathways other than `Delta T_acc`.

For each material pathway, classify it as:

`blocked by design | separately randomized | measured and adjusted under stated assumptions | included in contribution estimand | bounded | unresolved`.

An unresolved pathway does **not automatically fail D5** if the estimand remains identified/bounded despite it. It does fail D5 when the unresolved pathway prevents interpretation of the estimated `Delta T_acc` contribution.

### D5.4 — Causal-contribution classification

**D5-A — IDENTIFIED CONTRIBUTION**

The causal contribution of `Delta T_acc` is identified under a defensible estimand/design/assumption set, with relevant competing mechanisms addressed sufficiently for the estimand.

**D5-B — BOUNDED / ASSUMPTION-EXPLICIT**

Point identification is unavailable, but the contribution is meaningfully bounded or identified within a clearly defined interventional/principal-stratum framework, with materially constraining assumptions.

**D5-C — DIAGNOSTIC ONLY**

First-stage evidence and mediator/contribution diagnostics exist, but causal interpretation of the contribution is not authorized.

**D5-D — TOTAL EFFECT ONLY**

The candidate identifies only `Z -> Y` (possibly with `Z -> Delta T_acc`) and cannot defensibly isolate or bound the contribution of `Delta T_acc`.

### D5 admission rule

For ordinary C09 candidate admission:

**D5-A or D5-B is required.**

D5-C is informative but does not admit the candidate.

D5-D rejects the candidate.

The mere existence of direct or competing causal pathways is **not** a rejection criterion.

## 10. D6 — Full Provenance and Admission

D6 remains unchanged in principle. Every variable, transformation, linkage and mechanism-identification input actually used by the final contribution estimand must be publicly reproducible and provenance-frozen.

For cross-source candidates, Protocol 004 D6-E-F and D5-F requirements remain applicable.

## 12. Early-stop rules

Reject when:

- required public unit-level data are unavailable;
- stable linkage is impossible;
- no structural `T_acc,0/T_acc,1` can be defined;
- `T_acc` collapses into treatment/realized behaviour/outcome;
- no downstream trajectory exists;
- no contribution estimand can be defined;
- no identification/bounding architecture exists for the contribution;
- the only evidence is total `Z -> Y`;
- unresolved pathways make the proposed contribution estimand uninterpretable;
- required provenance is restricted/embargoed/non-reproducible.

Do **not** reject merely because `Y` has many other causal determinants.

## 13. Interpretation discipline

The following distinctions are mandatory:

`Z -> Y` ≠ `Z -> Delta T_acc -> Y`.

`Z -> Delta T_acc` + `Z -> Y` ≠ causal contribution of `Delta T_acc`.

But also:

`other causes of Y exist` ≠ `Delta T_acc contribution is unidentifiable`.

The scientific target is a contribution estimand, not an exclusive-causation claim.

Mediator/contribution diagnostics such as IV/Wald ratios may be reported, but remain diagnostic unless the chosen D5 estimand and assumptions authorize causal interpretation.

## 14. TGCV applicability implication

This refinement is methodologically relevant to TGCV itself.

Real-world value/outcomes normally arise from interacting mechanisms. A useful transversal theory should therefore be able to represent the effect of a transformation-space change as **one causal contribution within a multicausal system**, rather than requiring it to explain all observed value.

The TGCV chain remains:

`mechanism -> (S_t,C_t) -> (S_{t+1},C_{t+1}) -> Delta T_acc -> Delta Pi -> Delta V`

but the downstream value/trajectory may also depend on other causal mechanisms.

Demonstrating that a `Delta T_acc` contribution can be causally identified in such a multicausal environment would therefore constitute evidence of **TGCV applicability**, not merely evidence for a monocausal C09 claim.

This protocol revision does not by itself establish TGCV or C09. It changes the falsifiable empirical target to a more realistic and explicitly estimable causal-contribution question.

## 15. Historical preservation

Protocols 002, 003 and 004 remain frozen historical artifacts. No prior candidate result is rewritten.

The methodological correction is prospective. Existing candidate evidence may be **re-evaluated without repeating experiments**, using the revised contribution estimand and only the evidence already available in the canonical record.
