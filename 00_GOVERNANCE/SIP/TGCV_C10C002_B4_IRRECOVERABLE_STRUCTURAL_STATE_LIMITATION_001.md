# TGCV C10C-002 — B4 Irrecoverable Structural-State Limitation 001

## Status

`FROZEN — B4 CLOSED AS IRRECOVERABLE LIMITATION; EMPIRICAL CAUSAL EXECUTION NOT AUTHORIZED`

## Scope

This disposition closes the B4 search for a reproducible polygon-level structural state mapping using the admitted OpenICPSR 113705 V1 evidence. It does not reopen the completed bounded causal experiment and does not authorize a new causal execution.

## Evidence basis

The controlled B4 reproduction verified the frozen V1 input hashes, complete 342-polygon linkage, treatment/state separation, endpoint linkage, and exclusion of undocumented saturation variables. The structural reconstruction nevertheless could not establish a deterministic polygon-level state for the six elemental infrastructure dimensions.

The subsequent documentary search examined the admitted replication script for direct polygon-level infrastructure variables, `collapse`/`egen`/mean constructions keyed to `N_POLIGONO`, and explicit `b_Disp_*`/`d_Disp_*` rules. The reviewed script treats the `Disp_*` variables as household-level analytical variables; the explicit `collapse` located concerns `constr_*` variables, while `egen mean()` constructions concern other interaction-analysis quantities rather than a six-dimensional binary polygon state.

No admitted V1 source establishes a deterministic rule of the form:

`household observations → unique polygon-round structural state`

for the six dimensions required by the frozen TGCV causal design.

## Prohibited repair

No aggregation rule will be inferred merely from the data to force reproducibility. In particular, no unanimity, majority, weighted-mean threshold, presence, median, treatment-informed, saturation-informed, or outcome-informed rule is admissible absent independent documentary provenance.

The previously used B4 unanimity rule is withdrawn and must not be reinstated.

## Methodological consequence

The limitation is not that the V1 data are unusable for their original household-level analyses. Rather, the admitted evidence does not support the additional TGCV operationalization required to construct a reproducible polygon-level `T_acc,0`, `T_acc,1`, and `ΔT_acc` linked to the independent polygon-level value endpoint.

Accordingly, B4 cannot be closed as a successful reproduction of the frozen polygon-level TGCV causal pathway. It is closed only as an **irrecoverable evidence/operationalization limitation under the admitted V1 boundary**.

## Decision

**B4: CLOSED — IRRECOVERABLE LIMITATION FROM ADMITTED V1 EVIDENCE.**

**C10C-002 remains: `PROMISING / EVIDENCE GAP REMAINS`.**

**Empirical causal execution: NOT AUTHORIZED.**

B5 final analysis-script/specification freezing is not pursued for this candidate because the material B4 structural prerequisite is absent. Any future attempt would require a separately authorized source of polygon-level structural state or a newly admitted dataset/documentation boundary; it must not silently reuse the current V1 package.

## Non-reopening rule

This disposition does not reopen:

- the completed C10C-002 bounded causal experiment;
- T17;
- prior C10-C candidate discovery decisions;
- the frozen causal design;
- the V1 endpoint provenance limitation.
