## 1. Purpose

Resolve concrete, provenance-preserving contact routes for the MT5-VSL-24 batch without inventing individual recipients or treating a generic institutional route as evidence of partner interest.

## 2. Endpoint resolution principles

An endpoint is admissible only when:

1. it is published by the target organization or programme;
2. it is intended for the relevant research/collaboration/enquiry purpose;
3. its provenance can be recorded;
4. the outreach recipient role is clear enough to route the frozen MT5-VSL-22 message appropriately.

A public contact route is **not** equivalent to a named willing recipient and does not qualify a context.
