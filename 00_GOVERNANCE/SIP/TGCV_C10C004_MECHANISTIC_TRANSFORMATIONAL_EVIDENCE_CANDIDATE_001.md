# TGCV C10C-004 — Mechanistic / Transformational Evidence Candidate 001

**Source:** openICPSR Project 116333, V1

**Status:** **CANDIDATE — HIGH PRIORITY MECHANISTIC / TRANSFORMATIONAL EVIDENCE**

**Core/RMA:** Unchanged

## 1. Basis for registration

The completed label-scan reconstruction identifies an explicit longitudinal architecture linking intervention, credit access/use, household economic state, productive activity and women's agency/autonomy variables.

The strongest mechanistic candidate is the sequence:

`treatment → credit access/use → activity/resource transformation → capability/agency transformation → subsequent activity/trajectory`

The source contains explicit variables for Al Amana and other MFI loans, including loan type, disbursement/final-installment dates, amounts, number of loans, repayment frequency and duration. It also contains `client` (loan from Al Amana) and administrative variables describing timing and intensity of credit use.

The female-agency block `j1_*`–`j9d_*` records independent activity, decision authority over materials and inputs, purchasing capacity, production decisions, ability to trade and related activity/skills variables. `j10`–`j13` cover independent mobility, while `j14`–`j21` cover productive work participation and intensity. `j22`–`j2317` connect women's own credit exposure to the broader credit structure.

This combination is materially stronger for TGCV's mechanistic question than a conventional outcome-only result because it potentially permits reconstruction of intermediate state/capability changes through which an intervention can modify subsequent transformation conditions.

## 2. TGCV interpretation

Candidate chain:

`(S_t,C_t) → intervention/access mechanism → credit-use state → economic/productive/capability transformation → modified conditions for subsequent transformations → later trajectory/outcome.`

The candidate is relevant primarily to the TGCV distinction between state `S`, candidate transformations, accessibility/admissibility conditions, realized transformations, and downstream trajectory/outcomes.

It is **not** yet evidence that `ΔT_acc` occurred, because the current reconstruction has not defined and validated an independent admissibility predicate `Pτ(S,C,L)` for this dataset.

## 3. Established status

- Dataset and longitudinal linkage are established in the C10C-004 reconstruction.
- Treatment assignment is identified structurally at the matched-village / half-pair level.
- Treatment, credit access/use and downstream state/capability variables are semantically distinguishable.
- The label structure supplies explicit temporal and mechanistic candidates rather than only endpoint outcomes.
- Candidate priority: **HIGH**.

## 4. Not established

This record does not establish a validated `Pτ(S,C,L)`, `T_acc,0`, `T_acc,1`, `ΔT_acc`, causal mediation, causal downstream trajectory effect, causal value pathway, or any modification of the TGCV Core.
