## 3. Frozen response classification

Responses are classified before any evidence-bearing interpretation:

- **R0 — No response:** no substantive response observed in the monitoring window.
- **R1 — Contact acknowledgement:** receipt/acknowledgement only; no concrete research context.
- **R2 — Generic referral/information:** referral or generic information without a qualifying prospective context.
- **R3 — Potential institutional route:** institution/network/project route identified, but no concrete study/deployment context yet qualified.
- **R4 — Candidate context described:** a concrete context is described, but required evidence-bearing conditions remain incomplete or unverified.
- **R5 — Potential evidence-bearing context:** a concrete prospective study/deployment context appears potentially compatible with the frozen VSL conditions and is eligible for formal C1–C12 qualification audit.

Classification does not itself qualify a context scientifically.

## 4. Qualification rule for R5

Only an R5 response may trigger a formal candidate-context audit. The audit must independently assess the frozen VSL conditions, including as applicable:

1. identifiable individual participants and defined recruitment frame;
2. observable system/service transformation;
3. measurable pre/post transition;
4. independently specifiable accessibility consequences;
5. prospective individual-level financial-well-being measurement;
6. frozen external instrument;
7. item-level observations and scoring metadata;
8. independent reproducibility of measurement/scoring;
9. appropriate ethics/privacy/data-governance framework;
10. prospective study/deployment feasibility;
11. separation of accessibility reconstruction from outcome/value construction;
12. absence of retrospective substitution of conventional outcomes for TGCV Value.

An R5 candidate is therefore a **qualification target**, not evidence and not an authorization for execution.
