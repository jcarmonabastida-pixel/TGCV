**Date:** 2026-09-17

## 1. Purpose

Construct a bounded list of concrete organizations/programmes/study ecosystems to which the MT5-VSL-20 seven-question screening request can be directed.

This list is an outreach target list, **not** a qualification result. No target is represented as a TGCV partner, study context or validated deployment.

## 2. Selection rule

A target is included only where public evidence indicates at least one of the following:

- prospective research involving individual financial-wellbeing measurement;
- financial-product/service innovation with research collaboration mechanisms;
- a concrete financial-inclusion/financial-literacy programme ecosystem capable in principle of supporting prospective evaluation;
- a research group with demonstrated work on financial inclusion, vulnerability or financial wellbeing;
- an institutional/funding route capable of hosting a new prospective study.

Final admission still requires C1–C12 evidence from the target itself.

## 5. Outreach order

The list is ordered by **contextual relevance for screening**, not by scientific quality, likelihood of success, or expected research outcome.

Initial outreach should test whether a concrete context exists; it should not assume that one does.

## 6. Required first-contact evidence

For every target, use the MT5-VSL-20 Q1–Q7 request first.

A target remains `OPPORTUNITY ONLY` until it can identify:

1. named prospective study;
2. individual participant population;
3. concrete transformation;
4. baseline/post measurement feasibility;
5. feasible external Value instrument;
6. item-level reproducibility;
7. ethics/privacy/data-access pathway and responsible study lead.

## 7. No target promoted

No target in this list is currently `CONDITIONAL CONTEXT` or `QUALIFIED CONTEXT`.

Public evidence establishes relevance or opportunity mechanisms only. It does not substitute for candidate-specific evidence under C1–C12.

## 8. Governance boundary

This artifact authorizes only construction of an outreach target list.

It does not authorize:
- contacting participants;
- collecting data;
- recruiting a study population;
- changing the VSL;
- changing `T_acc` operationalization;
- intervention execution;
- causal estimation;
- Core/RMA/Evidence→Claim Matrix/STATUS/C09/M9 modification.
