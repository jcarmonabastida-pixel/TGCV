# TGCV — MT5-VSL-20 Context Acquisition Outreach Specification 001

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Status:** `FROZEN OUTREACH SPECIFICATION — NO OUTREACH SENT`

## 1. Purpose

Convert MT5-VSL-18 C1–C12 into a minimal, partner-facing request that can be used to identify a concrete prospective deployment context without requesting participant data or prematurely proposing an experiment.

## 2. Scientific proposition to communicate

TGCV is seeking a prospective applied-research context in which two analytically separate objects can be observed:

1. an independently specified system/accessibility transformation; and
2. a pre-specified individual financial-well-being measurement.

The intended analytical separation is:

`system transformation → ΔT_acc → subsequent trajectory → frozen external VSL → individual V*`

The VSL must be frozen before observing treatment outcomes and must not define the accessibility construct.

## 3. Minimal first-contact information request

A prospective partner only needs to answer the following seven screening questions initially:

**Q1 — Concrete context:** Do you have a named prospective programme, deployment or applied research study in which a system/service transformation will occur or be observed?

**Q2 — Participants:** Will identifiable individual participants be recruited or enrolled, with a prospective recruitment frame?

**Q3 — Transformation:** Can the initial and resulting system/service states be specified independently of financial-well-being outcomes?

**Q4 — Repeated measurement:** Can the same individuals be measured before and after the relevant transition, within a pre-specified time window?

**Q5 — Financial well-being:** Would administration of the CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale, or another independently justified external financial-well-being instrument, be feasible before and after the transition?

**Q6 — Reproducibility:** Could item-level responses and required scoring metadata be retained in a form that permits an independent reproduction of the resulting Value measurement, subject to ethics/privacy constraints?

**Q7 — Governance:** Is there an identifiable ethics/privacy/data-access pathway and a responsible study lead with whom a detailed protocol could be discussed?

No participant-level data, confidential business information or treatment results should be requested at first contact.

## 4. Qualification after positive first contact

Only after a positive screening response should the following C1–C12 package be requested:

- named study and responsible organization/investigator;
- site/geography and target population;
- sampling/recruitment frame;
- individual respondent unit and identifier strategy;
- target language and administration mode;
- exact VSL/instrument version;
- translation/adaptation status;
- system/intervention description and state transition;
- independent `T_acc,0`, `T_acc,1`, `ΔT_acc` operationalization;
- baseline/post timing and pairing rule;
- item-level data structure and metadata;
- scoring implementation and missing-data rule;
- ethics/consent/privacy/legal basis;
- data stewardship/access arrangement;
- independent reproduction arrangement;
- ability to freeze VSL and analysis rules before outcome observation.

## 5. Evidence standard for advancement

A candidate may advance only on evidence attributable to the candidate context itself.

Accepted evidence can include:
- public study protocol;
- official programme documentation;
- registered study description;
- formal partner statement;
- doctoral/research project description;
- ethics or data-governance documentation where appropriate;
- written confirmation from the responsible study lead.

Generic relevance, methodological precedent, funding availability or institutional interest is insufficient.

## 6. Admission states

### OPPORTUNITY ONLY

The organization, programme or funding route is potentially relevant, but no concrete study has answered Q1–Q7 sufficiently.

### CONDITIONAL CONTEXT

A concrete study exists and Q1–Q7 are materially positive, but one or more C1–C12 conditions require verification.

### QUALIFIED CONTEXT

C1–C12 are sufficiently evidenced and a frozen material package can be constructed.

### INCOMPATIBLE

The proposed context requires retrospective endpoint selection, proxy reconstruction, household/individual substitution, outcome-dependent VSL modification, or lacks independently reconstructible `ΔT_acc`.

## 7. No-go rules during outreach

Outreach must not:

- imply that the partner must validate TGCV;
- present TGCV Value as an established universal construct;
- ask the partner to select outcomes because they are expected to improve;
- ask for retrospective reconstruction of CFPB scores from unrelated variables;
- use treatment effects to justify the VSL;
- request participant data before a legitimate access pathway exists;
- promise a causal `ΔT_acc → ΔV*` result;
- imply that participation guarantees doctoral funding or scientific conclusions.

## 9. Governance boundary

This specification authorizes only partner/context screening and information acquisition.

It does not authorize:
- recruitment;
- intervention;
- translation;
- pilot execution;
- confirmatory Value measurement;
- causal estimation;
- modification of `T_acc` definitions;
- modification of Core/RMA/Evidence→Claim Matrix/STATUS/C09/M9.
