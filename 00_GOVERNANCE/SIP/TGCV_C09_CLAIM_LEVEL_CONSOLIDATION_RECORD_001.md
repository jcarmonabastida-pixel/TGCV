# TGCV C09 — Claim-Level Consolidation Record 001

**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Status:** **CLOSED — BOUNDED EMPIRICAL CAUSAL SUPPORT**  
**Claim:** Accessibility changes causally affect subsequent trajectories.

## 1. Decision scope

This record consolidates the already completed evidence. It does not rerun SWIM, RUST-DYN-2 or KGFS, does not search for another dataset, and does not upgrade TGCV Core/RMA automatically.

The governing C09 protocol requires a causal contribution of `Delta T_acc` to a subsequent trajectory/outcome to be identified or defensibly bounded. It explicitly rejects the requirement that accessibility be the sole determinant of the downstream outcome.

## 2. Evidence layers

### 2.1 SWIM

SWIM previously closed a bounded empirical accessibility-space result and a bounded trajectory-linkage reconstruction. Those results establish the structural/trajectory analytical relation in a controlled system, but are not treated as causal real-world validation by themselves.

### 2.2 RUST-DYN-2

RUST-DYN-2 previously closed a bounded structural empirical result concerning accessibility-space representation/change. It supplies independent structural evidence for the TGCV accessibility object and its change, but is not treated as a causal universal claim by itself.

### 2.3 KGFS Rural Banking

KGFS supplies the decisive causal layer for this claim-level gate:

`randomized structural branch expansion -> Delta T_acc -> subsequent household/economic trajectory`

The KGFS intervention is itself the randomized structural expansion of financial accessibility. D5-A is closed: the structural accessibility contribution is identified under the D5.2-S intervention architecture. The longitudinal technical audit is now closed at 74/74 DTA files with no missing files, preserving the separation between structural accessibility and downstream realization variables.

The KGFS trajectory bridge is therefore no longer blocked by the previously open technical reproducibility condition.

## 3. Claim-level assessment

The evidence now supports the following bounded empirical proposition:

> In the KGFS experimental setting, a randomized structural expansion of accessibility produced causally identified subsequent changes in household/economic trajectories over the observed post-intervention period.

This is evidence for the C09 relation:

`accessibility change -> subsequent trajectory`

It is stronger than a mere `Z -> Y` observation because the assigned intervention is explicitly represented as a structural accessibility transition and the subsequent trajectory is temporally downstream.

## 4. Scope boundary

The consolidation does **not** establish:

- universal causality across all generative systems;
- that accessibility is the sole cause of subsequent trajectories;
- that every accessibility change produces a trajectory change;
- that the magnitude or functional form of the KGFS effect generalizes across domains;
- that value creation `Delta V` has been causally established;
- that TGCV Core is empirically validated as a universal ontology.

The correct claim level is therefore **bounded empirical causal support**, not universal confirmation.

## 5. C09 gate result

**C09 — Accessibility changes causally affect subsequent trajectories:**

**PASS — BOUNDED EMPIRICAL CAUSAL SUPPORT.**

Evidence basis:

- structural accessibility change represented independently of downstream realization;
- randomized causal implementation of that structural change in KGFS;
- D5-A identified contribution;
- longitudinal baseline/endline ordering;
- reproducible 74/74 public-file technical audit;
- prior SWIM trajectory-linkage evidence;
- prior RUST-DYN-2 structural evidence.

The claim is admitted only with the bounded scope stated above.
