# TGCV — MT5-VSL-15 External Prospective Study / Partnership Opportunity Audit 001

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Status:** `CLOSED — OPPORTUNITY PATHS IDENTIFIED; NO DEPLOYMENT CONTEXT QUALIFIED`

## 1. Purpose

Identify concrete external programmes, research mechanisms or partnership routes that could supply the missing prospective population and transformation context required by MT5-VSL-12/13, without treating the existence of an opportunity as evidence for TGCV or as authorization to execute a pilot.

## 2. Search boundary

The audit looked for current or publicly documented mechanisms involving:
- prospective measurement of financial well-being;
- repeated individual-level CFPB scale administration;
- programme/intervention evaluation;
- research collaboration;
- European doctoral/research funding that could host a prospective study.

The audit deliberately separates **opportunity existence** from **qualified TGCV deployment context**.

## 3. Opportunity O1 — CFPB research / collaboration ecosystem

The CFPB documents use of the Financial Well-Being Scale in programme evaluation, including repeated administration to clients and tracking individual scores over time. Its case studies include financial coaching and a funding agency using the scale to evaluate programme progress. citeturn0search5turn0search11

The CFPB also documents Project Catalyst research collaborations with organizations testing financial products or services; its current archived page states that organizations may apply to participate in a research pilot. citeturn0search10

**Assessment:** `OPPORTUNITY — NOT A QUALIFIED TGCV DEPLOYMENT CONTEXT`.

Why: the public material establishes a mechanism for collaboration and prospective/repeated measurement, but does not provide a currently identified TGCV-compatible partner, respondent population, transformation protocol, geography/language or access arrangement.

## 4. Opportunity O2 — CFPB scale as a programme-evaluation component

The CFPB explicitly recommends integrating the scale into programme data collection and administering it consistently over time. It states that the scale complements rather than substitutes for other programme metrics. citeturn0search7turn0search11

**Assessment:** `METHODOLOGICAL COMPATIBILITY — NO DEPLOYMENT CONTEXT`.

This is useful for a future partner specification because it supports a design with individual baseline/post measurements and frozen administration, but it does not identify a field programme for TGCV.

## 5. Opportunity O3 — CFPB public survey infrastructure

The CFPB National Financial Well-Being Survey provides individual scale scores and associated characteristics in a public dataset. citeturn0search6

**Assessment:** `REFERENCE / BENCHMARK INFRASTRUCTURE — NOT PROSPECTIVE DEPLOYMENT`.

It cannot by itself provide the intervention-linked prospective transition required for the intended TGCV Value measurement route.

## 6. Opportunity O4 — Horizon Europe MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

The European Research Executive Agency documents the 2026 MSCA Doctoral Networks call and associated proposal/funding infrastructure. citeturn0search13

**Assessment:** `HOSTING / FUNDING ROUTE — NOT A STUDY CONTEXT`.

A future doctoral project could potentially use such an infrastructure to establish a prospective measurement study, but the call itself does not supply the required population, partner, intervention or instrument deployment context. No claim is made here about eligibility or selection outcome for any particular applicant.

## 7. Opportunity O5 — External empirical precedent for financial-wellbeing research

Recent research continues to use individual-level financial well-being as an empirical construct, including longitudinal associations with later outcomes and cross-country analyses. These studies demonstrate that the construct is actively researched, but they do not automatically provide a TGCV-compatible intervention or respondent frame. citeturn0search9turn0search14

**Assessment:** `SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT — NOT DEPLOYMENT CONTEXT`.

## 9. Key result

The search finds **real opportunity mechanisms**, especially CFPB programme/collaboration routes and European doctoral/research infrastructure, but none supplies all conditions required by MT5-VSL-13.

The principal missing element is no longer the existence of an external Value measurement standard. It is the existence of a **concrete prospective study context** in which:

`system transformation → independently specified ΔT_acc → subsequent trajectory → individual CFPB measurement V*`

can be observed with the VSL frozen before outcome observation.

## 10. Decision

**`MT5-VSL-15 — CLOSED — OPPORTUNITY PATHS IDENTIFIED; NO DEPLOYMENT CONTEXT QUALIFIED.`**

No opportunity is promoted to a TGCV experiment merely because it exists. A candidate becomes eligible only after a concrete study/partner context supplies population, respondent recruitment, language, administration, transformation, measurement timing, item-level data access and governance/ethics conditions.

## 11. Governance consequences

No changes authorized to:
- TGCV Core;
- RMA;
- Evidence→Claim Matrix;
- STATUS;
- C09;
- M9 `ΔT_acc → ΔV`.

No pilot recruitment or confirmatory Value measurement is authorized.
