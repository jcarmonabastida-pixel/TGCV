## Frozen V01–V12 contract

- **V01 Domain/unit:** one explicitly identified building, constructed asset, component or project decision unit. The unit is fixed before execution.
- **V02 Evaluative perspective:** owner/investor/procurer decision perspective, declared before execution.
- **V03 Outcome:** life-cycle cost/cash-flow quantity over the frozen analysis period, including only cost/income/externality categories declared in the protocol.
- **V04 Reference:** a pre-declared alternative or baseline fixed before observing TGCV outcomes.
- **V05 Evaluative objective:** minimize the declared life-cycle cost for the declared decision perspective, subject to the frozen scope and constraints.
- **V06 O → V* mapping:** `V* = -LCC` for the declared decision unit, scope and analysis period; higher V* therefore corresponds to lower declared LCC. This is a domain-bounded valuation convention, not a universal TGCV Value function.
- **V07 Directionality:** lower LCC is favorable under V05; the direction is frozen before execution.
- **V08 Time horizon:** an agreed analysis period, explicitly fixed before execution; it may cover part or all of the asset life cycle.
- **V09 Costs/trade-offs:** acquisition, operation, maintenance, replacement, disposal and any explicitly included income/externality categories. Included categories and constraints are frozen before execution.
- **V10 Aggregation:** primary analysis is at the single decision-unit level. Portfolio aggregation is prohibited unless separately specified and frozen before execution.
- **V11 Missingness/uncertainty:** all missing cost components are recorded as missing rather than silently imputed; uncertainty is represented by pre-specified assumptions/scenarios/sensitivity rules; no rule may be chosen after seeing TGCV outcomes.
- **V12 Identification status:** `VALUE_SPECIFICATION_FROZEN`.

## Independence boundary

The VSL does not define or alter `T_acc`, accessibility, transformation identity, trajectory selection or TGCV Core variables. The valuation layer is externally/domain-defined and is frozen before TGCV outcomes are inspected.

## Freeze statement

This artifact freezes the Value-identification rules for this candidate. It does not constitute experimental evidence, causal evidence, predictive validity, or a claim that TGCV accessibility changes create Value.
