# TGCV — VSL-KGFS-02 Closure Record 001

**Status:** CLOSED — COMPATIBILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Candidate:** CD-05 / C09 KGFS Rural Banking  
**Prospective context audited:** Tamil Nadu KGFS — Thanjavur / Thiruvarur

## 1. Closure decision

The prospective VSL-KGFS-02 route is formally closed as:

`COMPATIBILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED`

The closure is candidate-level. It does not close VSL-SPEC-01, VSL-EXP-01, the VSL programme, or the broader TGCV research programme.

## 2. Basis for closure

The controlled evidence-acquisition sequence established the following:

- the external CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale is an identified external Value construct and source standard;
- Indian research use of the instrument is supported;
- a concrete Tamil Nadu KGFS prospective context was identified independently of outcomes;
- individual respondent administration is compatible at the source-construct level;
- the remaining bridge required for prospective instantiation was not established:
  `CFPB source -> validated Tamil adaptation -> admissible administration -> KGFS respondent`;
- no independently validated Tamil CFPB adaptation was identified in the targeted evidence search;
- administration compatibility for the proposed KGFS implementation was not frozen;
- KGFS-specific validity/equivalence was not established.

The evidence trail is preserved in the preceding VSL-KGFS-02 audit records.

## 3. Stopping rule

The programme does not authorize an open-ended search for a Tamil CFPB version.

A future attempt to develop and validate a Tamil adaptation would constitute a **new methodological/psychometric work package**. It would require independent specification, validation, administration rules and a new freeze decision before any resulting instrument could be used as a VSL.

Such a future work package:

- is not part of the present VSL-KGFS-02 candidate;
- cannot be introduced retrospectively into the historical C09 route;
- does not alter the present closure state.
