## Routing decision

The C10-C programme therefore advances to the next high-priority candidate already identified in Candidate Discovery Round 001:

**C10C-001 — Exporting and Firm Performance: Randomized Experiment, Egypt**

Atkin, Khandelwal & Osman (QJE 2017), with the documented randomized opportunity-to-export intervention, repeated firm observations, and independent economic value endpoints.

## Required next operation

Perform a **candidate-specific documentary admission audit** for C10C-001 using only publicly documented study/replication metadata and scripts initially. The audit must establish, before any empirical execution:

1. exact treatment/intervention variables and experimental unit;
2. pre/post firm structural state variables relevant to export-related transformations;
3. a bounded `U_τ*` and structural predicates `P_τ(S,C,L)` that do not equate treatment assignment with accessibility;
4. reproducible `T_acc,0`, `T_acc,1`, and `ΔT_acc`, or a formally documented limitation;
5. independent value endpoint, unit, timing and missingness/provenance;
6. separation of accessibility from export take-up, learning, quality, productivity, prices and other downstream mechanisms;
7. counterfactual and identification integrity;
8. interference, selection and take-up issues;
9. exact public-data provenance and independent reproducibility route;
10. explicit admission decision and finite readiness blockers.
