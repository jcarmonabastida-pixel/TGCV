# TGCV — VSL-KGFS-02 Tamil External Evidence Search Audit 001

**Status:** CLOSED — NO VALIDATED TAMIL CFPB ADAPTATION ESTABLISHED
**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Candidate context:** Tamil Nadu KGFS — Thanjavur / Thiruvarur

## 1. Purpose

Resolve the remaining question from the prior translation/administration audit: whether an independently validated Tamil-language adaptation of the CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale can be identified.

## 2. Authoritative CFPB evidence

The current CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale page provides the standard and abbreviated questionnaires and scoring materials in English and Spanish. The official scoring guidance states that question wording and response options must not be changed for the scale to work properly and that scoring depends on respondent responses, age group and administration mode.

**Result: OFFICIAL SOURCE INSTRUMENT = PASS.**

**Result: OFFICIAL TAMIL VERSION = NOT IDENTIFIED.**

## 3. External India evidence

Searches located Indian research using the CFPB scale, including work measuring perceived financial well-being with the original five-item CFPB scale and an India-wide 2025 study using the CFPB scale as an external benchmark.

These sources establish Indian research use, but they do not establish a validated Tamil-language adaptation for KGFS.

A published Indian clinical study also reports use of the CFPB scale, but the evidence located does not establish a validated Tamil translation/equivalence protocol suitable for the proposed KGFS context.

**Result: INDIA USE = SUPPORTED.**

**Result: TAMIL KGFS EQUIVALENCE = NOT ESTABLISHED.**

## 4. Translation gate

No authoritative or independently validated Tamil CFPB adaptation was identified in the searched evidence.

This does not prove that no such adaptation exists anywhere. It establishes only that the current evidence acquisition did not locate sufficient evidence to close the gate.

Therefore:

- no ad-hoc translation is authorized;
- no analyst-created Tamil version may be labelled the CFPB scale;
- no post-hoc translation choice is permitted;
- no scoring modification is permitted.

## 5. Administration gate

The CFPB scoring documentation explicitly incorporates administration mode. Prior Tamil-language KGFS research is therefore not sufficient to establish that a translated CFPB instrument can be administered under the same scoring conditions.

**Result: ADMINISTRATION COMPATIBILITY = NOT ESTABLISHED.**

## 6. Decision

The targeted search has not produced the missing methodological bridge:

`CFPB source -> validated Tamil adaptation -> admissible administration -> KGFS respondent`

Therefore:

**VSL-KGFS-02 = NOT FROZEN**

**COMPATIBILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED**

The historical C09 Value route remains closed.

## 7. Stopping rule

The programme should not continue an open-ended search for a Tamil CFPB version.

The remaining alternatives are now explicit:

### A. Close the prospective KGFS VSL route

Close VSL-KGFS-02 as `COMPATIBILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

### B. Treat translation/validation as a new methodological study

If the programme deliberately undertakes development and validation of a Tamil adaptation, that is a new methodological work package. It must be specified, independently validated and frozen before the resulting instrument can serve as a VSL.

It cannot be introduced retrospectively into C09.
