# C09 Conversation Record — KGFS D5.2 Structural Accessibility Intervention Extension 001

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Status:** RECORDED — GOVERNANCE CONTINUITY
**Case:** KGFS Rural Banking retrospective re-evaluation

## Decision recorded

The KGFS re-evaluation showed that a randomized intervention can itself be the structural implementation of a TGCV accessibility transition. Therefore C09 D5.2 must explicitly recognize **D5.2-S — Structural Accessibility Intervention**.

The key distinction is:

`Z -> structural transition -> Delta T_acc -> downstream trajectory/Y`

rather than requiring an additional mediation chain through endogenous take-up:

`Z -> take-up -> Y`.

## Result

KGFS currently has:

- D5.2-S: PASS provisional;
- D5.3: bounded / assumption-explicit;
- overall D5 classification: D5-B pending any future stronger exclusion/bounding evidence.

The candidate is not promoted to D5-A, and C09 itself is not upgraded.

## Boundary preserved

`T_acc` must remain structural and independently defined from outcomes and endogenous adoption. Product take-up, investment, employment, income and wellbeing remain downstream realizations/trajectories.

All material intervention components must either be included in the structural representation or explicitly bounded/assumption-labelled. An unresolved residual direct pathway prevents automatic D5-A classification.
