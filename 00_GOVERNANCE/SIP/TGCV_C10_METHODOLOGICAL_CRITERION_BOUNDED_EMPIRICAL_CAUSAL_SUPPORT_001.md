# TGCV C10 — Methodological Criterion 001: Bounded Empirical Causal Support

**Status:** FROZEN — PROGRAMME METHODOLOGICAL CRITERION FOR C10
**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Scope:** C10 candidate discovery, operationalization and empirical progression
**Relationship to C10-C:** This criterion supplements the frozen C10-C design; it does not reopen or alter the C10-C gate.

## 1. Purpose

The C09 work established a methodological lesson with direct relevance for C10: when the transformation universe is bounded intelligently, it becomes materially easier to identify real-world cases that can be operationalized, reconstructed and tested causally.

The programme therefore adopts the following criterion for C10:

> **Prefer the minimum sufficient bounded transformation universe that permits an identifiable, reproducible and falsifiable empirical TGCV test, rather than attempting to operationalize the maximum theoretically conceivable universe at the outset.**

This is an operational criterion, not a limitation on the eventual scope of TGCV. Bounded empirical evidence may support later cross-domain comparison and broader generalization.

## 2. Methodological progression

The preferred empirical progression is:

`bounded universe → operationalizable real case → reconstruction → causal evidence → BOUNDED EMPIRICAL CAUSAL SUPPORT → cross-domain comparison → broader generalization`

The key distinction is between:

- **bounded empirical support:** evidence obtained within an explicitly delimited, observable and falsifiable transformation universe;
- **general empirical support:** evidence that survives systematic comparison across sufficiently heterogeneous domains and bounded universes.

A bounded result must not be reported as general empirical validation of TGCV.

## 3. Minimum sufficient universe criterion

For a candidate case, define a bounded universe `U_τ* ⊂ U_τ` when the selected transformation families are:

1. directly observable or deterministically reconstructible from admitted source data;
2. defined independently of the value endpoint `V`;
3. small enough to permit explicit reconstruction of each `P_τ`;
4. rich enough to produce a non-trivial and measurable `ΔT_acc`;
5. causally connected, through the study design, to the intervention or structural change under test;
6. reproducible by an independent execution using the same frozen inputs and rules.

The criterion is not to minimize the number of transformations mechanically. It is to minimize unnecessary semantic and operational breadth while preserving the causal test.

## 4. Causal pathway

Within a bounded empirical test, the preferred representation is:

`Z → ΔS → ΔT_acc(U_τ*) → mechanism / trajectory → V`

where:

- `Z` is the causal exposure/assignment;
- `ΔS` is the observed or reconstructible structural state change;
- `T_acc(U_τ*)` is accessibility restricted to the frozen bounded universe;
- `mechanism / trajectory` captures admissible downstream structural pathways without circularly defining accessibility from `V`;
- `V` is the independently defined value endpoint.

The bounded universe must therefore remain upstream of the value endpoint in the construction logic.

## 5. Selection rule for C10 candidates

During C10 candidate discovery and operationalization, preference should be given to cases where a bounded `U_τ*` can be identified before causal estimation and where the available data permit:

- exact source-variable provenance;
- baseline/follow-up structural state reconstruction;
- explicit treatment/state separation;
- deterministic `P_τ` definitions;
- reproducible `T_acc,0`, `T_acc,1` and `ΔT_acc`;
- independent value measurement;
- an explicit assessment of spillover/interference;
- a falsifiable causal comparison.

Candidates should not be rejected merely because their bounded universe is narrower than the eventual conceptual scope of TGCV, provided the bounded test is substantively non-trivial and methodologically defensible.

## 6. Application to C10C-002

For C10C-002, the frozen data-level admission specification already requires `U_τ` to be finite or operationally enumerable and prohibits outcome-informed construction of `P_τ`.

Accordingly, the next controlled operation should first determine whether a **minimum sufficient `U_τ*`** can be recovered from the six documented infrastructure dimensions and their native structural/context data. It should not attempt an unnecessarily broad enumeration of all conceivable transformations enabled by urban infrastructure.

A candidate bounded universe is admissible only if it satisfies the frozen data-level tests, including T1–T10. In particular, narrowing `U_τ` must never be used to hide a missing structural dimension, remove an adverse result, or improve the value association post hoc.

If a defensible `U_τ*` yields a reproducible `ΔT_acc` and the remaining causal gates are satisfied, the resulting evidence may support a **BOUNDED EMPIRICAL CAUSAL SUPPORT** disposition. It may not, by itself, justify a general TGCV validation claim.

## 7. Anti-gaming constraints

The bounded-universe principle must not become a mechanism for cherry-picking. Therefore:

- `U_τ*` selection rules must be frozen before causal estimation;
- transformations may not be selected because they strengthen the observed value result;
- excluded transformations and the reason for exclusion must be recorded when material;
- the bounded universe must remain structurally grounded;
- value, rents, treatment effects and downstream outcomes must not enter `P_τ`;
- a narrow universe that produces no meaningful structural change is not a successful test merely because it is easy to operationalize.

## 8. Interpretation hierarchy

The following evidential hierarchy applies:

**Level 1 — Operationalization:** the bounded universe and structural accessibility can be reconstructed.

**Level 2 — Bounded empirical causal support:** the reconstruction participates in a valid causal test within the explicitly bounded universe and the evidence supports the proposed TGCV pathway.

**Level 3 — Cross-domain support:** comparable bounded tests succeed across heterogeneous domains under independently specified universes.

**Level 4 — Generalization:** evidence supports claims extending beyond the bounded cases and domains actually tested.

Progression to a higher level requires new evidence. Success at a lower level does not automatically imply the next level.

## 9. Governance effect

This criterion is now part of the methodological interpretation of C10 candidate discovery and operationalization.

It does **not**:

- reopen the frozen C10-C causal-design gate;
- modify the frozen C10C-002 documentary audit;
- modify the frozen C10C-002 data-level admission package;
- authorize data acquisition or causal estimation;
- upgrade any candidate's admission status.

It establishes the preferred strategy for the already authorized future decision points: **bound the empirical transformation universe intelligently first; then operationalize and test; generalize only after bounded evidence accumulates.**

## 10. Traceability

This criterion records the methodological lesson explicitly identified during C09/C10 work: bounded transformation spaces facilitate discovery of real cases that can be operationalized and progressed toward bounded empirical causal support without prejudicing later, broader generalization.

The immediate C10 application is C10C-002, whose frozen admission specification already contains the necessary finite/operational `U_τ` requirement. The present criterion adds the methodological selection principle: seek the minimum sufficient bounded universe rather than maximum initial breadth.
