## 3. Frozen protocol boundary

VSL-EXP-01 is a pre-experimental compatibility/readiness protocol.

It does not execute experiments and does not itself establish Value, causality, experimental validity or predictive validity.

The protocol distinguishes:

- `VALUE_IDENTIFIED_READY`;
- `VALUE_PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED_READY`;
- `VALUE_NOT_IDENTIFIED_BLOCKED`;
- `INSUFFICIENT_DOMAIN_INFORMATION`.

These are methodological readiness dispositions, not evidence claims.

## 4. Frozen candidate-neutrality boundary

VSL-EXP-01 does not rank, score or declare a preferred candidate domain.

Multiple compatible candidates must be reported independently. Later selection among compatible candidates is outside the evidentiary claim of this protocol.

## 5. Frozen evidence boundary

Compatibility determinations must be traceable to candidate-domain evidence and must distinguish:

- observed domain fact;
- reconstructed domain fact;
- methodological inference;
- unresolved issue;
- non-claim.

Experimental results may not be used retrospectively to construct compatibility.
