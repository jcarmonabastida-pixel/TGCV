## 3. Mandatory execution control: RF-01

Before any outcome is inspected, a bounded decision-relevant transformation/option subset must have:

1. a declared inclusion universe;
2. a declared inclusion/exclusion rule;
3. a rationale independent of the target outcome;
4. a version/timestamp establishing pre-outcome freezing;
5. sufficient provenance to audit inclusion decisions.

Failure of any element makes the case **INDETERMINATE** and stops execution.

## 4. Authorization boundary

This preflight does not authorize:

- selection of an industrial case;
- contact with an industrial partner;
- data acquisition or empirical execution;
- comparative IUT measurement;
- Stage B, C or D;
- causal inference;
- financial-value inference;
- changes to TGCV definitions;
- epistemic status upgrades.

It establishes only that a narrowly bounded Stage-A execution authorization may now be prepared.

## 5. Scientific safeguard

The preflight confirms that EXT-UPD-4.8 can test industrial usefulness without resolving the previously exhausted question of complete global `T_acc` reconstruction. This does not weaken the scientific boundary: any bounded local representation must remain explicitly bounded and must not be presented as universal conformance.

## 6. Decision gate after Stage A

If Stage A cannot establish a valid, outcome-independent case specification and frozen comparison boundary, execution stops with **INDETERMINATE**.

If Stage A succeeds, that success authorizes only consideration of the next stage through a separate governance decision; it does not authorize Stage B automatically.

EXT-UPD-4.8 is structurally ready for a separate **Execution Authorization** limited initially to controlled Stage A case specification.
