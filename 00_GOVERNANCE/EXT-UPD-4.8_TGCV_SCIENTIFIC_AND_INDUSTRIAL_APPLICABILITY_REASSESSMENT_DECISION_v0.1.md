## 1. Purpose

EXT-UPD-4.8 opens a controlled reassessment of TGCV along two explicitly separated but strategically coupled tracks:

1. **Scientific sufficiency:** determine what TGCV can legitimately claim on the present evidence and what minimum additional evidence is necessary for a defensible doctoral-level contribution.
2. **Industrial applicability:** determine whether TGCV can provide differentiated, practically valuable decision support in industrial settings, independently of whether universal transversal theory has been established.

The second track is the strategic superior criterion for the programme: doctoral defensibility is necessary, but the ultimate programme question is whether TGCV can have a practically valuable industrial application.

## 3. Strategic question

EXT-UPD-4.8 adopts the following superior programme question:

> **¿Puede TGCV tener una aplicación práctica valorable en la industria?**

This question must be operationalized as a falsifiable utility assessment and must not be answered by assertion.

## 4. Critical industrial distinction

The reassessment must explicitly test whether industrial utility requires exhaustive reconstruction of the complete accessible transformation space `T_acc`.

Working question:

> **¿Necesita realmente una empresa reconstruir exhaustivamente `T_acc` para obtener valor de TGCV?**

The candidate industrial utility proposition is instead that TGCV may be useful if it can reliably expose decision-relevant changes in future transformation possibilities, such as:

- transformations that become newly possible;
- transformations that cease to be possible;
- configuration or architectural changes producing those differences;
- newly opened strategic or operational options;
- dependencies opened or closed;
- opportunities that did not previously exist.

This proposition is a test target, not an established claim.

## 5. TGCV as theory versus TGCV as decision instrument

EXT-UPD-4.8 explicitly distinguishes:

**TGCV as descriptive/explanatory theory**  
versus  
**TGCV as decision instrument.**

A bounded industrial utility result could therefore be scientifically meaningful even if universal theoretical conformance remains unestablished, provided the utility is demonstrably differentiated and the limitations are explicit.

Conversely, a theoretically interesting representation that produces no differentiated decision capacity should not be treated as industrially valuable merely because it is conceptually elegant.

## 6. Reassessment architecture

### Track A — Scientific sufficiency

A1. Establish the current legitimate claim set.

A2. Map each claim to its present epistemic status and evidence boundary.

A3. Identify the minimum additional tests required for doctoral defensibility.

A4. Distinguish indispensable scientific gates from routes whose marginal evidentiary value is no longer proportionate.

A5. Preserve all H/F/O/E boundaries and prohibit silent status upgrades.

### Track B — Industrial utility

B1. Define a concrete industrial decision problem in which future transformation possibilities matter.

B2. Define the unit of industrial analysis and relevant state/context representation.

B3. Define the TGCV-specific output expected by the decision process.

B4. Identify a credible incumbent/baseline analytical approach against which TGCV can be compared.

B5. Define measurable decision-relevant benefits.

B6. Define explicit failure/falsification criteria.

B7. Determine whether exhaustive `T_acc` reconstruction is required or whether relevant/local transformation-space change detection is sufficient.

B8. Determine whether a bounded controlled case can provide meaningful evidence without claiming causality or financial value prematurely.

B9. Only if warranted, design a subsequent industry-facing utility test.

## 7. Candidate Industrial Utility Test (IUT)

EXT-UPD-4.8 authorizes design work toward a **TGCV Industrial Utility Test (IUT)**, but does not yet authorize execution.

Candidate structure:

**Decision context → baseline process → TGCV representation → TGCV-specific output → decision comparison → measurable effect → utility interpretation.**

Potential measurable outcomes include:

- newly available options detected;
- lost options detected;
- missed-option rate;
- decision-relevant lead time;
- explanation quality;
- structural completeness;
- identification of configuration/dependency changes relevant to the decision.

These measures must not automatically be interpreted as financial value. Financial or organizational value requires separate evidence.

## 8. Strategic assessment categories

The reassessment will classify the resulting position using the following non-exclusive strategic categories:

1. **Universal + operationalizable:** high scientific and high industrial potential.
2. **Non-universal + operationalizable in concrete domains:** potentially strong scientific contribution and potentially high industrial utility.
3. **Transversal but difficult to operationalize:** high theoretical potential, industrial utility unproven.
4. **No differentiated decision capacity:** weak scientific/industrial case.

These categories are assessment outputs, not predetermined conclusions.
