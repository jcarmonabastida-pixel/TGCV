# TGCV — Conversation Record

**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Topic:** Strategic disposition of the EXT-UPD-4.8 industrial-applicability result and preparation of EXT-UPD-4.9

## Context

EXT-UPD-4.8 is closed at the Stage-B corrective boundary. The original IUT-2 classification was not accepted because O3 accessibility was not independently grounded. The single authorized corrective accessibility reassessment was repaired and re-executed; it is procedurally PASS but scientifically INDETERMINATE. Industrial utility therefore remains unproven.

The conversation explicitly rejected using further O3 rescue attempts to force a positive industrial result or to modify the TGCV Core/falsification criteria retrospectively.

## Strategic options considered

### Option A — Continue trying to demonstrate O3 under the existing route

Rejected as the default strategic path. Further attempts would risk converting an evidential boundary into a theory-rescue exercise and would not be justified by the exhausted current authorization.

### Option B — Separate the scientific Core from an industrial/applied track

Selected as the preferred strategy. The scientific Core remains unchanged. Industrial applicability is treated as an independent programme/application question requiring its own evidence, criteria and governance rather than as validation of the Core.

### Option C — Reorient TGCV into an applied theory of value

Recognized as a potentially ambitious future programme hypothesis, but not adopted as a present Core change. Value remains downstream and unproven.

## Agreed strategic disposition

EXT-UPD-4.8 will not be reinterpreted as evidence of industrial utility. No further O3 accessibility execution will be performed under the current authorization. A separate **INDUSTRIAL-TRACK** may be opened as a PROPOSED strategic line, subject to explicit governance and fresh evidence.

Any future industrial execution must, at minimum, establish:

1. an identifiable industrial case/domain;
2. observable variables and a bounded unit of analysis;
3. an independently closed accessibility definition;
4. an independently specified utility criterion;
5. a design that does not retrospectively modify the TGCV Core or its falsification criteria.

The industrial track is therefore a programme-level hypothesis/route, not a scientific claim, validation result or proof of value.

## Governance consequence

The next controlled governance action is **EXT-UPD-4.9 — Strategic Disposition of Industrial Applicability Result**, to formalize this disposition and define the authorization boundary for any future industrial/applied work.
