## 4. Industrial utility interpretation

The Stage-A PASS establishes **case-testability**, not industrial utility.

It provides the minimum structural condition for asking the IUT question in a concrete industrial episode: whether TGCV can reveal a decision-relevant option-space distinction that the incumbent baseline does not equivalently expose.

No evidence yet supports IUT-1 through IUT-4. The industrial utility state therefore remains **UNPROVEN / OPEN**.

## 5. Scientific interpretation

Stage A does not upgrade claims concerning:

- value creation;

The evidence instead strengthens the narrower proposition that TGCV can be framed as a candidate decision-analysis instrument in at least one bounded industrial setting.
