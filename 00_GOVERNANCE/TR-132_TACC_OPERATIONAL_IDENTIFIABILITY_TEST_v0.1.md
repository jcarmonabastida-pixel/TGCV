# TR-132 — T_acc Operational Identifiability Test v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Status:** CURRENT / OPERATIVE — DESIGN-ONLY  
**Execution:** NOT AUTHORIZED  
**Predecessor:** TR-131 / D-OPS-24 boundary

## 1. Purpose

Determine whether TGCV's analytical object `T_acc` is empirically identifiable under a non-circular operational procedure, and whether full-space identification is actually required for the empirical propositions currently retained by TGCV.

TR-132 is a methodological test. It does not itself modify the Core, the definition `T_acc = F(S,C,L)`, any falsification criterion, or any C01–C16 claim.

## 2. Trigger

Repeated scientific and industrial operations have encountered the same operational bottleneck: realized transformations can be observed, and normative/admissibility information can sometimes be documented, but the complete decision-time accessible transformation set cannot be independently closed. D-OPS-24 bounded this observation across three documentary domains without establishing industrial accessibility impossibility in principle.

The present question is therefore identifiability of the analytical object, not discovery of another dataset or rescue of an industrial result.

## 3. Test question

Given observable or independently reconstructable `S_t`, `C_t` and `L_t`, can a non-circular procedure identify:

1. the complete `T_acc,t`; or
2. if not, a bounded observable subset `T_acc,t^+` sufficient to demonstrate a valid change `ΔT_acc^+`?

## 4. Construct separation

TR-132 distinguishes four levels that must not be conflated:

- `T_poss(S,C,L)`: technically/physically possible transformations.
- `T_adm(S,C,L)`: transformations permitted/admissible under explicitly specified rules and constraints.
- `T_acc(S,C,L)`: transformations effectively accessible under the conditions relevant to the decision point.
- `T_obs`: transformations actually observed as realized.

The following implications are invalid unless separately demonstrated:

`T_obs = T_acc`  
`T_obs = T_adm`  
`τ ∉ T_obs ⇒ τ ∉ T_acc`  
`τ ∈ T_obs ⇒ all accessibility conditions for τ are independently closed`.

## 5. Full-space identifiability criterion

Full `T_acc` is identifiable only if, for a bounded decision point, the procedure can independently establish:

- the system/context boundary;
- the candidate transformation universe relevant to the decision;
- ex-ante admissibility/accessibility conditions;
- material, setup, temporal and authorization conditions where relevant;
- the state/context required to evaluate those conditions;
- a rule distinguishing unobserved from inaccessible transformations;
- an evidence path that does not depend on the observed outcome or on the TGCV result being tested.

If any material condition is supplied retrospectively, inferred solely from realization, or defined using the desired `T_acc` result, full-space identification fails or is indeterminate.

## 6. Bounded identifiability criterion

Full-space identification is **not presumed necessary** for every empirical test.

A bounded operationalization may qualify as `T_acc^+` if there exists a declared subset of candidate transformations for which accessibility can be independently demonstrated under frozen criteria.

A bounded change `ΔT_acc^+` is admissible only when:

`T_acc,t^+ ≠ T_acc,t+1^+`

is established by independently observable evidence and the difference cannot be generated by changing the candidate universe, admissibility rule, evidence threshold or measurement procedure after observing the result.

`T_acc^+` is therefore a lower/partial empirical representation unless exhaustiveness is separately demonstrated. It must never be reported as the complete `T_acc` by default.

## 7. Non-circularity controls

A valid TR-132 operationalization must satisfy all of the following:

1. Candidate transformations are specified before outcome assessment.
2. Accessibility/admissibility criteria are frozen before evaluating the comparison.
3. The evidence used to establish accessibility is independent of the target outcome or utility result.
4. Absence of realization is never treated as absence of accessibility without independent support.
5. The candidate universe is not narrowed after observing favorable results.
6. Thresholds are not relaxed after an indeterminate or negative result.
7. The operational proxy is not defined as a function of the TGCV conclusion it is supposed to test.

## 8. Relationship to TR-131

TR-131 addresses the ontological question whether `T_acc` is irreducible relative to `S`; it resulted in the current bounded Core position that `T_acc` is not an ontological primitive independent of `S`.

TR-132 addresses a different question: whether the analytical object retained by the architecture can be operationally identified from observable conditions.

A failure of operational identifiability must not be interpreted automatically as a failure of ontological necessity, and a bounded operationalization must not be interpreted automatically as proof of full-space identifiability.

## 9. Decision classes

- **PASS:** full `T_acc` is operationally identifiable under the stated conditions.
- **BOUNDED PASS:** full `T_acc` is not established as identifiable, but `T_acc^+` and/or `ΔT_acc^+` are identifiable under explicit, independently frozen conditions sufficient for a bounded empirical proposition.
- **FAIL:** no non-circular operationalization of the required object can be constructed from observable/independently reconstructable information.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** the test design cannot discriminate among the alternatives without introducing an unapproved information source or relaxing the frozen criteria.

A BOUNDED PASS does not upgrade C01–C16 automatically and does not authorize an empirical experiment by itself.

## 10. Required analysis before execution

The execution design must map each existing empirical claim to the minimum level of `T_acc` identification it actually requires. In particular, it must test whether C02, C07, C11 and C16 require complete `T_acc` or can be supported by a bounded operational representation without changing their epistemic wording.

The design must also specify whether `T_acc^+` is a lower bound, a certified subset, or another formally bounded object, and what claims are prohibited when only that representation is available.

## 11. Falsification / adverse outcomes

TR-132 must not be rescued by:

- changing `T_acc = F(S,C,L)` after the result;
- redefining accessibility to match available data;
- replacing accessibility with observed realization;
- replacing accessibility with normative admissibility without justification;
- treating a positive bounded result as full-space validation;
- changing existing falsification criteria after execution.

## 12. Authorization boundary

This artifact authorizes design analysis only. It does **not** authorize a new dataset, industrial case, partner evidence, O3 execution, causal analysis, value analysis, Core modification, claim upgrade or empirical execution.

Before execution, a separate governed authorization must establish the final protocol, Evidence→Claim Impact Assessment, execution scope, auditable result template, and current-state governance PASS.
