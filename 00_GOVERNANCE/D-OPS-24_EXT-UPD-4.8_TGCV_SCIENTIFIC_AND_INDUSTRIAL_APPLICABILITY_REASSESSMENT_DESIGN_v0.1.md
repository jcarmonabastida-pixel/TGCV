# D-OPS-24 — EXT-UPD-4.8 TGCV Scientific & Industrial Applicability Reassessment — Design v0.1

**Status:** FROZEN / DESIGN — AUDIT REQUIRED; EXECUTION NOT AUTHORIZED  
**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Parent decision:** `EXT-UPD-4.8_TGCV_SCIENTIFIC_AND_INDUSTRIAL_APPLICABILITY_REASSESSMENT_DECISION_v0.1.md`  
**Predecessor:** EXT-UPD-4.7 I-01 Constructive Operationalization  

## 1. Purpose

Design a controlled reassessment of TGCV's present scientific sufficiency and, separately, its potential practical industrial utility.

The strategic superior question is not only whether TGCV can support a doctoral contribution, but whether it can provide a differentiated analytical capability that is useful for industrial decision-making.

This design does **not** establish industrial value. It defines how that proposition may be tested falsifiably.

## 2. Current boundary conditions

EXT-UPD-4.7 is exhausted and closed. Its final result was INDETERMINATE because complete closure of `T_acc,D` and `ΔT_acc,D` required analyst-supplied completion of candidate membership evaluation, additional model-specific discretization/bounds, or data assembly.

The present evidence position therefore supports a bounded interpretation of a deeper operationalization boundary. It does not establish universal impossibility.

The current Evidence-to-Claim Matrix remains v0.6. In particular:

- C02 remains E0;
- C11 remains H with bounded independent-domain translation evidence strengthened;
- C16 remains H with upstream translation strengthened but downstream `T_acc` closure unestablished;
- causal, predictive, value and superiority claims remain open;
- G2 remains BOUNDED/PARTIAL;
- G7 remains open at the downstream extension boundary.

## 3. Design principle

EXT-UPD-4.8 must prevent a false dichotomy between:

1. exhaustive reconstruction of all `T_acc`; and
2. no industrial utility.

The reassessment must explicitly test whether industrial utility can arise from **decision-relevant/local changes in accessible transformation possibilities**, without requiring exhaustive enumeration of the complete transformation space.

## 4. Two-track architecture

### Track A — Scientific sufficiency

Assess:

- which TGCV claims are currently supportable;
- which remain hypotheses, falsified claims, open questions or originality propositions;
- what minimum additional evidence is genuinely required for a defensible doctoral contribution;
- which future scientific tests are necessary and which would be disproportionate after EXT-UPD-4.7.

Track A must not silently upgrade epistemic status.

### Track B — Industrial utility

Assess whether TGCV can produce a decision-relevant analytical output that is not merely a relabelling of information already available through an incumbent approach.

Track B must determine:

- the decision problem;
- the system/unit of analysis;
- the relevant state/context;
- the candidate transformation universe at the level required by the case;
- the accessibility criterion;
- the decision-relevant change in accessible possibilities;
- the TGCV-specific output;
- the incumbent/baseline representation;
- measurable comparative criteria;
- failure/falsification conditions.

## 5. Industrial Utility Test — IUT

The proposed core test is the **TGCV Industrial Utility Test (IUT)**.

### IUT objective

Determine whether a bounded industrial decision problem can be represented using TGCV in a way that yields a measurable decision-relevant capability beyond a credible incumbent/baseline method.

### IUT null proposition

**H0-IUT:** TGCV does not provide a differentiated decision-relevant capability beyond the incumbent/baseline approach for the bounded problem.

### IUT positive proposition

**H1-IUT:** TGCV provides at least one reproducible, decision-relevant capability that the incumbent/baseline approach does not provide, or provides materially less effectively, under the same bounded information conditions.

This is a capability proposition, not yet a financial-value proposition.

## 6. Candidate TGCV-specific outputs

The test may evaluate one or more of the following, provided they are defined before outcome inspection:

- newly accessible transformations/options;
- transformations/options that cease to be accessible;
- structural causes or configuration changes associated with those differences;
- newly opened or closed dependencies;
- decision-relevant option changes detected before the downstream outcome;
- explanation of why an option became accessible/inaccessible;
- lead time for identifying a relevant option-space change;
- missed-option rate;
- false-option rate;
- structural completeness of the representation;
- reproducibility of the analytical result.

No metric may be treated as “industrial value” merely because it improves.

## 7. Baseline requirement

The incumbent/baseline must be credible for the selected decision problem and must be frozen before comparative evaluation.

Possible baseline classes include, depending on the eventual case:

- current operational decision process;
- state-transition analysis;
- conventional optimization/planning representation;
- existing configuration-management analysis;
- risk/feasibility assessment;
- expert decision process with explicitly documented information inputs.

The baseline must not be chosen after seeing which comparator makes TGCV look strongest.

## 8. Unit of industrial analysis

The preferred unit is a bounded **decision episode** in which:

1. a system is in an identifiable state/context;
2. more than one candidate transformation/option is relevant or potentially relevant;
3. accessibility can be evaluated before the final outcome;
4. a subsequent state or decision-relevant transition occurs;
5. the representation can be compared against a credible incumbent approach.

The first case need not reconstruct the entire global `T_acc`. It may use a bounded decision-relevant transformation subset if the selection rule is frozen independently of the observed outcome and does not manufacture the desired result.

## 9. Industrial utility does not require financial-value proof at first gate

The first IUT gate evaluates **differentiated analytical/decision capability**.

A later value-linkage gate may be required to establish:

`TGCV capability → decision change → operational/economic consequence → value`.

Therefore:

- IUT PASS does not imply financial value;
- IUT PASS does not imply causality;
- IUT PASS does not imply universal validity;
- IUT PASS does not imply superiority in all contexts.

## 10. Falsification / failure criteria

The industrial route must be considered unsuccessful for the tested case if any of the following holds:

1. TGCV produces no differentiated output relative to the baseline.
2. The TGCV output can be reconstructed trivially from the baseline without additional analytical capability.
3. The result depends on analyst-supplied outcome-aware option selection.
4. Accessibility is defined from the observed outcome.
5. The representation requires arbitrary discretization or thresholds not justified by the native case.
6. The alleged benefit disappears under blinded/reproducible evaluation.
7. TGCV adds descriptive terminology but no decision-relevant information.
8. Any apparent advantage depends on information unavailable at the decision time.

## 11. Industrial utility tiers

The eventual result should distinguish:

- **IUT-0 — No differentiated utility:** no demonstrated decision advantage.
- **IUT-1 — Descriptive utility:** improved representation/explanation, but no demonstrated decision advantage.
- **IUT-2 — Decision-support utility:** reproducible improvement in a decision-relevant analytical capability.
- **IUT-3 — Operational utility:** decision-support capability demonstrably integrated into an operational process with measurable consequence.
- **IUT-4 — Value-linked utility:** operational consequence is linked by controlled evidence to economic/organizational value.

These are result classes, not assumptions.

## 12. Controlled-case progression

The design permits a staged progression, subject to subsequent authorization:

### Stage A — Controlled case specification

Freeze a bounded industrial decision problem and demonstrate that the TGCV representation is constructible.

### Stage B — Comparative analytical test

Compare TGCV against the frozen baseline on predefined decision-relevant outputs.

### Stage C — Decision consequence

If Stage B is positive, test whether the TGCV output changes an actual or simulated decision in a reproducible way.

### Stage D — Value linkage

Only if justified by prior results, test operational/economic consequences and their relation to value.

No later stage is authorized automatically by an earlier positive result.

## 13. Scientific sufficiency assessment

Track A must produce a bounded classification of the current scientific position using existing evidence and explicitly identified missing gates.

It should answer:

- What can TGCV defend today?
- What is the minimum remaining evidence required for the central thesis?
- Does the current evidence justify continued pursuit of transversal theory?
- Which claims should remain explicitly bounded?
- What scientific contribution survives even if universal conformance is not achieved?

## 14. Industrial utility decision criterion

The decisive industrial question is:

> **Can TGCV reveal, explain or structure a decision-relevant change in future transformation possibilities in a way that produces differentiated decision capability, without requiring unjustified exhaustive reconstruction of the complete accessible transformation space?**

A negative result on this question should be treated as substantive evidence against TGCV's industrial applicability, not merely as an operational inconvenience.

A positive result would justify a separate value-linkage programme; it would not by itself establish value.

## 15. Evidence and provenance requirements

Every industrial case must preserve:

- source-level provenance;
- pre-decision information boundary;
- frozen case specification;
- frozen candidate-option selection rule;
- frozen baseline;
- frozen metrics;
- blind/outcome-independent construction where applicable;
- reproducible calculation/assessment;
- explicit failure conditions.

No industrial case may be used as post-hoc confirmation of TGCV merely because it can be narratively mapped to the architecture.

## 16. Governance sequence

The controlled sequence is:

1. Design Audit.
2. Preflight.
3. Execution Authorization.
4. Controlled Stage A, if authorized.
5. Evidence→Claim Impact Assessment.
6. Propagation.
7. Consistency Closure.
8. Only then decide whether Stage B is justified.

No dataset search, industrial partner request, empirical execution, Gate D execution, causal analysis or value claim is authorized by this design alone.

## 17. Explicit exclusions

This design does not:

- reopen D-OPS-24 candidate-domain discovery;
- select a third scientific domain;
- reopen I-01 constructive operationalization;
- modify TGCV Core;
- alter TR-130 or TR-131;
- upgrade C11/C16 to full transversal conformance;
- establish causality;
- establish prediction;
- establish financial value;
- establish superiority;
- refresh external assets;
- authorize industrial execution.

## 18. Expected decision output

The next controlled stage must classify the programme into one of the following broad strategic positions, based on evidence rather than preference:

A. **Scientific + industrial route viable.**

B. **Scientific contribution viable; industrial utility remains unproven.**

C. **Industrial utility demonstrable in bounded contexts without universal theory.**

D. **No differentiated industrial utility demonstrated under tested conditions.**

E. **Evidence insufficient; further bounded testing justified.**

These classifications are not mutually exclusive in their scientific/industrial dimensions and must be reported separately where necessary.

## 19. Hard stops

Stop the procedure if closure requires:

- analyst-invented transformation classes;
- arbitrary discretization/bounds/thresholds;
- outcome-defined accessibility;
- post-hoc baseline selection;
- outcome-aware option selection;
- unsupported causal inference;
- unsupported financial-value inference;
- changing frozen TGCV definitions;
- treating improved explanation as proven economic value;
- treating one successful case as universal evidence.

## 20. Design conclusion

EXT-UPD-4.8 is therefore designed as a **strategic applicability gate**, not as another attempt to force universal operational closure of `T_acc`.

The key methodological innovation of this stage is to test whether TGCV's industrial usefulness can reside in the detection and explanation of **decision-relevant changes in accessible transformation possibilities**, rather than in exhaustive reconstruction of the entire transformation space.
