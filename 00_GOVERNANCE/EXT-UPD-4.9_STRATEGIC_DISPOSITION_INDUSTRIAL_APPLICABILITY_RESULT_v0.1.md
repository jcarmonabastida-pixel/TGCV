# EXT-UPD-4.9 — Strategic Disposition of Industrial Applicability Result

**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Status:** GOVERNANCE DECISION — CURRENT  
**Predecessor:** EXT-UPD-4.8  
**Decision type:** Strategic disposition / authorization boundary  

## 1. Decision

EXT-UPD-4.8 is accepted as **closed at its corrective accessibility boundary**. It does **not** establish industrial utility.

TGCV will not modify its scientific Core, accessibility definition, or falsification criteria in order to rescue the rejected IUT-2 result or obtain a positive O3 classification.

Industrial applicability is separated from Core validation and may be pursued, if justified, as an independent **INDUSTRIAL-TRACK** at programme/application level.

The INDUSTRIAL-TRACK is **PROPOSED only** by this decision. It is not evidence, validation, utility proof, superiority evidence, causal evidence, predictive evidence, financial-value evidence, or a Core upgrade.

## 2. Basis

EXT-UPD-4.8 established a controlled industrial case specification and executed a comparative route. IUT-2 was not accepted because O3 accessibility was not independently grounded. The single authorized corrective accessibility reassessment was repaired and re-executed; the repaired execution was procedurally coherent but scientifically INDETERMINATE because MC02 and MC03 remained unresolved and HS-AC01 remained the hard stop.

The resulting methodological interpretation is bounded: native candidate existence does not by itself close decision-time accessibility when material availability/setup conditions are not independently specified. This interpretation is not a universal impossibility claim.

The current canonical state explicitly records industrial utility as **UNPROVEN / OPEN**. Therefore this decision must not retrospectively characterize EXT-UPD-4.8 as an industrial success.

## 3. Strategic options and disposition

### A — Continue O3 rescue under the current route

**Disposition: REJECTED.**

No further O3 accessibility execution is authorized under the current route. The evidential boundary is treated as a result, not as a defect to be overcome by successive procedural rescue.

### B — Separate scientific Core from industrial/applied track

**Disposition: ADOPTED.**

The Core remains unchanged. Industrial applicability becomes an independently governed application/research track with its own evidence and acceptance criteria.

### C — Reorient TGCV into an applied theory of value

**Disposition: NOT ADOPTED AT PRESENT.**

This remains a possible future programme hypothesis. It cannot be promoted to the scientific Core while value linkage and downstream evidence remain open.

## 5. INDUSTRIAL-TRACK — proposed scope

A future industrial track may investigate whether TGCV can provide useful differentiated analytical capability in a real industrial decision context. Such work must be independently designed and governed.

Minimum entry conditions are:

1. **Identifiable case/domain** — a concrete industrial decision context and bounded unit of analysis.
2. **Observable variables** — state/context, candidate transformations, conditions/constraints and outcomes must be operationally observable or defensibly reconstructable.
3. **Independent accessibility closure** — the admissibility/accessibility criterion must be specified independently of the desired TGCV result and closed before comparative outcome analysis.
4. **Independent utility criterion** — utility must be defined ex ante using observable criteria distinct from merely reproducing TGCV's own analytical output.
5. **Non-retroactivity** — no modification of the Core, falsification criteria, thresholds or acceptance rules to accommodate the industrial case after evidence is observed.
6. **Comparative design** — where superiority or differentiated utility is tested, the comparator and evaluation protocol must be frozen before outcome assessment.
7. **Evidence separation** — industrial utility evidence must remain distinguishable from evidence supporting the ontological Core and from causal/value claims.

Satisfaction of these conditions is a prerequisite for a future execution authorization; it is not granted automatically by EXT-UPD-4.9.

## 6. Authorization boundary

Authorized by EXT-UPD-4.9:

- formal creation of the INDUSTRIAL-TRACK as a **PROPOSED** strategic line;
- definition of its minimum entry conditions;
- preparation of governance/design artifacts necessary for a future authorization decision.

Not authorized by EXT-UPD-4.9:

- any new industrial experiment or dataset execution;
- further O3 accessibility execution under EXT-UPD-4.8;
- Stage-C or Stage-D execution;
- engagement with an industrial partner as an evidential execution activity;
- causal inference;
- value optimization;
- Core modification;
- upgrading any existing claim from H/O/F to a stronger epistemic state;
- updating external research/application assets as though industrial utility had been established.

A future industrial execution requires a new explicit governance authorization after its design has been reviewed against these conditions.

## 7. Governance consequence

EXT-UPD-4.9 is a **governance decision, not an experiment**.

Its principal function is to preserve the scientific boundary established by EXT-UPD-4.8 while preventing the industrial question from being abandoned merely because the current O3 route could not close accessibility.

The next controlled work, if authorized, is therefore **design/preparation of the INDUSTRIAL-TRACK**, not another attempt to force O3 closure.
