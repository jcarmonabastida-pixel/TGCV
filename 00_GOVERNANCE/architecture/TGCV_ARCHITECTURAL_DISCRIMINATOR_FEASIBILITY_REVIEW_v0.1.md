## 1. Question

Can the current Ω_T/E3 route produce a genuine architectural discriminator with matched inherited representation A and independently differing structural relation E3?

## 2. Review basis

The review uses the already governed reconstruction/identifiability precedent: a proposed target observable cannot discriminate an architecture when it is derivable from the information frozen by the comparison representation.

The same fail-closed principle applies here.

## 3. Result

Under the current boundary and admissible auxiliary vocabulary, **no principled A₁ ≡ A₂ / E3₁ ≠ E3₂ construction has been demonstrated**.

The unresolved freedom required for E3 would have to be:

1. observable from primitive evidence;
2. absent from A;
3. absent from every admissible auxiliary mechanism of A;
4. not definitional of the outcome-generating transition rule;
5. stable under the frozen identity/equivalence and provenance rules.

No current specification establishes such a degree of freedom.

## 4. Why this is not a negative empirical result

This is not evidence that transformation-space structure does not exist.

It establishes only that the **present Ω_T boundary has not yet been made empirically discriminating** against the inherited A representation.

Adding E3 as another field, relation, or auxiliary mechanism would not solve the problem if that field is recoverable from A or from its admissible mechanisms. Conversely, declaring E3 irreducible by definition would be circular.
