## 1. Purpose

Determine whether the formal Ω-primary representation can be instantiated empirically with a transformation identity and a structural relation while preserving an information boundary that is genuinely distinct from the inherited architecture A.

This audit does not assume that such an instantiation exists.

## 3. Formal target

The formal target is an empirical instantiation of:

`Ω_T,t = (U_t, ≡_t, R_t)`

where:

- `U_t` identifies the observed transformation instances available in the frozen source;
- `≡_t` defines an explicitly frozen transformation identity/equivalence relation;
- `R_t` is a typed structural relation with a separately specified observation or construction boundary.

The audit does not permit `R_t` to acquire independent status merely by renaming a deterministic function of `U_t`.

## 4. Bridge requirements

A candidate bridge must establish all of the following:

### B1 — Transformation identity

A transformation instance has a reproducible identity across the empirical observation window. Identity must not depend on the downstream target.

### B2 — Temporal availability

The identity and relation observations available at time `t` must not use information from after `t`.

### B3 — Relation semantics

`R_t` must have a typed, falsifiable semantic definition. A graph edge without a specified empirical meaning is insufficient.

### B4 — Information boundary

The bridge must state exactly what information is available to A and exactly what is additionally observed for `R_t`.

### B5 — A-reconstruction test

Any purported B-only relation must be tested against the strongest deterministic reconstruction permitted from A. Implementation differences do not count as architectural non-equivalence.

### B6 — Provenance

Every relation observation must be traceable to an empirical source or explicitly defined measurement procedure.

### B7 — Missingness

Unknown, absent, and out-of-scope observations must remain distinct. No absence may be upgraded to complete absence without a completeness certificate.

### B8 — Falsifiability

The bridge must support at least one pre-specified observation under which the proposed distinction could fail.

## 5. Candidate empirical source classes

Potential sources may include:

- longitudinal runtime/event traces from an actual transformation system;
- versioned system-state observations where transformation events have explicit timestamps and identities;
- controlled transformation logs generated by a system under a pre-registered observation protocol;
- other independently timestamped records whose relation semantics are not reconstructed solely from U_t.

Static dependency declarations from the Rust snapshot do not satisfy independent R_t measurement by themselves.

## 6. Exclusion rules

A proposed bridge is not admissible if it:

- derives `R_t` solely from `U_t` and calls the result independent;
- derives `R_t` from `T_acc` when `T_acc` is the disputed architectural object;
- uses future state to define a relation claimed to exist at `t`;
- defines the relation after inspecting the downstream outcome;
- introduces value, utility, reward or outcome into construction of Ω;
- uses an implementation artefact as evidence of conceptual non-equivalence;
- reintroduces a previously closed candidate under different terminology.

## 7. Bridge decision states

**BRIDGE-PASS:** all B1–B8 are satisfied and an independently bounded empirical instantiation exists.

**BRIDGE-UNDERDETERMINED:** the formal object is sufficiently specified, but no admissible empirical source or observation boundary is currently established.

**BRIDGE-FAIL:** the available empirical instantiation collapses to an A-reconstructible representation or violates a bridge requirement.

## 8. Current assessment

The frozen Rust U_t source establishes transformation identities and dependency-derived structural information. It does not, by itself, establish an independent empirical measurement source for a distinct `R_t`.

Therefore the current bridge is **not yet BRIDGE-PASS**.

No scientific execution is authorized by this document.
