# TGCV — Ω_T Railway Source-Specific Representation Decision v0.1

**Status:** CLOSED — BLOCKED FOR PUBLIC-ONLY Ω_T INSTANTIATION
**Date:** 2026-10-01
**Gate:** RAILWAY_OMEGA_T_SOURCE_SPECIFIC_AUDIT

## 1. Decision

The railway rule-layer family is **not admitted** as an empirical Ω_T source under the current public-only reproducibility boundary.

## 2. What is established

- EULYNX supplies an independently governed, versioned engineering/rule layer with explicit signalling/interlocking semantics.
- ADIF/RINF supply governed railway-state resources and version/schema provenance.
- Historical railway resources exist, including versioned ADIF material and broader longitudinal railway reconstructions.

## 3. Decisive blocker

The existing D-OPS-10–14 record does not establish two recoverable, immutable historical ADIF/RINF states for the same infrastructure manager with stable object identity and a common rule-compatible semantic layer.

Therefore stable longitudinal π, exact cross-time state identity, reproducible U_[t,t+1] across adjacent intervals, final R_[t,t+1] mapping at the transformation-identity level, and matched A reconstruction remain unproven.

The blocker is **public longitudinal state recoverability**, not the quality of the EULYNX rule layer.

## 4. Boundary consequence

No synthetic or manually reconstructed historical state may be introduced to convert this into a PASS.

No current RINF state may be treated as a historical state.

No experiment may be designed on the assumption that the missing longitudinal bridge exists.

## 5. Disposition

**Ω_T railway source admission: BLOCKED.**

Railway remains retained as methodological/rule-layer evidence and as a possible future source if an independently reproducible longitudinal archive becomes available.

This decision is not evidence against TSDI and does not revise the Core, Evidence→Claim Matrix or RMA.

## 6. Next controlled operation

Return to **cross-domain discovery**, using the now-frozen two-asset filter:

1. independently governed rule/constraint layer capable of defining transformation identity, equivalence and structural relations ex ante;
2. public, versioned longitudinal state archive with stable identity;
3. common observation boundary for A and Ω_T;
4. no dependence on outcomes, value or future trajectories.

**Scientific execution: NOT AUTHORIZED.**
