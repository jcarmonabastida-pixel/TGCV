## 1. Purpose

Specify a candidate structural observable R* whose admissibility can be tested before any scientific execution. The candidate is deliberately defined as an observation of transformation organisation, not as a renamed derived statistic.

## 2. Candidate

R*_t is the independently observed temporal organisation relation over transformation instances at time t.

Operationally, R*_t is a set of ordered pairs `(τ_i, τ_j)` together with an observation timestamp, where the pair relation means that the occurrence or availability of transformation τ_j is conditionally organised by transformation τ_i according to a measurement rule frozen before outcome observation.

R*_t is therefore not defined merely as:

- adjacency inferred from package identifiers;
- transitive closure of dependency declarations;
- graph topology calculated from T_acc;
- any deterministic function of U_t;
- any relation selected because it predicts a downstream outcome.

## 3. Required independent measurement boundary

A candidate R*_t is admissible only if its relation can be observed from a source or measurement procedure that is not itself generated by the inherited A representation.

The measurement protocol must specify:

1. the observable event or record establishing the relation;
2. the temporal availability of that observation;
3. the identity of the two transformation instances;
4. the rule converting observations into relation membership;
5. exclusions and missing-data handling;
6. the point at which R*_t becomes available without future information.

If these elements cannot be specified independently, the candidate is UNDERDETERMINED.

## 4. Candidate A representation

For the reconstruction test, A receives the frozen information permitted by the inherited architecture, including the relevant state/context representation, accessible transformations and explicitly admissible transition mechanisms.

An A reconstruction is admissible only if it uses information available under that frozen boundary. It may compute arbitrary deterministic descriptors from those inputs; restricting A merely to cardinality or set membership is not permitted.

## 5. A-reconstruction function

Define:

`R_A,t = g_A(A_t)`

where `g_A` is any deterministic reconstruction function operating only on the frozen A information boundary.

The candidate is A-EQUIVALENT if there exists such a deterministic reconstruction that reproduces R*_t exactly for the specified observation domain.

The candidate is A-NON-EQUIVALENT only if independently observed R*_t contains information that no admissible `g_A(A_t)` can reproduce.

## 6. Identifiability test

The preflight must establish, before any downstream prediction analysis:

- exact identity domain of transformations;
- exact temporal indexing;
- exact relation semantics;
- provenance of every observed relation;
- whether relation observations are complete, partial or unknown;
- whether the same R*_t can be reconstructed from A;
- whether the relation source introduces information unavailable to A.

No predictive effect is required at this stage. The sole question is whether R* is independently identifiable and potentially non-reconstructible.

## 7. Decision states

### A-EQUIVALENT

R*_t is exactly reproducible from A under the frozen reconstruction boundary.

Disposition: candidate does not discriminate the architectures.

### A-NON-EQUIVALENT

R*_t is independently observed and cannot be reproduced from A under the frozen boundary.

Disposition: candidate may proceed to a separate predictive discrimination preflight.

### UNDERDETERMINED

Independent measurement, provenance, temporal availability, or the A reconstruction boundary cannot be established.

Disposition: no scientific execution; candidate remains unresolved.

### NON-DISCRIMINATING

R* is independently measurable and non-equivalent, but no pre-specified consequence distinguishes A from B.

Disposition: no architectural transition inference.

## 8. Anti-circularity rules

R* must not be constructed using:

- downstream outcomes;
- value, reward or utility;
- future trajectory information;
- post-hoc selection of relations;
- predictive performance on the target used to define the relation;
- terminology that presupposes TSDI;
- an accessibility predicate that is itself the disputed architectural object.

## 9. Relation to the frozen Rust U_t

The existing U_t primitive may provide transformation identities, temporal information and dependency declarations. Such fields are not automatically an independent R* measurement source.

In particular, a relation obtained deterministically from the frozen U_t dependency declarations is initially treated as A-reconstructible unless an independent observation boundary is demonstrated.

This prevents reintroducing the previously closed E_τ/O_T constructions under a new label.

## 10. Scientific firewall

This specification does not authorize:

- real-data analysis beyond preflight inspection;
- prediction testing;
- causal inference;
- accessibility derivation;
- Reach or trajectory computation;
- outcome/value analysis;
- Transformational Intelligence claims.

## 11. Required next preflight

The next gate is an **R* Identifiability and A-Reconstruction Preflight**.

The preflight must answer, using only frozen provenance and schema information:

1. What independent source, if any, measures R*?
2. Is R* available without future information?
3. Can R* be deterministically reconstructed from A?
4. If not, is the non-equivalence attributable to an independently observed variable rather than an implementation difference?
5. Is the candidate sufficiently specified to permit a falsifiable predictive discrimination test?

Only an A-NON-EQUIVALENT result may justify drafting the next scientific experiment package.
