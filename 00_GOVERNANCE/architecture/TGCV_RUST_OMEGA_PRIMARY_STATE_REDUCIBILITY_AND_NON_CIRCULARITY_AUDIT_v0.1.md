## 1. Audit question

Can the candidate Rust object

`Ω_T,t = (U_t, ≡_T, R_t)`

provide structural information that is not reconstructible from the inherited state/accessibility representation, while preserving an outcome-blind and non-circular construction?

This is the critical discriminator before empirical admission of Ω-primary.

## 2. Comparison classes

The audit distinguishes two representations:

**Inherited representation A:**

`A_t = (S_t, T_acc,t)`

with admissible auxiliary context/mechanism fields only where already governed by the current architecture.

**Ω-primary representation:**

`Ω_t = (U_t, ≡_T, R_t)`

with accessibility subsequently derived:

`T_acc,t = F(Ω_t,S_t,C_t,L_t)`

The test is not whether Ω contains more columns than A. The question is whether its structural content is **irreducible** to the information permitted in A.

## 3. Non-circularity requirements

A valid Ω construction must satisfy all of the following:

1. U_t is constructed from pre-outcome observational records.
2. ≡_T is frozen before outcomes are inspected.
3. R_t is constructed from primitive structural evidence, not from accessibility, execution success, reward, value or downstream outcome.
4. Longitudinal correspondence is frozen independently of outcome.
5. No component is selected because it improves prediction of an outcome.
6. No relation is inferred merely because two transformations are co-accessible, co-executed or jointly successful.

Failure of any condition prevents Ω-primary admission for that construction.

## 4. State-reducibility test

The relevant null hypothesis is:

> **H0:** Every information-bearing aspect of Ω relevant to the proposed architectural observation can be reconstructed from the inherited representation A under a frozen, pre-specified reconstruction rule.

A genuine architectural discriminator would require a matched construction in which:

- A_1 ≡ A_2 under the frozen inherited comparison;
- Ω_1 ≠ Ω_2 under the frozen Ω representation;
- the difference is present in primitive pre-outcome structural observations;
- the difference cannot be reconstructed by an admissible transformation of A.

The converse failure is decisive for the present route: if Ω_1 ≠ Ω_2 is produced only by adding fields that are already derivable from A, the result is an enriched representation, not evidence for a distinct primary object.

## 5. Rust assessment

The present governance record establishes candidate pre-outcome sources for transformation identity and dependency structure, but it does **not** yet provide a frozen matched construction satisfying:

`A_1 = A_2`

while simultaneously demonstrating:

`Ω_1 ≠ Ω_2`

through an irreducible primitive structural difference.

In particular:

- `U_t` is conditionally constructible;
- `≡_T` has a frozen canonicalisation form, but its empirical semantic adequacy is untested;
- `R_t` has a typed schema and candidate dependency evidence, but its irreducibility from A has not been demonstrated;
- `κ` remains conditional;
- no empirical matched pair has been frozen;
- no raw-data audit has established that the proposed Ω difference survives an admissible state-only reconstruction.

Therefore the discriminator is **NOT PASSED**.

## 6. Why this is not a negative result about Ω

The result does not establish that transformation-space structure is absent.

It establishes only that the current Rust route has not yet demonstrated that its proposed Ω representation contains information that cannot be represented by the inherited A boundary.

This distinction is essential:

`NOT DISCRIMINATED` ≠ `DISPROVED`

The current evidence is therefore insufficient to promote Ω-primary from architectural candidate to empirically admitted primary object.

## 7. Circularity finding

No direct outcome circularity is introduced by the current schema if the information firewall is enforced.

However, a second-order circularity remains possible: a relation may be called structural and then treated as evidence for Ω-primary even though that relation was itself reconstructed from the inherited accessibility/state representation.

Accordingly, provenance alone is insufficient. The audit requires a **state-reducibility proof obligation** for each proposed information-bearing Ω component.
