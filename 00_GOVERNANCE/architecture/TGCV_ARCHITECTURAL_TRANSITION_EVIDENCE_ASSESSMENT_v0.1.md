## 1. Evidence currently relevant to the transition

### MT5 — Dynamic Transformation Space reconstruction
**Assessment:** FOR-B / AGAINST-A (bounded).

MT5 demonstrates that a real-world case can preserve an ex-ante transformation universe and longitudinal structural/connectivity change while failing to identify a complete accessibility predicate and `T_acc`. This does not establish TSDI, but it exposes a boundary of the inherited operational chain: observed structural change cannot simply be treated as identified transformation-space change.

### C07 / Rust RUST-DYN-2
**Assessment:** FOR-B (bounded) and NON-DISCRIMINATING with respect to architectural superiority.

The frozen Rust representation provides extensive temporal evidence of persistence, expansion, contraction and reconfiguration of accessible transformation spaces. This is directly relevant to transformation-space dynamics. However, because the representation was itself built around `T_acc`, it does not independently demonstrate that a dynamic-space ontology is superior to the inherited representation.

### TR-131 VisitAll / PRISM V007
**Assessment:** NON-DISCRIMINATING / operationally supportive.

These results demonstrate that transformation-space states and transitions can be instrumented across distinct operational domains. They support the feasibility of representing dynamic transformation structures but do not discriminate architectural superiority.

### NEXT3 / NEXT4
**Assessment:** NON-DISCRIMINATING for the architectural transition.

NEXT3 provides a bounded condition-dependent reorganisation of action→profile correspondence under future-structure reassignment; NEXT4 provides design calibration/power evidence. Neither directly compares inherited and TSDI architectural representations. They must not be used as direct confirmation of TSDI.

## 2. Current architectural inference

The accumulated evidence is sufficient to justify treating **structural dynamics of transformation space** as an explicit architectural hypothesis.

It is **not yet sufficient** to declare:

- the inherited Core empirically falsified;
- TSDI empirically established;
- a new canonical Core;
- a new Evidence→Claim Matrix ontology;
- Transformational Intelligence established.

The principal unresolved issue is architectural discrimination: whether the dynamic-space representation provides empirically necessary explanatory/representational content that cannot be retained by the inherited architecture without equivalent reconstruction.

## 3. Architectural gap to be tested

The relevant distinction is not:

`static T_acc` versus `dynamic T_acc`.

The inherited architecture already permits `ΔT_acc`.

The stronger distinction is:

**A:** change in an already-defined accessible transformation space is the principal represented object.

versus

**B:** the **structure and dynamics of the transformation space itself** — including relations/dependencies, emergence/disappearance/reconfiguration of possibilities and trajectories through that space — is the object whose evolution carries explanatory information.

An experiment is architecturally discriminating only if its observations can distinguish these descriptions.

## 4. Design constraint for future experiments

Before any new scientific execution is proposed, its protocol must specify:

1. what observation is predicted to be informative under A;
2. what additional observation is predicted to be informative under B;
3. what outcome would count as AGAINST-A;
4. what outcome would count as AGAINST-B;
5. what result would remain NON-DISCRIMINATING;
6. how equivalent reconstruction under A is ruled out;
7. how terminology is prevented from becoming evidence for B.
