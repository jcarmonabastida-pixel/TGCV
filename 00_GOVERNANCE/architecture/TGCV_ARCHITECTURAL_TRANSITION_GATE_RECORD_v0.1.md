## 1. Purpose

Persist the outcome of the first explicit architectural-transition review before any new experimental design.

## 2. Established status

The transition review establishes three distinct facts:

1. The inherited working Core remains the canonical baseline: `A_Core = (S, T_acc)`.
2. Accumulated evidence makes **structural dynamics of transformation space** a legitimate architectural hypothesis requiring explicit evaluation.
3. The current evidence does not contain an independently measured E3 relation capable of discriminating that hypothesis from A.

These statements are compatible: an architectural hypothesis may be sufficiently motivated to warrant governed evaluation while remaining empirically unestablished.

## 4. Closed discriminator path

The O4/E3 path is currently closed at **E3 NOT PRESENT IN CURRENT CANONICAL EVIDENCE**.

No synthetic E3 relation may be invented merely to reopen that path.

## 5. Transition gate

**ARCHITECTURAL TRANSITION GATE: OPEN FOR GOVERNANCE FORMALIZATION; CLOSED FOR SCIENTIFIC EXECUTION.**

Opening this gate permits definition of architectural discrimination criteria and candidate observables. It does not authorize a fixture, sample-size calculation, power analysis, workflow, or scientific execution.

## 6. Required pre-experimental criteria

Any future discriminator must pre-specify:

- the A representation and its complete admissible auxiliary vocabulary;
- the candidate B structural object;
- an independently inspectable measurement rule for that object;
- the observations available to A and B;
- A-reconstruction/equivalence criteria;
- outcomes classified as AGAINST-A, AGAINST-B, or NON-DISCRIMINATING;
- an anti-post-hoc rule;
- the precise condition under which Core review would be triggered.

## 7. Governance consequence

The Core, Evidence→Claim Matrix v1.44, and RMA v3.37 are **not revised** by this record.

This record creates the explicit transition layer required to prevent either premature abandonment of A or premature confirmation of B.
