## Cumulative evidence policy

This matrix is cumulative. Every new version MUST preserve the full evidentiary content and schema of its predecessor and add, qualify, bound, supersede, or retire evidence explicitly. A new version MUST NOT silently reduce the number of claim-matrix columns, remove material evidence descriptions, collapse evidence basis into summary-only fields, or replace detailed evidence records with a short status table.

`CURRENT.md` is a stable alias of the complete versioned current matrix.

Evidence propagation does not imply claim upgrade. Claim upgrade is a separate decision requiring the applicable governance gate and evidence standard.

## Material methodological evidence — FOS C09

**Status:** CLOSED — NOT REPORTABLE UNDER FROZEN ESTIMATOR.

FOS C09 is now incorporated as a material cumulative evidence record. The experiment provides bounded evidence for:

- reconstruction of intervention availability;
- reconstruction of randomized SUB/UC assignment;
- linkage of assignment/intervention status to the 37-month endpoint;
- reconstruction of the weighted ITT arithmetic;
- explicit identification of the boundary between arithmetic reconstruction and a reportable causal estimate.

The frozen disposition is:

- retain FOS as methodological/reference evidence;
- do not report SE, CI, p-value, or a final causal effect;
- do not impute the single determinately unresolved SUB/UC endpoint;
- preserve the reconstructed weighted ITT only as arithmetic reconstruction, not as a causal result;
- no C09 claim upgrade;
- no TGCV Core change;
- no execution authorization;
- resume C09 candidate screening.

The key methodological finding is therefore two-sided: FOS demonstrates that substantial components of an intervention/endpoint reconstruction can be recovered and audited, while the currently frozen estimator cannot support a reportable causal estimate because a design-consistent randomization variance could not be demonstrated from the public-use files and frozen design information. This is material evidence about the observability and identifiability boundary of causal evaluation, not evidence of causal effect.
