# TGCV — D-OPS-24 Candidate-Pool Expansion / Empirical-Domain Discovery v0.2

**Execution:** NOT AUTHORIZED
**Scientific execution:** NOT STARTED

## 1. Purpose

Freeze the controlled protocol for bounded discovery of an independently observable empirical domain for D-OPS-24 after the historically established candidate pool did not yield an execution-ready independent candidate on the present record.

This protocol authorizes **discovery review only**. It does not authorize dataset download, data processing, empirical execution, outcome/value analysis, model fitting, or D-OPS-24 execution.

## 2. Scientific question preserved

The discovery operation serves only the residual D-OPS-24 question:

> Can an independently specified domain translation be shown, by an ex-ante trace, to preserve the role and semantic distinctions of the frozen TGCV objects without importing outcome information, collapsing objects into native proxies, or silently changing operational meaning across the translation?

Discovery must not redefine this question.

## 3. Mandatory scientific-memory gate

Before any external discovery search, the operator must consult:

1. current RMA and current pointer;
2. `02_EXTERNAL_SCIENCE/SCIENTIFIC_ASSET_REGISTRY_v0.1.md`;
3. relevant `02_LITERATURE/` SLR protocol and historical gates;
4. D-OPS-24 historical reconstruction;
5. D-OPS-24 design;
6. D-OPS-24 preflight;
7. D-OPS-24 candidate-domain eligibility audit;
8. D1 Rust, D2 MDE and D3 Self-Adaptive/Self-Evolving boundaries already established.

A relevant historical artifact blocks any claim that discovery starts "from scratch" unless explicit scientific irrelevance is recorded.

## 4. Starting candidate boundary

The discovery pool starts from the current controlled record:

- **D1 Rust:** not an independent second-domain validation on the present record because TGCV already contains a substantial Rust instantiation/validation chain.
- **D2 MDE:** prior-art family frozen; broad transformation/search-space claims are already covered and no execution-ready longitudinal empirical surface is established.
- **D3 Self-Adaptive/Self-Evolving:** prior-art family frozen; broad adaptation/variability/reachability/trajectory claims are already covered and no execution-ready independent empirical surface is established.
- **D4 Organizational:** open fallback family; requires independent evidence audit.
- **D5 State-space/Reachability:** open fallback family; requires explicit non-redundancy and empirical observability audit.
- **D6 Generativity/Adjacent-Possible:** open fallback family; requires transformation-level observability and non-collapse audit.

D4–D6 are not preferred domains. They are bounded search families only.

## 5. Bounded search scope

Discovery is limited to the following six source families, searched in this order:

**F1 — Organizational change / innovation systems**

Empirical longitudinal domains where organizational states, feasible actions/interventions and subsequent state changes can be independently represented.

**F2 — Physical/engineering reconfiguration systems**

Empirical systems where configurations, admissible operations/reconfigurations and time-ordered state changes are directly observable without defining accessibility from final performance.

**F3 — Biological/ecological adaptation or evolution**

Empirical systems where states, candidate changes and temporally ordered observations may permit independent reconstruction of accessible transformations, subject to strict non-collapse and causal-interpretation controls.

**F4 — Scientific/technological design spaces outside software**

Empirical longitudinal domains involving design alternatives, configuration changes or technological transformation choices where the candidate transformation universe is independently documented.

**F5 — Networked socio-technical systems outside software**

Empirical domains where time-indexed states and identifiable actions/interventions alter future feasible actions, with sufficient provenance and without defining accessibility from downstream success.

**F6 — Generative/design-production ecosystems outside software**

Empirical domains involving creation or transformation operations where candidate transformations are independently identifiable and their accessibility can be specified before observed outcomes.

The following are excluded from the discovery scope unless a later versioned gate explicitly reopens them: another software-package ecosystem substantially equivalent to Rust; MDE transformation frameworks already covered by the frozen MDE cluster; self-adaptive/self-evolving software families already covered by the frozen prior-art boundary.

## 6. Search sources

Discovery may use only publicly inspectable sources sufficient to establish candidate eligibility at documentary level:

- peer-reviewed literature and established scholarly indexes;
- official dataset repositories and dataset documentation;
- institutional repositories;
- authoritative project documentation;
- archival documentation with stable provenance.

Search engines may be used for discovery navigation, but candidate evidence must be anchored in an inspectable primary or authoritative source whenever available.

No candidate may be admitted solely from a search-result snippet, secondary summary, benchmark leaderboard, or promotional material.

## 7. Search terminology rule

For each family, searches must combine terms from four controlled semantic groups:

1. **state:** state, configuration, structure, composition, condition;
2. **transformation:** action, intervention, operation, transition, reconfiguration, mutation, design change;
3. **accessibility:** feasible, admissible, available, enabled, applicable, possible;
4. **temporal:** longitudinal, temporal, sequence, evolution, history, repeated observation.

Family-specific terminology may be added only when it describes the native domain construct, not when it imports TGCV terminology to manufacture a match.

## 8. Bounded search budget

The discovery pass is bounded as follows:

- maximum **3 query families per source family**;
- maximum **10 candidate records initially screened per source family**;
- maximum **6 source families**;
- no dataset download during discovery;
- no large-file processing during discovery;
- no outcome/performance ranking of candidates;
- no extension beyond the six families without a new versioned protocol decision.

The operator may stop earlier when a candidate reaches the evidence threshold required to open a full candidate-admission audit.

The budget is a governance bound, not a scientific claim about the completeness of the external literature.

## 9. Candidate unit and admission threshold

The unit of discovery is an **empirical domain candidate**, not a dataset.

A candidate may advance from DISCOVERY to candidate-admission audit only when documentary evidence can potentially support all of the following:

1. stable unit of analysis;
2. longitudinal ordering;
3. independently constructible native transformation universe `U_τ,D`;
4. non-circular native accessibility condition;
5. observable or reconstructable `T_acc,D` membership;
6. observable or reconstructable `ΔT_acc,D` across states/time;
7. accessibility assessable without requiring downstream outcome;
8. explicit treatment of empty/unresolved cases;
9. Reach/Trajectory distinction;
10. Outcome separation;
11. provenance and reproducibility;
12. substantive non-redundancy with TGCV historical work;
13. potential D-OPS-24 C1–C5 auditability.

A candidate failing a mandatory condition is not promoted by convenience or scoring.

## 11. Hard exclusions

A candidate must be excluded or held when any of the following is established:

1. accessibility is defined by downstream outcome, value, performance or adoption;
2. no meaningful longitudinal ordering exists;
3. transformations cannot be independently identified;
4. the proposed `T_acc` is only a relabelling of a native possibility/adaptation/state-space construct without a non-collapse distinction;
5. provenance is insufficient for reconstruction;
6. the candidate is substantively the same empirical representation as an already used TGCV domain;
7. admission depends on observing a favorable TGCV-like result;
8. the evidence is only a proxy where direct semantic correspondence is required;
9. the candidate belongs to an already frozen prior-art family without a distinct empirical question and representation;
10. dataset convenience is the principal reason for selection.

## 12. Outcome-blind selection rule

No candidate-selection decision may use:

- observed Outcome;
- observed Value;
- model performance;
- known successful adoption;
- effect size;
- post-hoc TGCV conformance;
- convenience of obtaining a desired result.

Dataset availability may be recorded only as a feasibility attribute after the domain candidate has entered the controlled candidate record.

## 13. D-OPS-24 translation-trace boundary

A retained candidate must be capable, in principle, of supporting the frozen trace:

`TGCV object → formal role → domain construct → semantic justification → evidence → mapping class → failure condition`

and the frozen conformance dimensions:

- C1 semantic role preservation;
- C2 non-circularity;
- C3 no downstream leakage;
- C4 non-collapse;
- C5 trace completeness.

Discovery does not itself determine conformance. It only determines whether a candidate is sufficiently observable to justify the subsequent audit.

## 14. Stopping rule

The bounded discovery pass ends at the first of:

**A. RETAIN:** at least one candidate has sufficient documentary evidence to open the full candidate-admission audit.

**B. NO ADMISSIBLE CANDIDATE IDENTIFIED:** all six source families have been screened within the frozen budget without a candidate reaching the admission threshold.

**C. GOVERNANCE STOP:** a contradiction, prior-art finding, leakage issue, or other governance nonconformance makes continuation invalid.

If B occurs, D-OPS-24 remains unexecuted. Any extension of scope requires a new versioned scientific decision; the search scope cannot be silently expanded.

## 15. Required discovery outputs

The discovery operation must produce in GitHub:

4. exclusion/prior-art record;
5. retained-candidate record, if any;
6. explicit stopping-rule result;
7. recommendation for the next controlled gate.

These outputs must preserve provenance sufficient for independent reconstruction.
