# TGCV — EXT-UPD-4.0 D-OPS-24 Continuation Decision v0.1

**Status:** CLOSED / GOVERNANCE DECISION
**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Predecessor:** EXT-UPD-3.9
**Scope:** D-OPS-24 continuation after v0.3 F1 governance hold

## 1. Trigger

D-OPS-24 v0.3 F1 documentary discovery was stopped at Q2 because the attempted F1-Q3 searches materially deviated from the exact pre-registered formulation. The searches remain non-admissible and v0.3 remains immutable. A new versioned decision is therefore required before further discovery.

## 2. Decision

D-OPS-24 will continue through a new, separately versioned documentary-discovery protocol. The v0.3 protocol, its Q1/Q2 execution and its governance deviation remain historical and are not reopened or retroactively repaired.

The next controlled operation is a **new candidate-discovery pass beginning with F2 — Physical/engineering reconfiguration systems**, because F2 is an explicitly retained open fallback in the prior candidate-domain eligibility audit and is structurally distinct from the already examined Rust, MDE and self-adaptive/self-evolving software families.

F1-Q3 is not resumed under v0.3. F1 may be reconsidered only through a future explicit versioned decision if documentary evidence justifies reopening it.

## 3. Scientific rationale

The purpose is not to find a domain that produces a favorable TGCV result. The purpose is to identify, without outcome leakage, an independently observable domain in which the frozen translation trace can potentially be constructed:

`TGCV object → formal role → domain construct → semantic justification → evidence → mapping class → failure condition`.

F2 is selected as the next discovery family because it remains open in the existing eligibility audit and offers a potentially explicit distinction between system configurations, admissible reconfigurations/actions and temporal state changes. This is a documentary-discovery hypothesis, not evidence that F2 will satisfy the threshold.

## 4. Mandatory safeguards

The next protocol must:

1. preserve the scientific-memory/from-scratch prohibition;
2. preserve all historical v0.2/v0.3 records immutably;
3. pre-register exact query-family formulations before execution;
4. use a fresh versioned search budget rather than modifying v0.3;
5. maintain outcome-blind candidate selection;
6. require independently constructible native transformation space and accessibility membership;
7. test `T_acc,D` and `ΔT_acc,D` before downstream outcomes;
8. preserve unresolved/empty cases;
9. separate Reach, Trajectory and Outcome;
10. record C1–C5 risks before any candidate admission;
11. prohibit dataset acquisition or empirical execution during documentary discovery;
12. stop immediately on procedural nonconformance and require another versioned decision.

## 5. Authorization boundary

This decision authorizes **design of the new discovery protocol and its preflight only**.

It does not yet authorize documentary search execution.

The new protocol must be frozen and propagated through the canonical governance surfaces before any search is run.

Dataset download: NOT AUTHORIZED.
Dataset processing: NOT AUTHORIZED.
Empirical execution: NOT AUTHORIZED.
Outcome/model/value analysis: NOT AUTHORIZED.
D-OPS-24 translation-conformance execution: NOT AUTHORIZED.
