# EXT-UPD-4.1 — D-OPS-24 Criteria Reassessment Decision v0.1

**Status:** CLOSED / GOVERNANCE DECISION — DESIGN REOPENED
**Date:** 2026-09-09

## Trigger

D-OPS-24 v0.4 F2 documentary discovery exhausted its three preregistered query families without admitting a candidate. The repeated zero-candidate outcome raises a methodological question: whether the bottleneck is the scarcity of suitable domains or an eligibility protocol that conflates domain admissibility with full downstream TGCV conformance.

## Decision

The programme will **not weaken the scientific invariants of TGCV**, but will reopen the D-OPS-24 design to separate:

1. **minimum cross-domain translation eligibility**, and
2. **full operational/conformance eligibility**.

A candidate should not be rejected merely because documentary evidence for downstream objects such as Reach, Trajectory, Outcome separation or complete C1-C5 conformance is not yet available at the discovery stage, provided the candidate can independently expose the core analytical distinction between a system state and a non-circularly defined set of accessible transformations, together with a temporally ordered basis for observing change.

## Scientific rationale

The transversal claim under examination is specifically whether the TGCV analytical layer can be translated across heterogeneous systems. Requiring a candidate domain to satisfy the complete downstream architecture before translation is attempted risks making the eligibility test circular: the domain is required to already expose the full TGCV architecture that the cross-domain translation is intended to test.

The following invariants remain non-negotiable at minimum eligibility:

- identifiable unit/system state;
- independently constructible transformation universe or transformation schema;
- non-circular accessibility criterion;
- explicit distinction between state and accessible transformation membership;
- temporal or ordered observations sufficient to define change in accessibility;
- provenance sufficient to reconstruct the mapping;
- no outcome-defined accessibility;
- no silent substitution of a native proxy for `T_acc`;
- explicit treatment of unresolved/empty cases;
- auditable semantic-role mapping.

Reach, Trajectory, Outcome and Value should be treated as **downstream translation tests**, not necessarily as prerequisites for candidate-domain discovery.

## Governance consequence

D-OPS-24 v0.4 remains immutable and closed. F2-Q1/Q2/Q3 remain historical discovery results and are not retroactively reclassified.

A new versioned D-OPS-24 protocol must be designed before any further candidate search. The new protocol may introduce broader discovery families and a staged eligibility gate, but cannot silently reopen v0.4 or create Q4 under its exhausted budget.

## Authorization boundary

This decision authorizes **protocol redesign and methodological assessment only**. It does not authorize candidate searching, dataset acquisition, empirical execution or conformance execution under the new protocol.

## Next gate

Create and audit a versioned D-OPS-24 v0.5 protocol implementing the staged distinction between minimum translation eligibility and full conformance, propagate it through the RMA/control surface, and obtain explicit execution authorization before broad search.
