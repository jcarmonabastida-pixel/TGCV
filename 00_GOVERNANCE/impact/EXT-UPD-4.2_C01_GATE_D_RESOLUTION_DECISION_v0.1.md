# EXT-UPD-4.2 — C-01 Gate D Resolution Decision v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Status:** CLOSED / GOVERNANCE DECISION — C-01 RETAINED; GATE D RESOLUTION OPENED

## Decision

C-01 is retained as bounded evidence of translation of the TGCV analytical Core and will not be discarded because Gate D is INDETERMINATE.

The next controlled operation is to attempt resolution of Gate D for C-01 by operationalizing the downstream analytical chain:

`ΔT_acc → ΔReach → ΔTrajectory → Outcome → Value`

The alternative of testing a second external domain is retained as a future option and is not the next operation.

## Rationale

C-01 has already passed MTE, Translation Readiness and Translation Trace. Gate D is INDETERMINATE because the available documentary evidence does not yet provide an auditable operationalization of Reach, Trajectory and Value connected through the downstream chain. This is a boundary of the current evidence, not evidence that the Core translation failed.

Resolving the boundary within C-01 is methodologically preferable before investing in a second-domain search: it allows the programme to determine whether the missing downstream layer is an operationalization problem within the present domain or a substantive limitation of the transversal architecture.

The second-domain route remains available if C-01 cannot resolve Gate D or if the resulting operationalization reveals domain-specific dependence that requires independent cross-domain testing.

## Scientific invariants preserved

- Core ontology remains `S`.
- `T_acc = F(S,C,L)` remains the analytical object.
- `ΔT_acc` remains the central phenomenon.
- `I` remains explanatory, not ontological Core.
- Observed transitions are not automatically identified with `T_acc`.
- Outcome information cannot be used to define accessibility retrospectively.
- Native constructs must remain distinguishable from TGCV constructs.
- No causal, predictive, universal, value-creation, originality or superiority claim is authorized by this decision.

## Controlled resolution architecture

The next design must separately specify and audit:

1. **Reach:** a native-domain construct corresponding to the set of states/configurations reachable under the accessible transformations, without defining Reach by observed success or downstream outcome.
2. **Trajectory:** an ordered path/sequence construct generated from accessible transformations and reachable states, rather than merely a record of observed historical transitions.
3. **Outcome:** a downstream state/event/performance construct that remains analytically distinct from accessibility, Reach and Trajectory.
4. **Value:** a further construct with an explicit operational meaning and evidence basis; engineering performance or desirability must not be silently equated with TGCV Value.
5. **Links:** explicit, auditable relations from `ΔT_acc` to `ΔReach`, from `ΔReach` to `ΔTrajectory`, from `ΔTrajectory` to Outcome, and from Outcome to Value.

## Gate-D resolution rule

A PASS may be assigned only if each link is independently operationalized and traceable without collapsing upstream constructs into downstream outcomes. If one or more links remain unsupported, the result remains INDETERMINATE or is classified as a bounded failure of the corresponding extension component; Gates A-C remain valid unless a direct contradiction is discovered.

## Authorization boundary

This decision authorizes **design of the C-01 Gate-D operationalization protocol only**.

It does not yet authorize:

- new empirical execution;
- dataset acquisition or processing;
- retrospective outcome-driven construction of accessibility;
- causal inference;
- value optimization;
- second-domain search;
- a new D-OPS-24 discovery QF;
- a final Gate-D conformance claim.

## Next gate

Create and audit a dedicated C-01 Gate-D operationalization design, including explicit definitions, admissibility criteria, temporal direction, outcome blindness, non-collapse tests, evidence requirements and a decision rule. Only after that design passes its own preflight should any empirical or documentary execution be authorized.
