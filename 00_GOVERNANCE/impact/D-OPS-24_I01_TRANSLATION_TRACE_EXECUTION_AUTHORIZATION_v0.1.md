## 3. Evidence requirements

Every material trace row must record primary source, precise locator where available, evidence type (direct/documentary reconstruction/analyst inference), semantic justification, mapping class, and explicit limitations. Analyst inference cannot be silently promoted to native evidence.

## 4. Outcome blindness

Outcome, performance, success, value and post-transition observations may not define `Pτ,D`, `T_acc,D`, or the mapping. They may be mentioned only when necessary to distinguish native constructs from observed transitions, and never as an accessibility criterion.

## 5. Protected boundaries

This authorization does not authorize:
- dataset acquisition or processing;
- empirical enumeration beyond documentary evidence;
- Reach/Trajectory/Outcome/Value extension;
- causal or predictive inference;
- Core/TR-130/TR-131 modification;
- C-01 A/B/C revision;

## 7. Evidence-to-Claim governance
