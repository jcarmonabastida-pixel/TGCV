## Decision

They are not reclassified as F1-Q3 and do not constitute candidate-discovery evidence.

The v0.3 protocol is not retroactively modified. F1-Q3 is considered **NOT EXECUTED** under the frozen v0.3 protocol, and the F1 v0.3 execution is closed at Q2 under governance hold.

## Scientific effect

No candidate is admitted. No evidence level, scientific claim, gate state, domain selection, empirical result or D-OPS-24 conformance result changes.

## Corrective rule

Any future documentary discovery must use a newly versioned protocol/decision. Exact pre-registered query formulations remain binding. A materially different formulation must be registered before execution and consumes its own budget under the applicable protocol.

The prior exploratory searches remain historical/non-admissible and cannot be reused as compliant discovery evidence unless a later decision explicitly defines a scientifically valid reuse pathway without retroactive reclassification.

## Authorization

This correction does not authorize F1-Q3, F2, dataset acquisition, data processing, empirical execution or D-OPS-24 conformance testing. A subsequent controlled operation requires a separate explicit authorization after propagation and consistency closure.
