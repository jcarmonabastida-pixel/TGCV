# EXT-UPD-4.6 — Second Independent Domain Route Decision v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Status:** CLOSED / GOVERNANCE DECISION — SECOND-DOMAIN ROUTE OPENED

## 1. Trigger

EXT-UPD-4.5 has been propagated and closed consistently. C-01 remains retained as bounded translation evidence, while both the original Gate-D route and the separately designed alternative Gate-D route remain INDETERMINATE. The alternative route stopped at D1 because a defensibly finite/enumerable candidate transformation universe could not be closed under the frozen C-01 construction.

## 2. Decision

Open the **second independent-domain route** for TGCV validation.

The purpose is not to replace or invalidate C-01, but to test whether the frozen TGCV analytical distinctions can be translated and, if supported, extended in a domain genuinely independent of the Rust and aircraft/control-system instantiations already used in the programme.

## 3. Independence requirement

The second domain must be independent from:

- Rust ecosystem/package-management evidence;
- aircraft/control-system reconfiguration evidence represented by C-01;
- previously rejected/absorbed candidate families where the existing record would compromise independent-domain status.

The domain-selection process must be outcome-blind and must not be chosen because it is expected to produce a positive TGCV result.

## 4. Scientific invariants

The following remain frozen:

- Core ontology and TR-130/TR-131 conclusions;
- distinction between native state and transformation;
- distinction between candidate transformation universe and accessible subset;
- accessibility defined independently of observed outcome;
- `T_acc` distinct from observed execution;
- `ΔT_acc` defined through ordered states;
- native-domain semantics preserved rather than renamed into TGCV terminology;
- unresolved/empty outcomes explicitly representable;
- no causal inference from temporal association;
- no value inference without an independent native valuation criterion.

## 5. Route architecture

The second-domain route will proceed in controlled gates:

**A. Domain eligibility → B. Minimum Translation Eligibility → C. Translation Readiness → D. Translation Trace → E. downstream operationalization/conformance only if upstream gates pass.**

Discovery must occur before domain commitment. Dataset search/download is not authorized merely by this decision.

## 6. Discovery protocol

Use the frozen D-OPS-24 v0.5 architecture as the governing discovery framework. A new domain-specific search protocol/QF authorization must be explicitly registered before execution. Historical v0.4/F2 budget is not reset or reused silently; v0.5 is the active redesigned protocol.

Initial authorized work under this decision is **design and preflight only**. No search execution is released by this decision alone.

## 7. Exclusions

This decision does not authorize:

- a new D-OPS query family execution;
- dataset acquisition or processing;
- empirical execution;
- causal/predictive analysis;
- value optimization;
- modification of the Core;
- revision of C-01 A-C;
- external asset refresh.

## 8. Evidence governance

Every material evidence item generated by the second-domain route will receive an explicit Evidence→Claim impact assessment before consistency closure, in accordance with EXT-UPD-4.3.

## 9. Expected outcome space

A candidate may be accepted, rejected, or remain indeterminate. A zero-result discovery does not establish absence of a domain phenomenon, universal validity, or failure of TGCV. A positive candidate does not upgrade claims until the corresponding gates are completed.

## 10. Next controlled operation

**Design the second-domain discovery/eligibility protocol under D-OPS-24 v0.5, then perform its dedicated preflight. Execution remains unauthorized until that preflight and an explicit execution authorization pass.**
