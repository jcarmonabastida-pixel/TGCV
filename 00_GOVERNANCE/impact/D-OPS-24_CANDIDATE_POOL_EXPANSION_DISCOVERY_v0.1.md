## 1. Purpose

Open a controlled candidate-pool expansion operation because the historically established D-OPS-24 candidate pool has been audited without identifying an execution-ready independent empirical candidate on the present record.

This operation does **not** select a domain, search for a preferred outcome, download a dataset, or execute D-OPS-24. It defines the controlled conditions under which a new empirical domain may subsequently be discovered and admitted.

## 2. Trigger and scientific boundary

The preceding candidate audit closed D2 MDE and D3 Self-Adaptive/Self-Evolving as prior-art boundaries and found no eligible execution-ready empirical surface. Rust is not an independent second-domain validation on the present record because TGCV already contains a substantial Rust instantiation and validation chain.

D4–D6 remain fallback candidates but are not selected by elimination.

Accordingly, a new domain may be introduced only through this explicit controlled expansion operation. No ad hoc domain is authorized.

## 3. Mandatory scientific-memory gate

Before any discovery search, the operator must consult:

- current RMA and current pointer;
- `02_EXTERNAL_SCIENCE/SCIENTIFIC_ASSET_REGISTRY_v0.1.md`;
- `02_LITERATURE/` SLR operational protocol and relevant historical gates;
- the D-OPS-24 historical reconstruction, design and preflight;
- the closed candidate-domain eligibility audit.

The existence of a relevant historical artifact blocks a claim that discovery starts "from scratch" unless explicit scientific irrelevance is recorded.

## 4. Discovery objective

The objective is to identify one or more **independently observable empirical domains** in which the frozen TGCV translation objects can potentially be reconstructed without importing TGCV definitions into the native domain.

The discovery target is therefore not "a dataset with a good result". It is a domain/data surface that can support an ex-ante audit of:

- stable unit of analysis;
- longitudinal observation;
- independently constructible candidate transformation universe `U_τ`;
- non-circular accessibility predicate `P_τ`;
- `T_acc` membership;
- `ΔT_acc` across time/state;
- accessibility before execution;
- empty/unresolved cases;
- Reach and Trajectory separation;
- Outcome separation;
- provenance and reproducibility;
- substantive non-redundancy;
- execution feasibility.

## 5. Admission criteria

A discovered candidate remains **DISCOVERY ONLY** until evidence supports all mandatory conditions:

`DS-3 ∧ DS-4 ∧ DS-5 ∧ DS-6 ∧ DS-7 ∧ DS-8 ∧ DS-10 ∧ DS-11 ∧ DS-13`

with no unresolved critical failure in DS-1, DS-2 or DS-12, and with D-OPS-24 C1–C5 potentially auditable.

C1–C5 are:

- C1 semantic role preservation;
- C2 non-circularity;
- C3 no downstream leakage;
- C4 non-collapse;
- C5 trace completeness.

A candidate failing a mandatory criterion is rejected or returned for bounded evidence clarification; it is not promoted by scoring convenience.

## 6. Discovery constraints

The discovery operation must not:

1. choose a domain because it already has a favorable TGCV-looking result;
2. choose a dataset because it maximizes a desired effect;
3. use downstream outcome/value to define accessibility;
4. redefine the TGCV objects to fit a convenient native representation;
5. treat a native "possibility/adaptation/state space" as `T_acc` without a non-collapse audit;
6. treat a proxy as direct evidence;
7. count a previously used TGCV domain as an independent external validation without explicit justification;
8. bypass the scientific-memory registry;
9. download or process a large dataset before candidate admissibility is established;
10. introduce a new candidate informally outside the controlled record.

## 7. Evidence hierarchy

Candidate discovery should proceed in this order:

**E0 — Historical repository evidence:** existing TGCV literature/gate artifacts.  
**E1 — Public domain/data documentation:** stable description of unit, events, timestamps, transformations and provenance.  
**E2 — Candidate structural audit:** evidence for DS-1–DS-14 using documentation and inspectable metadata.  
**E3 — Translation-trace preflight:** ex-ante C1–C5 assessment.  
**E4 — Dataset acquisition/processing:** only after candidate admission and separate execution authorization.

No E4 activity is authorized by this artifact.

## 9. Independence and non-redundancy

A candidate is not independent merely because its domain label differs from Rust, MDE or Self-Adaptive Software. Independence is assessed at the level of the empirical representation and scientific question.

In particular, a candidate must provide a meaningful opportunity to test whether the frozen TGCV analytical distinctions survive translation rather than merely rename a native transformation, adaptation, state-space or reachability construct.

## 10. Discovery stopping rule

Discovery may stop and a candidate-admission audit may open when at least one candidate has sufficient documentary evidence to justify a full DS-1–DS-14 and C1–C5 audit.

Discovery must not stop merely because a candidate is convenient.

If no candidate reaches that threshold after the predefined bounded search scope, the correct result is **NO ADMISSIBLE CANDIDATE IDENTIFIED** and a subsequent decision must determine whether to extend the search scope, revise a criterion through a versioned scientific gate, or close D-OPS-24 without execution.

## 11. Required next operation

The next controlled operation is **bounded empirical-domain discovery**, not dataset download and not D-OPS-24 execution.

The discovery protocol must first be registered/frozen with its search scope, evidence sources, exclusion rules and stopping rule. Only then may external empirical domains be searched.
