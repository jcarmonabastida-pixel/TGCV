## 1. Purpose

Provide a fresh, versioned and mechanically auditable documentary-discovery protocol for identifying an independently observable external domain capable of supporting the frozen D-OPS-24 translation question.

This protocol does not reopen v0.3. All v0.3 records, including F1-Q1/Q2 and the non-admissible F1-Q3 exploratory searches, remain immutable historical state.

## 2. Frozen scientific question

> Can an independently specified domain translation be shown, by an ex-ante trace, to preserve the role and semantic distinctions of the frozen TGCV objects without importing outcome information, collapsing objects into native proxies, or silently changing operational meaning across the translation?

## 3. Scientific-memory gate

Before any search execution, the operator must consult the current RMA/pointer, scientific-asset registry, relevant SLR history, D-OPS-24 reconstruction/design/preflight, candidate-domain eligibility audit, D1/D2/D3 prior-art boundaries, EXT-UPD-3.9 and EXT-UPD-4.0.

No activity may be represented as starting from scratch where relevant historical artifacts exist.

## 4. Scope

The initial discovery family is:

- **F2 — Physical/engineering reconfiguration systems**

Other families remain outside the initial execution and may only be opened under a subsequent explicit versioned decision.

The following remain excluded unless separately reopened: Rust/software-package ecosystems substantially equivalent to existing TGCV work; MDE transformation frameworks covered by the frozen prior-art boundary; self-adaptive/self-evolving software families covered by the frozen prior-art boundary.

## 5. Candidate unit and threshold

A candidate is a domain/empirical representation, not merely a paper, dataset or application label.

Promotion to candidate-admission audit requires documentary support for all mandatory dimensions:

1. stable unit;
2. longitudinal ordering;
3. independently constructible native transformation universe `U_τ,D`;
4. non-circular accessibility criterion;
5. constructible `T_acc,D` membership;
6. observable `ΔT_acc,D` across states/time;
7. accessibility assessable before downstream outcome;
8. explicit unresolved/empty cases;
9. Reach/Trajectory distinction;
10. Outcome separation;
11. provenance and reproducibility;
12. substantive non-redundancy with existing TGCV empirical work;
13. potential auditability of C1–C5.

Failure of any mandatory dimension prevents promotion.

## 6. Query-family control

A query family consists of exactly:

- one fixed state-term set;
- one fixed transformation-term set;
- one fixed accessibility-term set;
- one fixed temporal-term set;
- optional pre-registered F2 native-domain terms;
- one fixed source-type restriction.

Any material change to these components creates a new query family and consumes one budget unit.

Pagination, opening results, following links and inspecting an identified primary source do not create a new query family when the formulation is unchanged.

Every Q-ID must be recorded in the search log before execution.

## 7. Search budget

For F2:

- maximum 3 pre-registered query families;
- maximum 10 candidate records initially screened;
- cumulative budget across all engines, repositories, sessions and operators;
- no reset or retroactive reclassification.

The Q1–Q3 formulations must be frozen in the search log before any query is executed.

## 8. Outcome-blind selection

Search and candidate screening must not use downstream Outcome, Value, performance, adoption, effect size, post-hoc TGCV conformance or convenience of a favorable result.

Dataset availability is a feasibility attribute only and cannot determine candidate selection.

## 9. Evidence hierarchy

Discovery may navigate peer-reviewed literature, established scholarly indexes, official dataset repositories/documentation, institutional repositories, authoritative project documentation and stable archival sources.

Candidate admission requires an inspectable primary or authoritative source whenever available. Search snippets and secondary summaries cannot alone support admission.

## 10. Falsifiers and hard exclusions

Reject or hold a candidate if:

- accessibility is defined from outcome or downstream success;
- transformations cannot be independently identified;
- longitudinal ordering is absent or post hoc;
- `T_acc,D` collapses into a native possibility/adaptation/state-space construct without a distinct TGCV analytical role;
- the native transformation universe is not independently constructible;
- provenance is insufficient;
- the representation is substantively redundant with frozen TGCV work;
- admission depends on a favorable TGCV result;
- an impermissible proxy silently replaces the intended object;
- C1–C5 cannot be meaningfully audited.

## 11. Required candidate record

For every initially screened candidate, record:

- stable candidate ID;
- F2 and Q-ID;
- exact query formulation;
- timestamp;
- authoritative sources;
- native unit/state;
- native transformation representation;
- temporal ordering;
- accessibility construction;
- `U_τ,D`, `T_acc,D`, `ΔT_acc,D` feasibility;
- Reach/Trajectory and Outcome separation;
- provenance/reproducibility;
- DS-1–DS-14 preliminary status;
- C1–C5 preliminary status;
- leakage and collapse risks;
- mapping class;
- evidence gaps;
- admission/exclusion reason.

## 12. Stopping rules

**A — RETAIN:** at least one candidate reaches the documentary threshold and is transferred to a separate candidate-admission audit.

**B — NO ADMISSIBLE CANDIDATE:** F2 is exhausted within budget without a qualifying candidate.

**C — GOVERNANCE STOP:** any procedural deviation, prior-art conflict, leakage problem or other governance contradiction invalidates continuation.

If B or C occurs, no dataset or empirical execution follows. Further scope requires a new versioned decision.

## 15. Non-claims

This protocol establishes no second-domain validation, cross-domain generalisation, translation conformance, causality, prediction, value linkage, originality or superiority. It defines only a controlled route for documentary candidate discovery.
