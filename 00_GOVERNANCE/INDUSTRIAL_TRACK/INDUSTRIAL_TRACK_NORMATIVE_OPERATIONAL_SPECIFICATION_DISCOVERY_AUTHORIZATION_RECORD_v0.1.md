## Decision

The bounded documentary discovery operation for normative/operational specifications is explicitly authorized.

The authorization follows design review PASS and confirms that the canonical Industrial Track gate nomenclature is established in `INDUSTRIAL_TRACK_GOVERNANCE_SPEC_v0.1.md` (`IT-G0` through `IT-G5`). No new admission gate is created by this operation.

## Authorized scope

Execution is limited to documentary discovery and screening of external normative or operational specifications under the frozen protocol and authorization package.

The operation may identify and screen standards, rules, procedures, policies, technical requirements, regulatory instruments, architecture constraints, or equivalent normative/operational artifacts that independently constrain admissible alternatives at the relevant decision time.

## Frozen controls

The operation shall preserve without modification:

- inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- candidate identity and version requirements;
- temporal applicability rule;
- independence/non-circularity rule;
- accessibility boundary;
- evidence hierarchy;
- maximum 20 screened specifications;
- maximum 10 `RETAINED-CANDIDATE` dispositions;
- one bounded discovery cycle;
- stop rules;
- post-discovery routing through the existing Industrial Track gates.

No criterion may be relaxed or changed after candidate outcomes are observed.

## Exclusions

This authorization does **not** authorize:

- industrial experimentation;
- dataset or event-log execution;
- Rust / EXT-1.1 execution;
- O3 rescue;
- Stage-C or Stage-D execution;
- partner/customer evidential execution;
- causal inference;
- value optimization or financial-value proof;
- superiority or predictive-validity claims;
- Core modification;
- TR-131 or TR-132 modification;
- epistemic claim upgrades.

## Post-discovery disposition

No candidate is admitted merely by appearing in the discovery register. Any retained candidate must enter the established Industrial Track governance sequence, beginning with `IT-G0` / `IT-G1` as applicable, before industrial-case admission or comparative analysis.

The discovery result must be recorded independently of utility and outcome evidence.

## Scientific status

This authorization introduces no scientific evidence and does not change TGCV Core, TR-131, TR-132, C01-C16, or any existing epistemic status.
