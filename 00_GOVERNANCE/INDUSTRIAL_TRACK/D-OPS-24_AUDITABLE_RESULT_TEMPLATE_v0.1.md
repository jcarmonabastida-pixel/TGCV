## Adjudication rules

- **PASS:** all mandatory accessibility-identifiability conditions are independently closed by documentary evidence.
- **FAIL:** at least one mandatory condition is demonstrably unavailable or invalid.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** available evidence is insufficient to decide without additional privileged or non-specified information.

PASS is documentary accessibility identifiability only. It does not establish industrial utility, causality, value, superiority, or scientific validation.

## Evidence-class rule

Class A is necessary for accessibility closure. Classes B/C may support reconstruction. Class D outcome/utility evidence is excluded from accessibility adjudication.

## Anti-rescue rule

No missing condition may be supplied by analyst assumption, inferred from event absence, downstream outcome, utility, or retrospective criterion relaxation.

## Search closure

Record the final scope searched, sources considered, candidates screened, and the reason for every PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE disposition. If a candidate reaches the threshold for separately governed IT-G1 review, stop and do not perform that later gate within D-OPS-24.
