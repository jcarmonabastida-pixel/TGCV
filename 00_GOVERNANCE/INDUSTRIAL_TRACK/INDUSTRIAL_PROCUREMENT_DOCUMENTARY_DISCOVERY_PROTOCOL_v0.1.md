# TGCV — Industrial Procurement Documentary Discovery Protocol v0.1

**Status:** PROPOSED — GOVERNED PREPARATION ONLY  
**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Parent line:** Industrial Track  
**Previous discovery cycle:** IT-NOSD-EXEC-001 CLOSED and NOT REOPENED

## Purpose
Define a new, separately governed documentary-discovery operation to identify public procurement / technology-service procedures whose decision-time documentation may support later industrial case construction.

This protocol is documentary discovery only. It does not admit industrial cases, establish utility, validate TGCV, or authorize IT-G2–IT-G5.

## Scope
Target completed public procurement procedures involving technology, software, digital services, infrastructure or technically configurable solutions, preferably where public records may contain:
- tender/specification documents;
- explicit admissible technical alternatives or constraints;
- evaluation criteria and scoring rules;
- committee/minutes or evaluation reports;
- award decision/resolution;
- identifiable publication and decision dates;
- identifiable contracting authority and procedure.

## Frozen screening budget
- Maximum candidates screened: **12**
- Maximum candidates retained: **6**
- Indeterminate candidates: recorded separately and not silently retained
- One bounded discovery cycle
- Stop when the maximum retained set is reached or the evidence universe is exhausted within the 12-candidate cap.

## Frozen inclusion criteria
A candidate may be retained only if all are satisfied:
1. identifiable contracting authority;
2. identifiable procurement procedure / file identity;
3. identifiable publication and decision-time temporal identity;
4. bounded procurement object and system/domain context;
5. publicly identifiable admissible alternatives, constraints, or decision criteria;
6. decision-time availability of the relevant public documents independently established;
7. independent public provenance;
8. candidate identity does not depend on later outcomes;
9. no retrospective modification of TGCV Core, accessibility criteria or acceptance rules;
10. sufficient public documentation to justify a later G1 screening attempt without inventing missing case facts.

## Exclusions
Exclude candidates requiring:
- confidential bids or non-public technical annexes to reconstruct the decision;
- proprietary evidence as an admission prerequisite;
- outcome-dependent candidate identity;
- retrospective inference of decision-time accessibility;
- a new scientific dataset execution;
- causal/value inference during discovery.

## Evidence separation
Discovery records documentary facts only. Later IT-G1 assessment determines whether a retained candidate constitutes a concrete industrial case. Retention is never case admission.

## Routing
For each retained candidate:
`documentary retention → IT-G1 case identification → IT-G2 variable observability → IT-G3 accessibility closure → IT-G4 utility protocol freeze → IT-G5 execution authorization`

No gate may be skipped or inferred from discovery status.

## Deliverables
A separately governed execution must produce:
1. execution record;
2. candidate register;
3. evidence references;
4. screening disposition for every screened candidate;
5. closure record;
6. explicit statement that no industrial case was admitted by discovery alone.

## Scientific boundary
Scientific Core remains unchanged. No C01–C16 status changes are permitted by this protocol. Industrial Track remains PROPOSED and industrial execution remains NOT AUTHORIZED unless a case-specific IT-G5 authorization is subsequently granted.

## Authorization boundary
This protocol is **not an execution authorization**. A separate authorization package and authorization record must freeze this protocol before discovery starts.
