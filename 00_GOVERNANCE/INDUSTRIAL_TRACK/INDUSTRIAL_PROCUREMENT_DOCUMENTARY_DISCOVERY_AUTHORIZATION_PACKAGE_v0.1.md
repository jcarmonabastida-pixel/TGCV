## Operation

New bounded documentary discovery operation focused on public procurement / technology-service contracting.

## Separation from prior cycle

This operation is **not** a continuation, extension or reopening of `IT-NOSD-EXEC-001`. The previous normative/operational specification discovery cycle remains CLOSED. Its 13 screened candidates and 10 retained candidates are not re-screened under this package.

## Frozen authorization conditions

Subject to explicit authorization, the operation is limited to:
- documentary discovery only;
- maximum 12 candidates screened;
- maximum 6 retained;
- frozen inclusion/exclusion criteria from protocol v0.1;
- public evidence only;
- no confidential/proprietary evidence;
- no industrial execution;
- no IT-G2/G3/G4/G5 execution;
- no new scientific dataset execution;
- no causal, value, utility, superiority or predictive claim;
- no modification of Core or scientific gate status.

## Candidate selection priority

Prefer completed technology/IT procurement procedures with public availability of tender/specification documents plus evaluation reports, committee acts/minutes and award resolution. Preference is given to procedures where the decision-time record explicitly exposes competing technical configurations, admissible solution alternatives, constraints or scored criteria.

## Stop and closure rules

Stop at the first of:
1. 12 screened candidates;
2. 6 retained candidates;
3. demonstrable exhaustion of the bounded search universe.

Every screened candidate receives a disposition. No candidate is retained merely because later outcome evidence is attractive.

## Required execution artifacts

If authorized:
1. execution record;
2. candidate register;
3. evidence references;
4. per-candidate disposition/rationale;
5. closure record;
6. explicit routing statement for any retained candidate.
