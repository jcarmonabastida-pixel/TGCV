## 1. Purpose

Translate the closed IT-METH-I FAA AMOC experiment into explicit methodological requirements for any future industrial utility comparison.

This is a methodological governance artifact. It does not reopen IT-METH-I, alter IT-G4, modify TGCV Core, or authorize a new experiment.

## 2. Evidence basis

The requirements are derived from the governed closure of IT-METH-I. That closure records an INCONCLUSIVE result: R001 and R002 recovered the same bounded documentary structure, but reproducibility was not cleanly scorable because of an unresolved documentary date discrepancy, effort was not validly comparable because the R002 package lacked an admitted effort convention, and no practically relevant improvement over the conventional comparator was established.

## 3. Adopted requirements

### MR-01 — Effort convention must be frozen before blind execution

If analytical effort is a utility dimension, the blind package must contain the complete operational convention before execution begins, including:

- start event;
- stop event;
- included and excluded activities;
- treatment of interruptions;
- unit of measurement;
- treatment of tool/runtime overhead;
- required recorded timestamps.

No post-hoc effort convention may be introduced after observing results.

### MR-02 — Comparison-critical documentary anchors must be resolved before execution

Any documentary datum that can affect field-level agreement or adjudication must have a canonical source and anchor before blind execution.

If competing documentary anchors remain unresolved, the case must either:

1. resolve them before execution; or
2. explicitly classify the datum as non-critical to the decision rule before execution.

A comparison-critical discrepancy discovered after execution must not be silently corrected inside a sealed artifact.

### MR-03 — Candidate cases must have discriminative utility potential

A future industrial case should not be selected merely because TGCV and the conventional method can both reconstruct the same information.

Before IT-G1 authorization, the case specification must identify at least one plausible, pre-declared decision-relevant dimension on which the TGCV representation could produce a practically relevant improvement over the conventional comparator without relaxing the comparator or introducing post-decision information.

If no such discriminative dimension exists, the case should remain documentary/accessibility work rather than advance to a comparative utility experiment.

### MR-04 — Utility metric observability must be established before authorization

Every mandatory utility metric must have an executable observation rule, an admissible evidence source, and a deterministic adjudication rule before IT-G5 authorization.

A metric that cannot be measured under the frozen package must be marked as non-observable before execution or the case must not advance to a decision rule that depends on that metric.

### MR-05 — Reproducibility must be designed into the package

Where reproducibility is a required utility dimension, the blind package must define:

- the reconstruction unit;
- the comparison unit;
- the field-level matching rule;
- treatment of indeterminate fields;
- the agreement threshold;
- treatment of documentary discrepancies;
- the conditions under which agreement is scored versus declared inconclusive.

### MR-06 — Evidence integrity and semantic integrity are separate gates

Future packages must preserve both:

- byte-level integrity of frozen evidence; and
- semantic/documentary integrity of the information used by the decision rule.

A byte-exact artifact may still contain a documentary ambiguity. Byte integrity therefore cannot substitute for documentary-anchor governance.

### MR-07 — No new execution merely to repair a methodological deficiency discovered after closure

A closed experiment that is INCONCLUSIVE because a precondition was absent must not be rerun solely to manufacture a cleaner result from the same case.

Any rerun requires a separately governed experimental question, a revised frozen protocol, explicit authorization, and a justification that the new execution addresses a substantive research question rather than post-hoc optimization of the outcome.

## 4. Consequence for industrial-track case selection

The next candidate should be screened for four properties before experimental authorization:

1. **Documentary closure:** decision-relevant accessibility conditions can be frozen and independently evidenced.
2. **Metric observability:** every mandatory utility metric is executable under the package boundary.
3. **Discriminative potential:** TGCV and comparator have a credible opportunity to differ on a pre-specified practical dimension.
4. **Reproducibility readiness:** independent reconstruction and comparison rules can be frozen before execution.

A candidate failing any one of these conditions should not advance directly to a comparative IT-G4-style utility experiment.

## 5. Relationship to TGCV Core

These requirements constrain experimental methodology only.

They do **not** imply:

- a change to TGCV Core;
- a change to the ontological status of interactions/mechanisms;
- a new primitive;
- confirmation or refutation of TGCV as a whole.

The IT-METH-I result remains INCONCLUSIVE and bounded to the industrial case.

That assessment should be performed on the candidate case and package, not by modifying the closed FAA experiment.
