# IT-G1-B — ERA Case Identification Attempt 001

**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Status:** CLOSED — BOUNDED DOCUMENTARY IDENTIFICATION ATTEMPT  
**Candidate:** IT-NOSD-008 — ERA vehicle authorisation

## 1. Result

The public ERA material confirms that a Vehicle Type Authorisation Case is a formally structured case. ERA's current data vocabulary defines it as a process supporting a vehicle-type/vehicle authorisation application, determined by one vehicle type and a set of vehicles, with explicit properties including area of use, configuration-dependent conditions/restrictions, configuration-dependent compliance, safety declaration and authorising Member State. citeturn0search3turn0search4

ERA also confirms that decisions following applications are held in the One-Stop Shop, while authorised vehicle-type information is subsequently made public through ERATV; some supporting documents are public through ERADIS. citeturn0search0turn0search2

## 2. Critical identifiability finding

The public material establishes the **case schema and the existence of public post-authorisation records**, but the accessible evidence located in this bounded search does not yet establish one complete historical case with all four frozen IT-G1 fields simultaneously recoverable from public sources.

In particular, the public ERATV documentation exposes the structure and attributes of authorised vehicle types, but the application-level decision record itself remains in the OSS, where ERA states that applicants can access information concerning their own applications. citeturn0search0turn0search23

Therefore we must not equate an ERATV record with a fully reconstructable application/decision episode.

## 4. Decision

**IT-G1-B ERA ATTEMPT 001 = NOT SUFFICIENT FOR FORMAL CASE ADMISSION.**

The candidate is materially stronger than the FAA AMOC route at the **schema and public-record level**, but the current bounded search does not justify claiming a concrete, reproducible industrial case.

No outcome-dependent reconstruction is permitted. No inference from the existence of an ERATV record to the hidden application process is permitted.

## 5. Routing consequence

IT-G1 remains **HOLD / NOT CLOSED**.

The ERA candidate remains the strongest documentary candidate but requires a narrower public-record identification operation, potentially using a specific ERATV record plus independently published authorisation material, before formal IT-G1 admission can be considered.

If that operation cannot close the missing fields, the candidate will be marked non-admissible for the current industrial track and the next retained candidate will be screened.
