# IT-G1-J — ENAC Operator AltMoC Case Identification Screen 001

**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Status:** CLOSED — INSUFFICIENT FOR FORMAL CASE ADMISSION  
**Candidate:** `IT-NOSD-003` — EASA Air Operations  
**Screen type:** bounded public case-identifiability screening

## 1. Purpose

Assess whether publicly accessible evidence can identify one concrete industrial air-operations decision episode satisfying the frozen IT-G1 criteria, without using post-decision outcomes or private evidence.

This is a screening operation only. It does not enter IT-G2.

## 2. Public evidence located

ENAC's official Alternative Means of Compliance page states that AltMoCs may be issued by the competent authority or proposed by an organisation for prior approval, and separately lists AltMoCs approved to operators. The page identifies multiple operator-level AltMoC records, including:

- `2026-00001` — OPS — transport of large dogs in passenger cabin for specified Airbus aircraft;
- `2024-00003` — OPS — alternative means for CAT.OP.MPA.160;
- `2024-00002` — OPS — alternative means related to AMC1 ORO.CC.115(c);
- `2024-00001` — OPS — flight data recorder for Boeing 777-F;
- `2023-00002` — OPS — flight data recorder;
- `2022-00001 rev. 1` — OPS — standard mass values for passengers/baggage on a specified route;
- `2021-00003 rev. 1` — OPS — LVO approval;
- `2021-00002` — OPS — mass and balance/loading;
- `2021-00001` — OPS — ETOPS operational approval;
- `2019-00001` — OPS — ORO.FC.240, subsequently revoked;
- `2018-00005` — OPS — CAT.IDE.H.145 for Leonardo AW109 variants;
- `2015-00001` — OPS — navigation database as primary navigation means.

The same official page states that the content of operator-approved AltMoCs is available on request to entitled parties. Therefore the public table establishes existence, temporal identity, sector, and regulatory subject, but does not expose the complete organisation-level decision package.

## 4. Decision

**IT-G1-J = INSUFFICIENT FOR FORMAL CASE ADMISSION.**

The public ENAC register is strong evidence that real operator-level AltMoC decisions exist, but the public evidence boundary does not provide a complete, independently reconstructable individual decision episode satisfying all frozen IT-G1 fields.

No IT-G2 assessment is authorized from this screen.

## 5. Methodological significance

This screening does not invalidate the candidate domain. It identifies a recurring evidence-access constraint: regulatory registers can expose the existence and high-level identity of an industrial decision while withholding the underlying application, technical justification, configuration, and organisation identity needed for a fully bounded public case.

The screen therefore remains a documentary negative result, not a scientific or industrial failure.
