**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Status:** CLOSED — BOUNDED DOCUMENTARY SCREENING  
**Gate:** IT-G1 — Case identifiability  
**Scope:** retained candidates IT-NOSD-001 to IT-NOSD-010

## 1. Purpose

Screen the retained documentary candidates for their ability to support identification of a concrete industrial case using independently accessible public information, without partner evidence, proprietary data, outcome-dependent case construction, or modification of the frozen IT-G1 criteria.

This is a routing screen. It is **not** IT-G1 admission and does not authorize IT-G2.

## 2. Screening criteria

A candidate receives a higher routing priority when public sources provide a plausible path to independently establish:

- a concrete industrial decision context;
- a bounded system boundary;
- a reproducible unit/case identifier;
- a decision-time temporal frame;
- the relevant normative/operational version at that time;
- a separation between admissible alternatives/constraints and the eventual outcome.

## 4. Primary routing candidate — IT-NOSD-008

**IT-NOSD-008 — ERA-PRG-005/02-361 Practical Arrangements for Vehicle Authorisation** is routed as the primary candidate for the next concrete case-identification attempt.

The decisive advantage is not the normative specification alone. ERA explicitly models a **Vehicle Type Authorisation Case** as a case associated with a specific vehicle type and set of vehicles, with properties including area of use, authorising Member State, configuration-dependent restrictions and conformity/compliance information. citeturn1search0turn1search2

ERA also states that decisions following vehicle/type authorisation applications are held in the One-Stop Shop and that, after authorisation, vehicle-type information is publicly available through ERATV, with certain supporting documents publicly available through ERADIS. citeturn1search6

The ERA authorisation process itself has explicit stages, including identification of applicable rules and the authorisation case, alternative approaches during pre-engagement, assessment, application, processing and final authorisation. citeturn0search48

This provides a materially stronger public path to an identifiable case than the FAA AMOC candidate.

## 5. Boundary condition

This screening does **not** establish that a concrete railway case has already been admitted.

The next operation must identify one actual vehicle/type authorisation record and independently freeze:

1. case/application identifier;
2. vehicle type and bounded vehicle set;
3. area of use / system boundary;
4. authorising entity/member state;
5. applicable decision-time rule/specification versions;
6. decision or authorisation date;
7. any relevant alternative/constraint mechanism observable before the outcome.

If these fields cannot be reconstructed without outcome-dependent inference, IT-NOSD-008 shall not be admitted and the next candidate will be screened.
