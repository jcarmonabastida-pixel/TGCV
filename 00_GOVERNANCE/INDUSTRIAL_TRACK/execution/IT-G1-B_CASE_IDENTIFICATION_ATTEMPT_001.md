## 1. Purpose

Attempt to identify one concrete industrial AMOC decision case using independently available public FAA material, without using outcome information to define the candidate case or importing partner/proprietary data.

## 2. Documentary basis

FAA identifies AC 39-10 as active guidance for persons seeking approval of an AMOC to an Airworthiness Directive. FAA also states that an AMOC provides an alternative way of addressing the unsafe condition specified by an AD. citeturn0search1turn0search0

FAA Order 8110.103B provides a concrete **illustrative** AMOC approval chain involving AD 2010-01-XX, paragraph (g), Service Bulletins X/Y, and replacement of sealant type A with type B. However, the document explicitly uses placeholders (`AD 2010-01-XX`, `Service Bulletin X`, `Service Bulletin Y`) as an example of how an approval statement should be written. It is therefore not evidence of a real identifiable industrial episode. citeturn1search12turn1search13

FAA material on AD 2006-15-15 confirms that AMOCs are real operational decisions and describes the request/approval pathway, but the publicly available report does not provide a sufficiently bounded individual AMOC decision episode for the present IT-G1 criteria. citeturn0search25

## 4. Decision

**IT-G1-B = INSUFFICIENT FOR CASE ADMISSION.**

The attempt successfully confirms that the AMOC mechanism is an appropriate candidate mechanism, but it does **not** identify a concrete industrial case satisfying the four mandatory IT-G1 fields.

The illustrative AD 2010-01-XX material is explicitly excluded from case admission because its identifiers are placeholders. No retrospective substitution of a real AD is permitted.
