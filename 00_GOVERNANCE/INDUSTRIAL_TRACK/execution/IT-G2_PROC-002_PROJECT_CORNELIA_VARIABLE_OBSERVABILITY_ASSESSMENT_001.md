## 4. Critical observability gap

The decisive problem is **not case identity**. It is the transition from document existence to recoverable decision-time variable content.

For TGCV industrial-track purposes, it is insufficient to know that an `ANEXO 4 PPT`, pliego and technical evaluation report exist. Their relevant contents must be recoverable sufficiently to establish, independently and without outcome-driven inference:

1. the decision-time system/context representation;
2. the relevant candidate transformations or technical alternatives;
3. the conditions enabling each admissible alternative;
4. the constraints/limits excluding alternatives;
5. the relation between those conditions and the evaluated alternatives.

The present public evidence boundary does not close all five items.

## 5. Non-retroactivity

The 2024 contract modification listed on the procurement record is explicitly excluded from reconstruction of the 2021 decision-time state. Later implementation or operational outcomes cannot be used to infer what was accessible or admissible at the original decision point.

Likewise, the identity of the awarded supplier is not used to define the pre-decision transformation space.
