## 2. Case identified

A public EASA Safety Publications Tool record identifies a specific FAA AMOC associated with **US AD 91-12-10**, concerning wing spar attachment modification for Textron Aviation Inc. Super King Air aircraft. The record states that the FAA approved the AMOC on **23 February 2018** and identifies the AMOC reference as **7K0-18-00734**. The same record identifies the affected approval holder/type-designation context and provides the FAA AMOC attachment reference. citeturn1search2

The FAA's governing material confirms that an AMOC is an alternative method for complying with a specific AD and that an AMOC case can be bounded by the AD, approval holder, approval date, applicable make/model and affected AD provisions. FAA Order 8110.103B explicitly identifies these fields as information that may be publicly provided on request. citeturn0search13turn1search18

FAA AC 39-10 is the retained normative candidate and is identified by FAA as active, issued 2016-09-14, with the purpose of guiding persons seeking approval of an AMOC to an AD. citeturn0search1
