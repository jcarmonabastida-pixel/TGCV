## 1. Purpose

Perform a bounded public-record screening of the next retained documentary candidate after the LynxOS-178 case reached IT-G2 HOLD. The operation seeks one concrete, independently reconstructable industrial decision episode. It does not enter IT-G2 unless IT-G1 closes.

## 2. Documentary basis

EASA's Initial Airworthiness rules explicitly permit alternative means of compliance and require prior approval by the competent authority. For organisation-proposed AltMoCs, the organisation must provide a full description, including relevant manual/procedure revisions and an explanation of how compliance is achieved; the competent authority's approval is individual to the organisation. citeturn2search5turn3search2

EASA also publishes a list of AltMoCs notified by competent authorities. The Agency states that the list is based on information received from competent authorities, is informational, and may be incomplete or inaccurate. citeturn4view0

## 4. Decision

**IT-G1-E = INSUFFICIENT FOR FORMAL CASE ADMISSION.**

The public evidence establishes a strong **case class**, but not one sufficiently bounded public **case episode**. The EASA AltMoC notification list cannot by itself be treated as a complete case register because EASA expressly disclaims completeness and official case-level authority for the list. citeturn4view0

No retrospective reconstruction from later certification or production outcomes is permitted to close the missing identity fields.
