## 1. Candidate identified

AESA publishes a concrete dated resolution: **Resolución de 5 de marzo de 2018 de la Dirección de la Agencia Estatal de Seguridad Aérea**, approving an alternative means of compliance to ORO.FC.230(b) concerning operator proficiency checks in single-pilot helicopters for certain commercial air transport operations. citeturn6search0turn6search18

This is substantially stronger than a purely generic regulatory example: the decision has an identifiable authority, date, regulatory reference and bounded operational subject. The underlying regulation also explicitly permits alternative means of compliance and establishes prior approval by the competent authority. citeturn5search3turn5search6

## 3. Critical finding

The resolution is a **real regulatory decision**, but it is not equivalent to a concrete organisation-level industrial case. It establishes an alternative compliance method at the authority level for a defined operational context rather than exposing an individual operator's decision episode with a uniquely bounded organisational state and decision unit.

Using the resolution as if it were an operator case would therefore collapse two different analytical levels and would violate the frozen IT-G1 requirement for a concrete, reproducible case.

## 4. Decision

**IT-G1-C = NOT SUFFICIENT FOR FORMAL CASE ADMISSION.**

The candidate remains useful evidence that the documentary ecosystem contains real alternative-compliance decisions, but it does not close the industrial case-identifiability gate.

No inference is made about which operator may have used the approved means, and no outcome-dependent reconstruction is permitted.
