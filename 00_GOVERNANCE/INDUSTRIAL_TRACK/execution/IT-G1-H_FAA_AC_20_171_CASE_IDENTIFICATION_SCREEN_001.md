## 2. Documentary basis

FAA AC 20-171 is active and was issued on 2011-01-19. It describes an acceptable means for an applicant to seek FAA approval of an alternative to RTCA/DO-178B, and states that the alternative should be evaluated within a certification project for airborne systems/equipment seeking FAA approval. citeturn0search0turn0search20

The AC requires early communication with FAA, project-specific agreement before implementation, and a formalized certification project. It describes FAA issue papers as a vehicle for negotiating and documenting significant certification issues. citeturn1search3

## 4. Negative finding and corroborating evidence

The AC itself establishes that the mechanism is project-specific rather than a generic approval that can be assigned to a product merely because the product later received certification. The case therefore requires an identifiable applicant/project and a documented alternative decision. citeturn1search3turn0search20

A 2024 EASA certification review contains a specific retrospective statement that no applicant had been able to successfully use AC 20-171. This is corroborative evidence about the practical history of the mechanism, but it is not itself a substitute for a case-level decision record. citeturn1search30

Accordingly, the screening does not infer a case from later certification outcomes, generic references to alternative methods, or the existence of the AC.
