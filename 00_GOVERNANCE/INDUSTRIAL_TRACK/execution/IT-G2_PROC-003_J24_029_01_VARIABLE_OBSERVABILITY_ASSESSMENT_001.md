## 4. Critical finding

The candidate's apparent richness does not justify upgrading G2 merely because a pliego and technical valuation report are publicly listed. The decisive question is whether the underlying contents can support an independent reconstruction of:

1. decision-time system/context;
2. candidate technical alternatives/configurations;
3. enabling conditions for those alternatives;
4. limiting constraints;
5. the mapping between those conditions and the evaluated alternatives.

That closure is not demonstrated within the evidence actually established for this assessment.

The presence of three lots is an additional methodological constraint: no cross-lot aggregation is permitted unless a common state/context and transformation representation is independently justified.

## 5. Non-retroactivity

Awarded suppliers, offer counts, subsequent formalisation or later contract execution are not used to infer the pre-decision accessible transformation space. The awarded solution is not treated as the complete alternative universe.
