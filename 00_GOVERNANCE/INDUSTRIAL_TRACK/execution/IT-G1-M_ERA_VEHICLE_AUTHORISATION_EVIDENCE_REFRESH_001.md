## Purpose

Perform one bounded evidence refresh after the earlier ERA case-identification attempt, specifically to determine whether public ERA material now exposes a complete historical application/decision episode sufficient to close IT-G1 without relying on inference or applicant-only OSS access.

## New public evidence checked

1. ERA's current vehicle-authorisation applicant page states that all decisions following an application are available in the One-Stop Shop (OSS), but the applicant can access only information relating to its own applications. After authorisation, vehicle-type information becomes publicly available through ERATV.  
2. ERA's ERATV documentation confirms that the public register concerns authorised vehicle types and supporting information, while the underlying application/decision record remains in OSS.  
3. ERA's public vocabulary for a Vehicle Type Authorisation Case identifies a case object and parameters such as area of use and configuration-dependent conditions/restrictions, but this does not expose a complete historical applicant-specific decision package for a selected episode.

The public evidence architecture itself confirms the key limitation: the authoritative application-level decision record is held in OSS with applicant-restricted access, while ERATV is a post-authorisation public register rather than a complete decision-episode archive.
