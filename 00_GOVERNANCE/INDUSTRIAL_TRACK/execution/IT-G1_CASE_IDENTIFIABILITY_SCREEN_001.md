## 1. Purpose

Evaluate whether the retained documentary material is sufficient to identify a **concrete industrial case** without importing outcome information or inventing missing operational facts.

IT-G1 is restricted to closure of:

1. concrete industrial decision context;
2. explicit system boundary;
3. bounded unit of analysis;
4. temporal frame.

No IT-G2, IT-G3, IT-G4 or IT-G5 determination is made here.

## 2. Selection rule

The retained candidate specifications were screened for their ability to support a concrete case definition from documentary material alone.

The strongest structural candidate is **IT-NOSD-006 — FAA AC 39-10 Alternative Methods of Compliance**, because its normative mechanism is explicitly tied to a concrete regulatory decision pathway: an Alternative Method of Compliance (AMOC) to an identified Airworthiness Directive (AD).

This provides a clean case-construction schema without requiring TGCV to define the alternatives retrospectively from an observed outcome.

## 3. Candidate case schema

**Provisional case class:** AMOC decision under an identified Airworthiness Directive.

A valid concrete case would require, at minimum:

- one uniquely identified Airworthiness Directive;
- one affected aircraft/product or bounded fleet context;
- one identifiable regulated/operator/maintenance decision context;
- the relevant decision date or bounded decision window;
- the applicable regulatory and guidance versions at that time;
- the decision unit at which the AMOC alternative is considered.

The documentary discovery currently establishes the normative pathway but does **not** establish any one real-world episode satisfying all of those fields.

## 5. Why this is not a failure of the documentary discovery

The discovery operation was authorized to identify normative/operational specifications, not to identify or acquire a partner-specific industrial episode. Its successful completion therefore does not imply that a concrete industrial case exists or is accessible.

Likewise, selecting the AMOC pathway does not authorize contacting a partner, acquiring proprietary evidence, executing an event-log analysis, or assessing utility.
