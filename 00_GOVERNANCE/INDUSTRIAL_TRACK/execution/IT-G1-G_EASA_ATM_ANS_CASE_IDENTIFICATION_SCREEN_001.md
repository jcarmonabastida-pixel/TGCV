## 2. Documentary basis

EASA's ATM/ANS rules expressly permit alternative means of compliance. A service provider wishing to use an AltMoC must provide the competent authority, before implementation, with a full description, relevant manual/procedure revisions and an assessment demonstrating compliance; implementation is subject to prior approval and notification. The competent authority must also operate a system for evaluating AltMoCs. citeturn0search0turn0search1

AESA's public material confirms that ATM/ANS providers are subject to approval and notification procedures and that AltMoC under ATM/ANS.OR.A.020 is an approval-relevant change. citeturn2search0turn2search3

## 4. Important negative finding

AESA publicly documents the existence of ATM/ANS AltMoC approval procedures and the requirement to submit documentation through official registration. It does not expose, in the public material recovered here, a complete provider-specific AltMoC decision record containing the fields required to freeze one reproducible case. citeturn2search0turn2search3

The EASA public AltMoC resource provides a list of AltMoCs notified by competent authorities, but the public page itself warns about information quality and does not by itself establish a complete case-level decision archive. citeturn0search3

The existence of the approval mechanism therefore cannot be substituted for identification of one concrete decision episode.
