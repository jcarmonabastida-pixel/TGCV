## 1. Purpose

Perform a bounded public-record screening for one concrete, independently reconstructable industrial decision episode under the retained Part-145 alternative-means-of-compliance specification.

## 2. Documentary basis

EASA's current Part-145 rules state that an organisation may propose an alternative means of compliance, must provide a full description before use, and may use it only after prior approval by the competent authority. EASA guidance further states that organisation-specific AltMoC approval is individual and restricted to the specific applicant. citeturn5search0turn5search14

The underlying framework also requires competent authorities to notify EASA of AltMoCs used by organisations under their oversight. citeturn5search0

EASA maintains a public Approval Data Library, including Part-145 approvals, but the public library does not expose a bounded, case-level register of organisation-specific 145.A.120 AltMoC approval episodes sufficient to identify one complete decision unit. citeturn5search5
