## 4. Critical methodological finding

This case demonstrates an important distinction: a detailed technical specification can make the **baseline admissibility boundary** highly observable while leaving the **candidate transformation space** underdetermined.

The following inference is therefore prohibited:

`PPT requirements + 3 bidders + qualitative evaluation report → complete T_acc`.

The competing technical offers are decision-relevant because the procedure evaluates qualitative criteria, but their full contents and the evidence-to-score mapping are not established as independently recoverable. The awarded solution cannot be used as a substitute for the unobserved alternative space.

## 5. Non-retroactivity
