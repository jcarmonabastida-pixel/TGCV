## 4. Critical gap

The candidate is stronger than a case whose public record contains only an award notice: it exposes a technical evaluation process and multiple decision documents. Nevertheless, G2 requires more than evidence that documents exist.

To close G2, the accessible evidence must permit an independent reconstruction of:

1. the decision-time system/context;
2. the relevant candidate transformations or technical alternatives;
3. conditions enabling those alternatives;
4. constraints limiting/excluding alternatives;
5. the relation between those conditions and the evaluated alternatives.

The present evidence boundary does not close all five components at sufficient field-level granularity without inference.
