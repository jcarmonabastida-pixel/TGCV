# TGCV — EXT-UPD-4.8 — Stage B Comparative Industrial Utility Test — Governance Decision v0.1

**Status:** GOVERNANCE DECISION — CLOSED / STAGE B DESIGN ROUTE OPENED  
**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Parent:** EXT-UPD-4.8 Scientific & Industrial Applicability Reassessment  
**Prerequisite:** Stage A IUT-A-01 PASS + Evidence→Claim impact assessment + propagation + consistency closure + governance-current-state machine verification PASS

## 1. Decision purpose

This decision opens a **design route** for Stage B of the TGCV Industrial Utility Test (IUT).

It does **not** authorize Stage B execution.

The purpose is to determine whether a controlled comparative test can establish whether TGCV provides differentiated decision-relevant analytical capability beyond a credible incumbent/baseline approach in the bounded industrial case established during Stage A.

## 2. Why Stage B is justified

Stage A established that a concrete industrial decision episode can be specified with:

- a bounded native state/context;
- a finite source-defined candidate-option universe;
- an independently frozen pre-decision information boundary;
- a credible incumbent baseline;
- a distinct TGCV-specific analytical output;
- a reproducible candidate case specification.

Stage A did **not** establish utility. Its result therefore justifies design work, not a positive utility conclusion.

The Stage-A impact assessment explicitly leaves industrial utility **UNPROVEN / OPEN** and does not upgrade causality, value, superiority, prediction, universal validity or complete `T_acc` operationalization.

## 3. Strategic question

Stage B addresses the following falsifiable question:

> **For a bounded industrial decision problem, does TGCV provide at least one reproducible decision-relevant analytical capability that the frozen incumbent/baseline does not provide, or provides materially less effectively, under the same information available at decision time?**

The null proposition is:

> **H0-IUT:** TGCV provides no differentiated decision-relevant capability beyond the frozen baseline under the controlled conditions.

The positive proposition is:

> **H1-IUT:** TGCV provides at least one reproducible decision-relevant capability beyond the frozen baseline under the controlled conditions.

## 4. Scope boundary

Stage B must remain bounded to the Stage-A industrial case family unless a new governance decision explicitly changes scope.

No third scientific domain is opened.

I-01 is not reopened as a scientific translation route.

Stage B is an **industrial utility comparison**, not a renewed attempt to prove complete transversal operational closure of `T_acc`.

## 5. Critical methodological test

The comparative design must directly test whether useful TGCV output can be obtained without exhaustive reconstruction of the complete accessible transformation space.

A bounded decision-relevant subset is permitted only under RF-01:

- inclusion universe frozen before outcome inspection;
- inclusion/exclusion rule explicit;
- rationale independent of observed outcome;
- timestamp/version recorded before outcome evaluation;
- provenance preserved.

If these conditions cannot be met, the case is INDETERMINATE and Stage B stops.

## 6. Baseline control

The incumbent/baseline must be frozen before comparative evaluation.

For IUT-A-01 the candidate baseline is the conventional fixed/linear process-plan decision representation documented in Stage A.

The Stage-B design audit must verify that the baseline is credible for the exact bounded decision episode and that its information inputs are comparable to TGCV's inputs.

No post-hoc baseline selection is permitted.

## 7. Information boundary

Both TGCV and baseline must receive only information available at the defined decision time.

Observed downstream performance, realised production outcomes, post-decision configuration, and any information unavailable when the decision was made must not be used to construct the comparative prediction/option-space output.

Outcome information may be retained separately for later assessment of decision consequences, but must not leak into the construction of the compared analytical outputs.

## 8. Candidate comparative outputs

The Stage-B design may evaluate, subject to pre-freezing:

- newly accessible native options detected;
- lost native options detected;
- option-space differences identified before outcome;
- structural/configuration conditions explaining those differences;
- decision-relevant lead time;
- missed-option rate;
- false-option rate;
- reproducibility;
- structural completeness within the frozen bounded option universe.

The final metric set must be frozen before comparative execution.

## 9. Differentiation requirement

A positive result requires more than different terminology or a different representation of the same information.

The claimed TGCV advantage must be:

1. reproducible;
2. decision-relevant;
3. attributable to the TGCV analytical construction rather than post-hoc interpretation;
4. unavailable from the frozen baseline under the same bounded information conditions, or materially less effectively obtained by it.

A merely richer description is IUT-1, not IUT-2.

## 10. Failure and falsification criteria

Stage B must return INDETERMINATE or NEGATIVE where applicable if:

- TGCV adds no differentiated output;
- the baseline reproduces the same output with equivalent information;
- apparent advantage depends on outcome leakage;
- option selection is outcome-aware;
- accessibility is defined from observed success;
- analyst-supplied discretization or thresholds are necessary without native justification;
- the comparison cannot be reproduced;
- the result is driven by post-hoc case selection;
- the alleged utility is merely terminological relabelling;
- the decision-relevant output cannot be connected to the actual bounded decision episode.

## 11. Result classification

Stage B should distinguish at least:

- **IUT-0:** no differentiated utility demonstrated;
- **IUT-1:** descriptive/explanatory utility only;
- **IUT-2:** differentiated decision-support utility demonstrated;
- **INDETERMINATE:** evidence insufficient or methodological boundary reached.

IUT-3 and IUT-4 remain outside the authorization boundary of this decision. Operational consequence and value linkage require later explicit gates.

## 12. Scientific interpretation constraints

Even if Stage B returns IUT-2, this does not establish:

- universal validity;
- transversal theoretical conformance;
- causal efficacy;
- predictive validity;
- financial value;
- superiority across contexts;
- complete `T_acc` operationalization.

Conversely, a negative result in one bounded case does not by itself falsify TGCV universally. It is evidence against industrial utility under the tested conditions.

This decision does not authorize:

- Stage B execution;
- industrial partner engagement;
- financial-value measurement;
- causal inference;
- operational deployment;
- new domain discovery;
- new I-01 constructive operationalization;
- modification of TGCV Core;
- modification of TR-130 or TR-131;
- modification of frozen TGCV definitions;
- external-asset refresh.

## 15. Decision

**DECISION: OPEN THE STAGE-B DESIGN ROUTE.**

The evidence is sufficient to justify a controlled comparative design because Stage A established a testable industrial decision episode, while the scientific and industrial impact assessment explicitly preserved utility as unproven.

The next gate is therefore methodological design, not execution.
