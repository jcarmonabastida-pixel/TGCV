# TR-132 — Executable Protocol v0.1

**Status:** CURRENT / OPERATIVE — DESIGN-ONLY  
**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Execution:** NOT AUTHORIZED  
**Predecessor:** TR-132 design v0.1 + Claim Identifiability Requirements Matrix v0.1

## 1. Purpose

Operationalize TR-132 as a falsifiable methodological test of the empirical identifiability of `T_acc`, without modifying the TGCV Core, existing claim wording, thresholds or falsification criteria.

The protocol tests the minimum identifiable representation required by each current claim before any new scientific or industrial empirical operation is attempted.

## 2. Test question

Given a bounded decision point and observable or independently reconstructable `S_t`, `C_t`, `L_t`, can a non-circular procedure identify:

1. a certified accessible candidate transformation (`L1`);
2. a reproducible bounded subset `T_acc,t^+` (`L2`);
3. a valid temporal change `ΔT_acc^+` under frozen criteria (`L3`); and/or
4. the complete `T_acc,t` (`L4`)?

The protocol does not assume that L4 is necessary for every current claim.

## 3. Preconditions

Execution may begin only after explicit governance authorization. Before authorization, the following must be frozen:

- unit of analysis and system boundary;
- decision time(s) and temporal horizon;
- candidate transformation universe;
- operational accessibility/admissibility predicate;
- material, setup, temporal and authorization conditions;
- evidence sufficiency rules;
- treatment of unknown, unavailable and unobserved conditions;
- identity rule for transformations;
- subset-selection rule for `T_acc^+`;
- comparison rule for `ΔT_acc^+`;
- outcome-blinding or outcome-independence controls;
- auditable result schema.

No execution is valid if any of these are chosen after inspecting the target result.

## 4. Four-object separation

The executor must keep distinct:

- `T_poss`: technically/physically possible transformations;
- `T_adm`: transformations admissible under independently specified rules;
- `T_acc`: transformations effectively accessible under decision-time conditions;
- `T_obs`: transformations actually realized/observed.

Invalid substitutions include:

- `T_obs = T_acc`;
- `T_obs = T_adm`;
- `τ ∉ T_obs ⇒ τ ∉ T_acc`;
- realized success as the sole evidence of accessibility.

## 5. Sequential test ladder

### Stage L1 — Certified candidate accessibility

Pre-register at least one candidate transformation `τ` before outcome assessment.

For each `τ`, reconstruct the decision-time conditions and independently verify the frozen accessibility predicate. Record positive, negative, unknown and unavailable conditions separately.

**L1 PASS:** at least one candidate is certified accessible and the certification is independent of realization/outcome.

**L1 FAIL:** no candidate can be certified accessible under the frozen predicate despite sufficient relevant information.

**L1 INCONCLUSIVE:** material information needed to adjudicate the predicate is unavailable or cannot be independently reconstructed.

L1 success alone does not establish the extent of `T_acc`.

### Stage L2 — Certified bounded subset

Pre-specify a candidate universe `U*` and a non-outcome-dependent subset-selection rule. Identify `T_acc^+ ⊆ U*` using the frozen predicate.

The subset must be:

- reproducible by an independent executor;
- bounded by an explicit inclusion rule;
- independent of downstream outcome/utility;
- comparable across repetitions or time points where relevant.

**L2 PASS:** a reproducible certified subset is obtained and its membership can be audited.

**L2 FAIL:** no non-circular bounded subset can be identified under the frozen rules.

**L2 INCONCLUSIVE:** subset construction depends on unresolved material conditions or inaccessible evidence.

A bounded subset must never be reported as exhaustive `T_acc`.

### Stage L3 — Pairwise temporal change

Use the same frozen candidate universe, predicate, evidence threshold and subset rule at `t` and `t+1`.

Compute the certified bounded representations `T_acc,t^+` and `T_acc,t+1^+`. Test whether their identity differs.

**L3 PASS:** `T_acc,t^+ ≠ T_acc,t+1^+` is independently established and the difference is not generated by methodological drift.

**L3 FAIL:** no valid difference can be established under frozen criteria.

**L3 INCONCLUSIVE:** apparent difference cannot be separated from changes in candidate universe, evidence availability, predicate interpretation or measurement procedure.

L3 PASS establishes only bounded `ΔT_acc^+`, not full-space `ΔT_acc`.

### Stage L4 — Full-space closure

Attempt exhaustive identification only after L1–L3 have established that a bounded representation is operationally viable.

L4 requires independent closure of:

- candidate universe completeness;
- all material accessibility conditions;
- decision-time state/context;
- admissibility and accessibility rules;
- evidence sufficiency;
- distinction between inaccessible and merely unobserved transformations.

**L4 PASS:** complete `T_acc` is independently reconstructable and auditable.

**L4 FAIL:** at least one material component of the full accessible set cannot be independently closed under the frozen protocol.

**L4 INCONCLUSIVE:** the available evidence cannot discriminate completeness from incompleteness without an unapproved assumption.

Failure at L4 does not invalidate an L1–L3 result.

## 7. Non-circularity gate

Execution is invalid if any of the following occurs:

1. candidate universe is narrowed after seeing the result;
2. accessibility predicate is changed after seeing the result;
3. evidence threshold is relaxed after an indeterminate/negative result;
4. accessibility is inferred from realization;
5. non-observation is classified as inaccessibility without independent evidence;
6. subset membership is selected using downstream outcome or utility;
7. the proxy is defined from the desired TGCV conclusion;
8. L1/L2/L3 success is represented as L4 without separate completeness evidence.

Any violation requires adjudication as **INVALID EXECUTION**, not reinterpretation.

## 8. Result schema

The auditable result must contain at minimum:

- protocol/version and execution identifier;
- frozen input manifest;
- unit/system/timepoint definition;
- candidate universe and selection rule;
- accessibility predicate and version;
- evidence inventory with provenance;
- per-candidate adjudication;
- `T_acc^+` membership where applicable;
- pairwise comparison where applicable;
- L1/L2/L3/L4 decision;
- non-circularity audit;
- deviations, unknowns and unavailable conditions;
- executor identity/environment where relevant;
- deterministic/reproducibility information where applicable;
- final scientific interpretation bounded to the achieved level.

## 9. Decision logic

Overall TR-132 classification is determined by the highest valid level reached without violating non-circularity:

- **PASS:** L4 passes.
- **BOUNDED PASS:** L1 and/or L2 and/or L3 passes sufficiently to support the pre-mapped bounded claim requirements, while L4 is not established.
- **FAIL:** the minimum level required for the target proposition cannot be achieved non-circularly.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** the minimum level cannot be adjudicated because material information is unavailable or unresolved without an unapproved assumption.

A protocol execution can therefore be procedurally successful while producing FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE scientifically.

## 10. Stop conditions

Stop immediately if:

- a mandatory frozen definition is unavailable;
- candidate identity cannot be reconstructed independently;
- accessibility depends on outcome realization;
- a required distinction collapses into observation or downstream outcome;
- methodological drift is detected between timepoints;
- evidence provenance cannot be audited;
- execution would require changing an existing claim, threshold or falsification rule.

No rescue execution is permitted after a stop condition.

## 11. Scientific boundaries

TR-132 does not test causality, prediction, value creation, industrial utility, superiority, universal generalization or Core ontology. Those remain separate governed questions.

A BOUNDED PASS is evidence for a bounded operational representation only. It does not upgrade C01–C16 automatically.
