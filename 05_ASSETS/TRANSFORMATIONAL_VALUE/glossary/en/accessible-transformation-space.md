# Accessible transformation space

An analytical representation of the set or structure of transformations accessible to a system under a specified state and relevant conditions. In the TGCV formulation, it is derived from the system state and the relevant conditions, constraints and relationships through an explicit accessibility criterion.

The accessibility criterion must be defined independently of the transformations that are realised and of downstream outcomes. Accordingly, (T_{acc}) describes which transformations are available under those conditions, not which ones will actually be performed.

The space should not be confused with the universe of candidate transformations, the transformations that are realised, the trajectory resulting from them, or subsequent outcomes and value. Its boundaries depend on how the system, candidate transformations and accessibility criterion are defined for the case under analysis. Not every case requires the prior enumeration of every conceivable transformation.

**Notation:** (T_{acc}).

**Key distinction:** an accessible transformation is not necessarily executed, and its execution does not necessarily create value.
