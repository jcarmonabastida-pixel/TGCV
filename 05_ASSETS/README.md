## Structural rule

One external asset = one canonical identity = one canonical location.

Asset identity is determined by the stable `TGCV-EXT-*` identifier, not by historical filenames or former directories.

## Source and history boundaries

Scientific source material remains in `01_SCIENTIFIC_CORE/`. External-science preparation remains in `02_EXTERNAL_SCIENCE/`. Impact/transfer analysis remains in `04_IMPACT_TRANSFER/`. Governance and canonical state remain in `00_GOVERNANCE/`.

Historical or preparatory copies outside this surface are not competing current assets and must not be silently promoted or overwritten.
