## Vision

TGCV proposes that an important but insufficiently isolated object of analysis in adaptive, evolving and generative systems is not merely how a system changes state, but how its **structure of accessible transformations changes over time**.

A system may undergo changes that alter not only its present configuration, but the structure of transformations that can subsequently be realised from that configuration under relevant conditions.

## Problem

Many research traditions analyse fragments of this territory: state transitions, affordances, capabilities, adaptation, reconfiguration, evolvability, learning, path dependence, reachable sets, dynamic capabilities and related constructs.

The research question is whether a transversal analytical structure can make explicit the changing organisation of a system's future transformations and connect such structural change to subsequent trajectories.
