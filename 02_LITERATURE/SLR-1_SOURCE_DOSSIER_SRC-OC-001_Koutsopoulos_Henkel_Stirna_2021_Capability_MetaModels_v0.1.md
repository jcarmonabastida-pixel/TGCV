# SLR-1 Source Dossier — SRC-OC-001

**Source:** Georgios Koutsopoulos, Martin Henkel & Janis Stirna (2021), *An analysis of capability meta-models for expressing dynamic business transformation*, Software and Systems Modeling 20, 147–174. DOI `10.1007/s10270-020-00843-0`. citeturn0search4turn0search7

**Classification:** **AC2 — STRONG PRIOR ART / AC3 NOT ESTABLISHED**

## Relevance

This paper systematically analyses capability meta-models for representing dynamic business transformation. It explicitly studies how capability adaptability and change are represented, including capability realization, adjustment/adaptation or transformation, and impacts on related capabilities. citeturn0search4

## TGCV mapping

- `S`: enterprise/capability system and its contextual environment.
- capability: a system-level capacity enabling achievement of a goal in a context.
- change mechanism: adaptation/transformation of capability realization elements.
- future operational possibilities: indirectly represented through capability adaptability and changed capability architecture.
- relations: dependencies/interrelationships among capabilities are explicitly considered.
- value/outcome: capabilities are linked to business goals and value delivery.

## AC2 assessment

AC2 is **STRONG** because the source provides a structured meta-model perspective on changing capabilities and their relations, and explicitly analyses the concepts used to represent capability change in existing approaches. It therefore absorbs any claim that organizational capability change, capability adaptation/transformation, and propagation of change through capability relationships are themselves novel analytical objects. citeturn0search4

## AC3 assessment

AC3 is **NOT ESTABLISHED**. The source does not provide the complete TGCV architecture:

1. no domain-independent transformation object `τ`;
2. no general accessibility predicate `P_τ(S,C,L)`;
3. no explicit `T_acc` as the set of all currently accessible transformations;
4. no central `ΔT_acc` object as the primary phenomenon;
5. no general chain `ΔT_acc → ΔReach → ΔTrajectory → Outcome → Value` independent of the organizational-capability domain.

The capability architecture is therefore a strong domain-specific structural antecedent, not a demonstrated Level-3 architectural absorption.

## Decision

- AC2: **STRONG / CONFIRMED PRIOR ART**.
- AC3: **NOT ESTABLISHED**.
- Broad novelty claim around capability change/adaptation/transformation: **ABSORBED**.
- TGCV Core: **UNCHANGED**.
