## 4. Architectural absorption test

### Level-3 requirement

For AC3, a source or convergent body of evidence must establish, without substantive theoretical addition:

1. a domain-independent system/state representation comparable to `S`;
2. an explicit object corresponding to currently accessible transformations `T_acc` rather than merely possible states, outputs, actions or capabilities;
3. an explicit accessibility condition comparable to `P_τ(S,C,L)`;
4. an explicit representation of change in that transformation-accessibility object, i.e. `ΔT_acc`;
5. a demonstrated relation from that change to reachability/trajectory or equivalent downstream structure;
6. a mechanism/interactions layer that explains how the accessible transformation structure changes, without requiring the mechanism to become a Core primitive;
7. no substantive analytical addition remaining for TGCV in the architecture itself.

### Result

The screened literature establishes strong and sometimes near-direct antecedents for individual components and relations, but the cross-family comparison does **not** establish all seven requirements in one architecture or through a convergent body of Level-2 evidence that leaves no substantive TGCV analytical addition.

In particular, the persistent remainder is the explicit, domain-independent treatment of **accessible transformations themselves** as the central analytical object, together with **change in that object (`ΔT_acc`)** as the organizing variable linking mechanism/state change to downstream reachability and trajectory.

## 5. Decision

**AC3 — ARCHITECTURAL ABSORPTION: NOT ESTABLISHED.**

**Bounded current finding:** `NO_FULL_ABSORPTION_IDENTIFIED`.

The evidence does establish substantial prior art for the broader phenomena:

- changing action sets;
- changing adaptation spaces;
- capability-space evolution;
- evolving state/possibility spaces;
- reachability and trajectory dependence;
- adjacent-possible expansion;
- combinatorial innovation;
- generative/expressive ranges.

Therefore TGCV must **not** claim novelty for these broad phenomena individually.

The remaining candidate contribution is the specific transversal analytical construction centered on `T_acc` and `ΔT_acc`, with explicit accessibility semantics and the downstream chain to reachability/trajectory/outcome/value.
