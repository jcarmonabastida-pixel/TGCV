# SLR-1 Source Dossier — SRC-OC-002

**Source:** Dagnino, G.B. & Mariani, M.M. (2007), *Dynamic gap bridging and realized gap set development: The strategic role of the firm in the coevolution of capability space and opportunity space*, in *Innovation, Industrial Dynamics and Structural Transformation*, Springer, pp. 321–341, DOI `10.1007/978-3-540-49465-2_17`. citeturn1search1turn1search0

**Classification:** **AC2 — VERY STRONG / NEAR-DIRECT; AC3 NOT ESTABLISHED**

## Why this source matters

This is a much stronger organizational-capability antecedent than a generic dynamic-capabilities treatment. The source explicitly treats **Capability Space (CS)** as a space whose elements are capabilities, models its coevolution with an Opportunity Space, and analyses expansion/contraction and evolution of the spaces. It also distinguishes potential versus realized capability/opportunity gap sets and explicitly models capability sets as conditions for seizing opportunities. citeturn1search1turn1search0

The source therefore directly challenges the proposition that an evolving organizational **space/set of capabilities that conditions future possibilities** is novel to TGCV.

## TGCV mapping

- `S`: firm/organizational system.
- capability space `CS`: explicit space of organizational capabilities.
- opportunity space `OS`: external/strategic possibility space.
- capability set: subset of capabilities potentially or actually deployed to bridge a strategy gap.
- transformation/change: capability identification, predisposition, deployment and evolution.
- future possibilities: opportunities that can be seized depend on available/assembled capability sets.
- relations: capabilities interact within the capability space and influence which opportunities can be sensed/seized.
- temporal change: CS and OS coevolve; capability-space structure changes over time.
- outcome/value: strategy-gap closure and innovation/execution consequences.

## AC2 assessment

AC2 is **VERY STRONG / NEAR-DIRECT** because the source provides all of the following:

1. an explicit capability space;
2. an explicit capability set within that space;
3. dynamic evolution of the capability space;
4. expansion/contraction and coevolution of capability and opportunity spaces;
5. distinction between potential and realized capability sets;
6. explicit relational interaction among capabilities;
7. capability sets functioning as necessary/sufficient conditions for seizing particular opportunities. citeturn1search1

This substantially absorbs any organizational-domain claim that merely introducing a changing capability space/set that conditions future opportunities constitutes the TGCV novelty.

## AC3 assessment

AC3 is **NOT ESTABLISHED**.

Despite its structural proximity, the source remains an organizational-strategy construction and does not establish:

1. a domain-independent transformation object `τ`;
2. a general accessibility predicate `P_τ(S,C,L)`;
3. `T_acc` defined as the set of accessible transformations rather than capabilities/opportunities;
4. `ΔT_acc` as the central cross-domain analytical object;
5. a general mechanism-independent chain `ΔT_acc → ΔReach → ΔTrajectory → Outcome → Value`.

The source therefore represents a serious Level-2 challenge but not Level-3 architectural absorption.
