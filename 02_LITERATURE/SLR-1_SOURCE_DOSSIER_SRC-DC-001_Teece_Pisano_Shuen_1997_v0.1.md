## Retrieved evidence

The source defines dynamic capabilities as the firm's ability to **integrate, build, and reconfigure internal and external competences** to address rapidly changing environments. It further connects dynamic capabilities with achieving new and innovative forms of competitive advantage under path dependencies and market positions. citeturn0search25

## Structural comparison

This source is highly relevant because it explicitly concerns mechanisms that alter organizational configurations and future strategic possibilities. However, the retrieved evidence does **not** establish the full TGCV architecture.

In particular, it does not yet establish an explicit object corresponding to:

`T_acc = {τ | P_τ(S,C,L)=1}`

nor an explicit analytical comparison:

`T_acc,t ≄ T_acc,t+1`.

The source instead focuses on dynamic capabilities as organizational abilities/processes that reconfigure competences and on the resulting competitive advantage. Its central analytical object is therefore not demonstrably the set of transformations accessible to a system.

## Absorption assessment

**AC1 candidate.** Strong construct-level proximity exists around change, reconfiguration, capabilities, path dependence, and subsequent advantage. On the present evidence, however, this is not structural equivalence or architectural absorption.

A deeper review should inspect whether the dynamic-capabilities literature contains an explicit formalization of a changing possibility/transformation space, rather than merely a capability to change resources/configurations.
