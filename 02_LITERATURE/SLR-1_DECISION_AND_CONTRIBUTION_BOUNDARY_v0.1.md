## 1. Decision

After completion of the predefined SLR-1 families, the global architectural absorption matrix, and the direct comparative stress test against the strongest near-direct AC2 sources, the current bounded decision is:

**AC3 — ARCHITECTURAL ABSORPTION: NOT ESTABLISHED.**

**Bounded current finding:** `NO_FULL_ABSORPTION_IDENTIFIED`.

This is a falsification result within the documented search boundary. It is **not** a proof of universal originality and must not be stated as one.

## 2. What has been absorbed

The SLR-1 evidence establishes that TGCV cannot claim novelty for the broad phenomena individually. Prior art already establishes, in different domains and formalisms:

- changing action sets;
- changing adaptation spaces;
- evolving capability and opportunity spaces;
- evolving state, viability and reachability spaces;
- path-dependent changes in future possibility structures;
- adjacent-possible expansion;
- combinatorial innovation;
- generative transformation pipelines;
- expressive/generative ranges that change with system parameters.

Accordingly, these phenomena are treated as **absorbed prior art**.

## 3. Direct stress-test result

The strongest near-direct comparisons do not collapse into a single equivalent TGCV construction.

### 3.1 Self-adaptive adaptation spaces

Gheibi & Weyns define an adaptation space as the set of adaptation options a self-adaptive system can select at a given time, and study drift in that space, including disappearance of options and emergence of new options. This is a very strong structural antecedent for a time-varying accessible-option space. citeturn0search24turn0academia25

Residual distinction: the object is an adaptation-option space within self-adaptation, not a domain-independent space of transformations of an arbitrary system; the construction does not establish TGCV's general accessibility predicate `P_τ(S,C,L)` nor make `ΔT_acc` the transversal organizing variable across domains.

### 3.2 Capability and opportunity spaces

Dagnino & Mariani explicitly model the coevolution of capability space and opportunity space, including potential and realized gap sets and expansion/contraction processes. citeturn1search0turn1search1

Residual distinction: the spaces concern capabilities and opportunities in organizational strategy rather than a general accessible-transformation object. The construction does not supply the TGCV transformation-level accessibility semantics or the central `ΔT_acc` relation.

### 3.3 Possibility spaces

Ross et al. frame complex systems through constraints and a system's possibility space of available outcomes. citeturn1search7turn1search8

Residual distinction: the primitive is a possibility/outcome space rather than the set of transformations currently accessible from the system, and therefore it does not establish `T_acc` or `ΔT_acc` in the TGCV sense.

### 3.4 Reconfigurable transition systems

Reconfigurable transition-system work provides strong prior art for dynamic transition structures whose accessibility relation, nodes or labels can change during execution. citeturn1search12

Residual distinction: the formalism is centered on reconfigurable transition/state structures; it does not establish the domain-independent analytical separation of an accessible transformation object and its change as the primary cross-domain explanatory variable.

## 4. Residual TGCV contribution candidate

The contribution that survives the stress test is therefore **not** the claim that systems can have changing possibilities, actions, capabilities, states or reachability. Those are established.

The residual candidate is the following analytical construction:

`Core_ontological = S`

`T_acc = F(S,C,L)`

`τ ∈ T_acc iff P_τ(S,C,L)=1`

`T_acc,t ≄ T_acc,t+1`

`ΔT_acc → ΔReach → ΔTrajectory → Outcome → Value`

with `I` retained as an explanatory mechanism rather than a Core primitive.

The differentiated claim is therefore a **transversal analytical re-description** in which:

1. transformations are the explicit object whose accessibility is analyzed;
2. accessibility is represented by an explicit predicate rather than inferred from observed execution;
3. change in accessibility, `ΔT_acc`, is itself the central comparative object;
4. the same object is intended to bridge mechanism/state change with downstream reachability and trajectory;
5. the construction is intended to remain domain-independent rather than being tied to adaptation, organizational capability, state reachability, generativity, or another specific domain construct.

## 5. Strength of the contribution claim

At the present stage, the contribution should be stated in **three levels**, not as a single undifferentiated novelty claim.

### Level A — Defensible now

TGCV provides a **distinct analytical formulation and vocabulary** that explicitly separates:

`system/state` → `accessible transformations` → `change in accessible transformations` → `reachability` → `trajectory`.

The SLR-1 stress test did not identify a prior framework that absorbs this exact transversal formulation without substantive analytical remainder.

### Level B — Plausible but requiring further comparative demonstration

TGCV may provide a **cross-domain analytical layer** that can translate heterogeneous constructs such as adaptation spaces, action sets, capability spaces, possibility spaces and transition structures into a common representation based on accessible transformations and their change.

This should be presented as a research contribution to be demonstrated, not as an already established fact.

### Level C — Not established

The following claims are **not authorized** by SLR-1:

- universal novelty of `T_acc`;
- universal novelty of `ΔT_acc`;
- absence of equivalent constructions in all literature;
- empirical causal effect of `T_acc` on value;
- predictive superiority of T_acc-based representations;
- proof that TGCV as a whole is a new theory.

## 6. Scientific consequence

SLR-1 therefore supports a **bounded originality position**:

> Existing literature strongly establishes many domain-specific and partially formalized constructions in which the set of future options, actions, capabilities, states, or reachable outcomes changes. The present review has not identified, at its documented bounded depth, a single or convergent Level-2 architecture that fully absorbs a domain-independent analytical construction centered on the accessibility of transformations themselves and on `ΔT_acc` as the organizing change variable connecting system/mechanism dynamics with reachability and trajectory.

This statement is the maximum originality claim currently supported.
