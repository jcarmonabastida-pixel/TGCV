## 3. Final adjacent source screened

### SRC-BSTAR-001 — B* Expansion Technique

Mayorquín Posadas & Vega (2026), *Thinking Is Not Enough: The B* Expansion Technique for Enhancing Autonomous LLM Agents*.

The source explicitly treats an evolving action space as a first-class object: an initial action basis `B`, generated actions `A*`, and an expanded space `B* = B ∪ A*`. It studies how action-space expansion affects subsequent agent/environment interaction and performance.

Classification:

**AC2 — STRONG STRUCTURAL ANTECEDENT**  
**AC3 — NOT ESTABLISHED**

The source absorbs another broad novelty claim: an action/operation repertoire can itself be explicitly constructed, expanded and treated as an evolving object with downstream performance consequences.

It does not establish the full TGCV architecture because the construction remains agent/action-specific, lacks a transversal predicate over arbitrary transformations, does not separate a general explanatory mechanism from the accessible transformation space, and does not formulate the complete chain:

`mechanism → ΔT_acc → ΔReach → ΔTrajectory → Outcome → Value`.

The detailed source dossier is:
