## Analytical construction found

The framework defines generativity as a pipeline of successive **data transformations**, where transformations generate, transform, expand, augment, reduce or prune information. It explicitly shifts attention from isolated procedural-content outputs to the architecture of generative pipelines and identifies a design space of generative pipelines. citeturn1search6turn1search34

This is relevant to TGCV because it treats transformations themselves as first-class analytical units and studies how compositions of transformations construct or modify a generative space.

## Absorption classification

**AC2 — STRONG / STRUCTURAL ANTECEDENT**  
**AC3 — NOT ESTABLISHED**

The source materially absorbs the broad claim that generative systems can be analyzed through explicit transformation units and their composition into a space-producing pipeline. It also weakens any claim that simply making transformations explicit is novel.

However, the framework is concerned with the construction and analysis of generative pipelines, not with a domain-independent analytical object representing the **currently accessible transformations of a system and the change in that accessibility structure**. It does not establish TGCV's `T_acc = F(S,C,L)` formulation or central `ΔT_acc` relation to reachability, trajectory, outcome and value.
