# TGCV — Cross-Domain Architecture Closure / Contribution Non-Redundancy Gate v0.1

**Status:** RECONSTRUCTED / WORKING  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Purpose:** provisional architectural closure after the completed SLR-1 and contribution gates.

## 1. Purpose

This gate consolidates the preceding analytical tests and asks a narrower question:

> After prior-art absorption, formalization, cross-domain reconstruction, information-sufficiency, dynamic, reachability/trajectory and outcome/value analysis, does TGCV retain a substantive analytical remainder that is coherent, non-redundant and falsifiable?

This is **not** a proof of universal originality and does not convert the current candidate into an established theory.

## 2. Inputs being consolidated

The closure depends on the following accepted results:

1. SLR-1 global architectural absorption: AC3 not established; `NO_FULL_ABSORPTION_IDENTIFIED` at bounded depth.
2. Contribution Specification Gate: bounded candidate defined; `ΔT_acc` identified as the primary differentiator.
3. Contribution Formalization Gate: minimal formal candidate established.
4. Cross-Domain Reconstruction / Non-Redundancy Gate: bounded reconstruction with substantive remainder.
5. ΔT_acc Information Sufficiency / State Reduction Gate: general state-reduction identity rejected.
6. Dynamic ΔT_acc Test: temporal distinction formally established; empirical dynamic validation remains open.
7. Reachability / Trajectory Sufficiency Gate: `T_acc`, `Reach` and `Trajectory` analytically distinguished.
8. Value-Link / Outcome Sufficiency Gate: downstream outcome/value layer specified without definitional leakage.

## 3. Canonical architecture under closure

The candidate architecture is:

`Core_ontological = S`

with:

`T_acc,t = {τ ∈ U_τ | P_τ(S_t,C_t,L)=1}`

and:

`ΔT_acc(t,t+1) = T_acc,t+1 ≄ T_acc,t`.

The temporal downstream architecture is:

`mechanism → (S_t,C_t) → (S_t+1,C_t+1) → ΔT_acc → ΔReach → ΔTrajectory → Outcome → Value`.

The arrows express conditional analytical dependency/constraint, not universal causality.

`I` remains an explanatory mechanism and is not a Core primitive.

## 4. Prior-art absorption boundary

The following are explicitly absorbed and therefore are **not** standalone TGCV novelty claims:

- transformation rules and transformation applicability;
- action sets and changing action sets;
- adaptation spaces and adaptation-space drift;
- capability spaces and opportunity spaces;
- evolving state spaces;
- reachability and viability methods;
- path dependence and trajectory structure;
- adjacent possible and combinatorial innovation;
- generative/expressive ranges and generative pipelines;
- the generic proposition that changes in available possibilities can affect future states or outcomes.

TGCV cannot claim these concepts individually as new.

## 5. Residual candidate

The residual candidate is narrower:

> A transversal analytical layer that explicitly represents the set of transformations accessible to a system under specified present conditions, represents its change over time as `ΔT_acc`, and uses that change as an organizing intermediate between mechanism/state change and changes in reachable futures, trajectories, outcomes and contextually evaluated value.

The candidate therefore has four distinguishable layers:

### Layer A — Accessible transformations

`T_acc` represents alternatives that are presently accessible under an independently specified accessibility predicate.

### Layer B — Change in accessible transformations

`ΔT_acc` represents expansion, contraction, reconfiguration or substitution in that accessible transformation space.

### Layer C — Downstream structural consequences

`ΔT_acc` can constrain or modify `Reach` and `Trajectory` without being identical to either.

### Layer D — Realized consequences and evaluation

Selected trajectories produce Outcomes, which can then be evaluated under an explicit Value function.

## 6. Non-redundancy test

### NR-C1 — Not merely state change

A state transition does not by itself identify all accessible alternatives. Therefore `ΔS` is not accepted as a general substitute for `ΔT_acc`.

**Result: PASS — bounded analytical argument.**

### NR-C2 — Not merely action-set change

Changing action sets are strong prior-art analogues. In some domains they instantiate a local equivalent of `T_acc`. Therefore local redundancy exists.

However, the action-set construct does not establish a single domain-independent architecture covering transformations, capabilities, possibilities and state-space alternatives.

**Result: PASS WITH LOCAL REDUNDANCY EXPLICITLY ACKNOWLEDGED.**

### NR-C3 — Not merely capability/possibility change

Capability spaces, opportunity spaces and possibility spaces strongly antecede the idea that future alternatives change. They do not establish, at the bounded review depth, the same general transformation-level accessibility predicate and central `ΔT_acc` architecture.

**Result: PASS — substantive remainder retained.**

### NR-C4 — Not merely reachability

Reachability can encode consequences of available transitions, but `Reach` and `T_acc` answer different questions: what can be done now versus what futures can be reached through sequences.

**Result: PASS.**

### NR-C5 — Not merely outcome/value

Outcome and Value occur downstream and cannot safely define present accessibility. Therefore the value layer does not absorb `T_acc` or `ΔT_acc`.

**Result: PASS.**

### NR-C6 — Not an isolated vocabulary claim

The candidate is not supported merely by introducing a new label. Its remainder consists of an explicit relational architecture and distinctions that survive comparison with multiple native constructs.

**Result: PASS — bounded.**

## 7. Cross-domain closure

The five reconstructed domains produce different native instantiations:

| Domain | Native analogue | Local redundancy | Residual TGCV distinction |
|---|---|---:|---|
| MDE / transformation systems | applicable transformation rules | High | transversal abstraction across domains |
| Self-adaptive systems | adaptation space | Very high | transformation-level generalization beyond adaptation |
| Organizational capabilities | capability/opportunity space | Medium | explicit transformation-accessibility layer |
| State-space / reachability | transitions and reachable futures | Medium-high | separation of accessibility, reachability and trajectory |
| Generativity / adjacent possible | possibility/generative space | Medium-high | distinction between possibilities and accessible transformations |

The closure therefore does **not** claim that every domain lacks an equivalent construct. It claims that the screened evidence does not establish one common architecture that already performs the full TGCV analytical role.

## 8. Strongest possible falsification

The current candidate should be rejected or materially reformulated if subsequent controlled work establishes any of the following:

**F-AC1. Full architectural absorption:** a prior framework already contains the same domain-independent `T_acc`, accessibility predicate, central `ΔT_acc`, reachability/trajectory link and outcome/value architecture, with no substantive TGCV remainder.

**F-AC2. State sufficiency:** once system state and correct native transition semantics are fully represented, `T_acc` and `ΔT_acc` add no analytically relevant distinction for the research question.

**F-AC3. Dynamic collapse:** temporal change in accessibility is completely and losslessly equivalent to an already established native variable in all relevant domains.

**F-AC4. Cross-domain failure:** the formal mapping cannot be reconstructed without importing TGCV assumptions that are not supported by the target domain.

**F-AC5. Downstream collapse:** Reach/Trajectory/Outcome/Value provide no analytically distinct role once the native construct is correctly represented.

**F-AC6. Non-falsifiability:** the candidate cannot be instantiated with independently specified candidates, accessibility conditions and observable downstream consequences.

## 10. Gate decision

**PASS — BOUNDED CROSS-DOMAIN ARCHITECTURAL CLOSURE WITH SUBSTANTIVE NON-REDUNDANCY REMAINDER.**

At the current bounded evidence level, TGCV retains a coherent analytical remainder after systematic prior-art absorption.

That remainder is sufficiently distinct to justify continued research, but not sufficiently established to justify the stronger claim that TGCV is already a universally novel theory.

## 11. Scientific status after closure

### Defensible now

- `S` can remain the ontological Core candidate.
- `T_acc` can remain the central derived analytical object.
- `ΔT_acc` can be treated as the primary differentiated contribution candidate.
- `Reach` and `Trajectory` can be treated as analytically downstream and distinct.
- Outcome and Value can be represented downstream without contaminating accessibility.
- The framework is sufficiently specified to support further falsifiable empirical and cross-domain research.

### Plausible but requiring further validation

- TGCV provides a genuinely transversal translation layer across heterogeneous domains.
- `ΔT_acc` is practically useful as an organizing variable for explaining downstream trajectory differences.
- The architecture can support a reproducible methodology of value construction.

### Not established

- universal originality;
- universal ontological irreducibility of `T_acc`;
- causal sufficiency;
- positive value creation from accessibility expansion;
- predictive superiority;
- universal empirical validity;
- complete absence of an equivalent prior architecture.

## 12. Consequences for the TGCV program

The program should now move from **architectural falsification** to **architectural development and empirical validation**.

The scientific question is no longer primarily “can the candidate survive prior-art absorption?” at the current bounded level. It becomes:

> Can the residual architecture be operationalized, measured and falsified in heterogeneous real systems, and can its downstream explanatory/value role be demonstrated without circularity or post-hoc reinterpretation?

This does not reopen SLR-1. It converts the bounded SLR-1 result into a stable prior-art boundary for subsequent work.
