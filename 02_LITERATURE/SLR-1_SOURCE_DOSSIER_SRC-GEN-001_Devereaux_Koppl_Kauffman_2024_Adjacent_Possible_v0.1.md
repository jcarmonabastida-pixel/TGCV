# SLR-1 Source Dossier — SRC-GEN-001

**Source:** Abigail Devereaux, Roger Koppl & Stuart Kauffman (2024), *Creative evolution in economics*, Journal of Evolutionary Economics 34, 489–514. DOI `10.1007/s00191-024-00855-9`.

## Family

**Generativity and combinatorial possibility approaches**  
**Status:** INCLUDED — substantive comparison

## Analytical construction found

The paper develops a representation of creative evolution based on the theory of the adjacent possible. It explicitly defines a system-level possible set `Π_t` and system-level adjacent possible `A_t`, with `A_t` changing through time as the system evolves. It states that the adjacent possible represents the creation of new possibilities and models creative evolution as an open-ended sequence `{…, A_{t-1}, A_t, A_{t+1}, …}`. citeturn1search0

The source therefore provides unusually strong prior art for a changing possibility structure whose evolution is itself analytically represented.

## Absorption classification

**AC2 — VERY STRONG / NEAR-DIRECT STRUCTURAL ANTECEDENT**  
**AC3 — NOT ESTABLISHED**

The source materially challenges any claim that TGCV is the first framework to explicitly represent a changing future-possibility structure and connect its evolution to subsequent trajectories. It is especially important because it provides a cross-disciplinary formulation rather than a single technical application.

However, the analytical primitive remains the adjacent possible/possible-state structure rather than a domain-independent set of **accessible transformations**. The paper does not formulate a generic transformation predicate `P_τ(S,C,L)` nor isolate `ΔT_acc` as the transversal object connecting mechanism, reachability, trajectory, outcome and value.

## Decision

Retain as a major prior-art boundary for the generativity/combinatorial family. It establishes strong AC2 prior art but does not establish AC3 architectural absorption.
