## Decision

The organizational-capability family provides a **strong and near-direct Level-2 antecedent** for dynamic spaces, potential-versus-realized distinctions and changes in the set of capabilities conditioning future opportunities. This substantially absorbs the corresponding domain-specific novelty claim.

However, no screened source establishes the complete Level-3 TGCV architecture. In particular, the literature does not establish the domain-independent analytical object `T_acc` as the set of currently accessible transformations, nor `ΔT_acc` as the central phenomenon connecting mechanism-induced system change to reachability, trajectory, outcome and value.

**Bounded finding:** `NO_FULL_ABSORPTION_IDENTIFIED`.

This is not a universal novelty proof; it is the result of the bounded search performed under the reconstructed SLR-1 protocol.
