# TI-001 Decision Acquisition Protocol 001

**Status:** FROZEN — SCIENTIFIC EXECUTION AUTHORIZED, NOT STARTED

## 1. Purpose

This protocol defines how the transformation choice is obtained for each TI-001 record. It is part of the frozen execution package and does not alter the primary estimand.

## 2. Decision interface

For each instance, the decision mechanism receives only the information permitted by the assigned condition:

- current state;
- currently executable transformations;
- task information;
- condition-specific information specified by the frozen fixture.

Treatment may additionally receive the structured future transformation-space signal specified in the fixture.

The decision mechanism must return exactly one `selected_transformation` from the currently executable transformations.

## 3. Information boundary

Before the decision is recorded, the mechanism must not receive, access, infer from execution output, or query:

- successor state;
- successor accessibility;
- post-decision observations;
- reward, utility, payoff, value, or outcome;
- any preferred-action label generated by the executor.

The decision is therefore temporally prior to successor revelation.

## 4. Decision record

Each decision must contain:

- `pair_id`
- `instance_id`
- `condition`
- `selected_transformation`
- `decision_timestamp`

The decision record must not contain successor information.

## 5. Invalid decision conditions

A decision is invalid if:

- the selected transformation is not currently executable;
- any required identity field does not match the fixture;
- successor information was available before the decision;
- prohibited outcome/value information was available;
- more than one transformation is returned;
- the decision is missing or cannot be reproduced from the recorded input condition.

An invalid decision is recorded as a deviation and the affected observation is excluded from scientific interpretation unless the frozen deviation rules establish otherwise.

## 6. Separation from executor

The decision mechanism is separate from `TI001_SCIENTIFIC_EXECUTOR_001.py`.

The executor applies the frozen transition after receiving the decision. It must not generate, alter, or substitute the decision.

## 7. Assignment

The condition and execution slot are taken directly from the frozen fixture. No reassignment is permitted.

## 8. Persistence

The complete decision input, decision output, and decision timestamp must be persisted in the execution evidence package, subject to the frozen package schema. No post-successor information may be inserted into the pre-decision record.

## 9. Governance boundary

This protocol freezes the decision-acquisition procedure. It does not change the fixture, randomisation, primary estimand, or execution specification.

**Scientific execution remains governed by the existing authorization record and must not be modified retrospectively.**
