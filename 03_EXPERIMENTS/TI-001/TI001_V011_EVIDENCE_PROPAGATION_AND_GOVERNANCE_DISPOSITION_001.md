## 3. Bounded scientific interpretation

V011 operationalizes a decision-level test in which the treatment condition exposes a future transformation structure while the control condition does not. The resulting `TI_DC` quantities are descriptive contrasts under the frozen construction.

The observed negative estimates do not by themselves establish absence of Transformational Intelligence, nor do they establish a general positive or negative capability of generative systems. The experiment is bounded to its frozen task, interface, fixture, model/runtime configuration and decision rule.

The cross-execution agreement is evidence of reproducibility of the executed response-level record, not evidence of theoretical validity by itself.

## 6. Governance disposition

The experiment is scientifically closed and should be treated as a completed bounded test.

Future work on Transformational Intelligence must proceed from this closed evidence rather than rerunning V011 merely to obtain another estimate. Any new experiment should address a clearly identified unresolved limitation, such as:
- construct validity;
- alternative operationalizations of future transformation structure;
- robustness across independently specified tasks/models;
- discriminating controls;
- or a pre-specified mechanism-level hypothesis.

No automatic claim upgrade follows from this record.
