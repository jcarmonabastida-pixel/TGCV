# TI-001 V012 — Discrimination Mapping from V011 Q1–Q6
## Design Derivation 001

**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Status:** DESIGN DERIVATION — V012 NOT YET SPECIFIED / SCIENTIFIC EXECUTION NOT AUTHORIZED  
**Antecedent:** TI-001 V011 closed evidence; TI Discrimination Register 001; TI Discrimination Requirements Gate 001; TI Scientific Design Readiness Gate 001.

## 1. Purpose

Translate the six unresolved questions left by V011 into explicit design obligations for the future V012 experiment.

This document is a derivation boundary. It does not freeze a V012 fixture, select a final sample size, authorize scientific execution, or replace the future V012 experimental specification.

## 2. Immutable antecedent

V011 remains closed and immutable.

V011 established bounded decision-level evidence under its frozen construction. It did not resolve Q1–Q6, did not establish a universal Transformational Intelligence construct, did not establish causality from decision behaviour to accessible transformation space or value, and did not modify the TGCV Core.

V011 must not be rerun merely to obtain additional observations.

## 3. Six-question derivation matrix

| Question | Unresolved issue | V012 discrimination obligation | Required design contrast/control | Primary observable | Falsification / non-discrimination condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 Construct validity | Whether V011 isolates future-structure incorporation rather than task/interface cues or simpler heuristics. | Isolate action-relevant future transformation-space information from ordinary task information and non-causal structural cues. | Matched current state/action space; treatment with reconstructable future-space mapping; control without mapping; structural/sham control that preserves presentation complexity without the relevant mapping; no recommendation/outcome information. | Condition-dependent transformation selection after controlling current action set and ordinary task information. | Effect persists equally in sham/ordinary-information controls, or disappears when future-space mapping is removed. |
| Q2 Presentation dependence | Whether V011 response varies because of presentation/order. | Separate future-structure sensitivity from presentation/order effects. | Semantically equivalent treatment encodings presented in independently varied/counterbalanced formats and orders; condition assignment independent of presentation. | Choice contrast stratified by presentation format/order and interaction between information condition and presentation. | Effect follows presentation/order rather than future-space information, or is present only in one presentation encoding. |
| Q3 Null/control behaviour | V011 null behaviour differed across executions. | Distinguish treatment sensitivity from baseline response variability. | Explicit null condition with matched structure/format but no future-space signal; repeated independent instances; pre-specified comparison of control/null/treatment. | Treatment-minus-control contrast relative to control/null baseline variation. | Treatment response is not distinguishable from baseline/null variation under the pre-specified comparison. |
| Q4 Robustness | Whether V011 pattern generalises beyond its operational setting. | Test persistence under independent operationalisation rather than repeated V011. | Multiple independently specified finite-state environments/transition structures and task instances; same decision mechanism and protocol where appropriate; no reuse of a single fixed instance as the sole test. | Direction and consistency of condition contrast across independent operationalisations. | Effect is confined to one fixture/task/encoding or systematically reverses under independent operationalisation. |
| Q5 Mechanism | What mechanism mediates any future-structure effect. | Distinguish future-structure incorporation from association, presentation, or generic information effects. | Mechanism-sensitive ablation/perturbation preserving current state/action space and presentation burden while selectively removing, scrambling, or preserving action-to-future consequence correspondence. | Change in choice pattern specifically attributable to intact action-conditioned future mapping. | Equivalent behaviour when the future mapping is removed/scrambled, or effect tracks generic information/presentation rather than mapping correspondence. |
| Q6 Relation to TGCV | Whether decision-level evidence connects to accessible transformation space, and only later potentially value. | Establish an independent bridge from decision to realised change in T_acc before any value endpoint is considered. | Execute selected transformation after decision; reconstruct successor state and T_acc,t+1 independently of the decision agent; compare realised transition-space consequences. Value/reward/utility/performance excluded from primary V012 inference. | Traceable sequence: information condition → decision → realised transformation → S_t+1 → T_acc,t+1. | No reproducible relation between decision differences and realised transformation-space change, or bridge depends on post-hoc value interpretation. |

## 4. Cross-question design constraints

Every V012 candidate design MUST preserve:

1. V011 immutability.
2. Current-state and current-accessibility identity where the construct test requires matched decisions.
3. Explicit separation between present executable transformations, future transformation-space information, and realised future accessibility.
4. No direct recommendation, reward, value, utility, performance or outcome-quality information in the primary TI manipulation.
5. Pre-specified observables and discrimination criteria before inspecting outcomes.
6. Independent reconstruction and integrity verification.
7. No composite TI score as the primary inferential endpoint.
8. No claim upgrade from a single V012 result.
9. Q6 must stop at the independently supported decision-to-T_acc bridge unless separate evidence warrants a later value study.
10. Any presentation, null, mechanism, or robustness manipulation must be specified independently rather than inferred retrospectively from V011.

## 6. V012 design boundary

The future V012 specification must be traceable to all six questions or explicitly document why a question is deferred.

A V012 design is not complete merely because it reproduces the action-conditioned mapping preflight. It must show how its manipulations jointly discriminate the unresolved alternatives from V011.

The future specification must therefore define, at minimum:

- experimental factors and their rationale from Q1–Q6;
- matched current state/action-space construction;
- presentation/order manipulation;
- null/control structure;
- independent operationalisations;
- mechanism-sensitive manipulation;
- decision and transition observables;
- primary and secondary estimands;
- falsification criteria;
- independent reconstruction;
- integrity/freeze boundary;
- explicit non-claims.
