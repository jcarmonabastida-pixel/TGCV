# TI Discrimination Register 001

## Status
FORMALIZED — derived from the six open questions after TI-001 V011. This register does not adjudicate among alternatives.

| ID | Unresolved issue | Compatible alternative explanations | Discriminating observables | Informative result patterns | Current non-claims |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Whether the V011 decision contrast isolates incorporation of future transformation structure. | Future-structure incorporation; task/interface artifact; simpler response heuristic; contextual or order-driven response policy. | Decision behaviour under conditions that separately distinguish access to future structure from these alternative cues. | A pattern that changes specifically with future-structure information while relevant alternative cues are held or independently varied would discriminate among explanations. | V011 does not establish construct validity or a universal TI capability. |
| Q2 | Whether the decision response is invariant to presentation/order. | Genuine future-structure sensitivity; presentation/order effect; interaction between future-structure information and presentation. | Pre-specified comparisons across independently varied presentation/order conditions, with the relevant information otherwise controlled. | Convergent versus systematically divergent decision patterns across presentation conditions would distinguish invariance from presentation dependence. | V011 presentation contrasts do not by themselves identify the mechanism of the dependence. |
| Q3 | What explains variation in control/null behaviour and how it bears on the treatment contrast. | Baseline response variability; condition-specific response policy; presentation sensitivity; interaction with the treatment manipulation. | Observations that separate baseline behaviour from sensitivity specifically attributable to future-structure information. | Stability or systematic change of baseline response across relevant controls would discriminate baseline variability from treatment-specific effects. | V011 does not establish a unique explanation for null/control variation. |
| Q4 | Whether the decision-level pattern is robust beyond the V011 operational setting. | Robust property of the proposed capability; task-specific effect; fixture-specific effect; model-specific effect; operationalisation-specific effect. | Independently specified evidence across relevant tasks, fixtures, models, or equivalent operationalisations. | Persistence versus systematic attenuation/reversal under independent operational changes would distinguish robustness from contextual specificity. | V011 cannot establish transversal validity or generalisation. |
| Q5 | What mechanism mediates any effect of future-structure information on present decisions. | Explicit incorporation of future structure; heuristic association; order/context processing; other decision mechanism compatible with observed outputs. | Mechanism-sensitive observations that produce different predictions for competing explanations while preserving the intended information distinction. | Differential response patterns predicted by competing mechanisms would provide mechanism-level discrimination. | V011 does not identify a causal or uniquely explanatory mechanism. |
| Q6 | Whether and under what conditions decision-level TI evidence connects to accessible transformation space and, only if separately warranted, value. | Decision effect remains local to choice behaviour; decision changes alter accessible transformations; transformation changes may or may not affect later value. | A separately justified evidential bridge linking decision behaviour to changes in T_acc, followed only if warranted by evidence linking those changes to value. | A traceable sequence of independently supported observations would be informative; absence of such a bridge leaves the relation unresolved. | V011 does not establish TI → ΔT_acc → ΔV, causality, or a value effect. |
