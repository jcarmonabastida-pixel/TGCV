# TI Scientific Design Readiness Gate Specification 001

## Purpose
Establish the conditions that must be satisfied before a future TI discrimination experiment is designed. This gate evaluates design readiness; it does not define, approve, or authorize a specific future experiment.

## Required readiness conditions
1. The prior TI-001 V011 evidence is canonically closed and consolidated.
2. The six open questions Q1–Q6 are explicitly preserved.
3. The discrimination register is complete and integrity-checked.
4. The minimum discrimination requirements for Q1–Q6 are formalized.
5. The principal compatible alternative explanations remain explicit and unadjudicated.
6. The intended discriminating observables are identifiable independently of observed outcomes.
7. The boundary between decision-level evidence, transformation-space evidence, and value evidence is explicit.
8. A future design must be traceable to one or more specific open questions and their discrimination requirements.
9. A future design must specify how the proposed evidence would discriminate among alternatives, rather than merely reproduce V011.
10. Any future design must preserve independent execution, auditability, reproducibility, and pre-specified analysis boundaries where applicable.

## Design discipline
A future scientific design should, before authorization, make explicit:
- the primary open question addressed;
- the competing explanations to be discriminated;
- the observable pattern that would be informative for each explanation;
- controls or comparisons necessary to separate those explanations;
- the boundary of conclusions supported by the resulting evidence.

These are design requirements, not a V012 protocol.

## Boundary
This gate does not:
- define V012;
- select a task, fixture, model, sample size, randomization, estimator, prompt, or execution protocol;
- authorize scientific execution;
- select a winning hypothesis;
- upgrade claims;
- modify the TGCV Core;
- mutate V011 scientific results.

## Final disposition
If all readiness conditions and design-discipline requirements are represented, the disposition is `TI_SCIENTIFIC_DESIGN_READY`.
