# TI Open Questions & Discrimination Gate — Specification

## Purpose
Formalize the unresolved scientific questions left by the closed TI-001 V011 evidence and define discrimination requirements for future work without designing, authorizing, or executing V012.

## Required questions

### Q1 — Construct validity
Does the V011 decision contrast isolate incorporation of future transformation structure, or can it be explained by task/interface artifacts or simpler decision heuristics?
**Discrimination requirement:** future evidence must distinguish future-structure incorporation from plausible non-TI alternative mechanisms.

### Q2 — Presentation dependence
V011 shows presentation-stratified differences. Is the observed decision response invariant to presentation/order, or is it substantially driven by presentation effects?
**Discrimination requirement:** independently specified presentation manipulations and pre-specified comparison criteria.

### Q3 — Null/control behaviour
The two executions differ in null-condition behaviour. What mechanisms explain this variation, and what does it imply for interpretation of the treatment-control contrast?
**Discrimination requirement:** controls that distinguish treatment sensitivity from baseline decision variability.

### Q4 — Robustness
Does the observed decision-level pattern persist under independently specified tasks, fixtures, models, or equivalent operationalisations?
**Discrimination requirement:** replication/generalisation tests with independently specified evidence, not repeated identical execution solely for more observations.

### Q5 — Mechanism
If future-structure information affects present decisions, what identifiable mechanism mediates that effect?
**Discrimination requirement:** observations capable of separating competing mechanism-level explanations.

### Q6 — Relation to TGCV
Does decision-level Transformational Intelligence evidence connect to changes in accessible transformation space, and under what conditions could a later value endpoint be meaningfully introduced?
**Discrimination requirement:** a separately justified bridge from decision-level evidence to transformation-space and, only if warranted, value-level evidence.

## Explicit non-claims
V011 does not establish:
- a universal Transformational Intelligence capability;
- causal `Delta T_acc -> Delta V`;
- transversal validity;
- explanatory or predictive superiority;
- ontological necessity of a new TGCV Core primitive.

## Gate boundary
This gate produces a research-question/discrimination register only. It does not:
- create a V012 specification;
- create or modify a fixture;
- authorize scientific execution;
- alter V011 results;
- upgrade C01–C16;
- modify the TGCV Core.

## Final disposition
`TI_OPEN_QUESTIONS_FORMALIZED` when all required questions and discrimination requirements are recorded and the non-claims boundary is preserved.
