# TI-001 — Transformational Intelligence Evidence Consolidation Gate Specification

## Purpose
Consolidate the scientific meaning of closed TI-001 V011 evidence and identify the unresolved empirical questions that would justify future work.

## Required checks
1. V011 closure is canonical and status is CLOSED_FOR_TI001_V011.
2. E1-R and E2-R are independently valid and audited.
3. Scientific analysis and primary analysis audit are PASS.
4. V011 is classified as bounded experimental evidence, not as a claim-level upgrade.
5. Existing C01–C16 statuses remain unchanged.
6. TGCV Core remains unchanged.
7. The observed TI_DC estimates are recorded descriptively without directional capability claims.
8. Cross-execution agreement is treated as reproducibility evidence, not construct validation.
9. The main unresolved questions are explicitly separated from completed V011 evidence.
10. No V012 design, execution, or authorization is implied by this gate.

## Consolidation boundary
This gate is a scientific evidence-consolidation step. It does not reanalyse V011, modify its results, upgrade a TGCV claim, or authorize a new experiment.

## Required final disposition
`TI_EVIDENCE_CONSOLIDATED` when all checks pass.
