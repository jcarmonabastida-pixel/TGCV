# TI Discrimination Closure Gate Specification 001

## Purpose
Close the TI discrimination phase after TI-001 V011, the discrimination register, and the discrimination requirements have been formally consolidated. The closure establishes readiness for a later scientific design phase without defining or authorizing V012.

## Closure conditions
1. TI-001 V011 is canonically closed and its evidence is consolidated.
2. The TI discrimination register contains exactly Q1–Q6.
3. Each Q1–Q6 has an explicit minimum discrimination requirement.
4. The register records compatible alternatives without adjudicating among them.
5. No Q1–Q6 is declared scientifically resolved by V011.
6. No claim status has been upgraded as a consequence of this phase.
7. The TGCV Core remains unchanged.
8. V012 has not been designed or authorized.
9. V011 scientific results remain immutable.
10. The phase boundary and next-stage readiness are explicit.

## Evidence required
- TI-001 V011 scientific closure gate result.
- TI evidence consolidation gate result.
- TI open questions and discrimination gate result.
- TI discrimination register integrity gate result.
- TI discrimination requirements gate result.

## Boundary
This closure gate does not:
- define a V012 experiment;
- select a future task, fixture, model, sample size, randomization, estimator, or execution protocol;
- authorize scientific execution;
- resolve any open scientific question without new evidence;
- upgrade claims;
- modify the TGCV Core;
- mutate V011 scientific results.

## Final disposition
If all closure conditions and required evidence are present, the final disposition is `TI_DISCRIMINATION_PHASE_CLOSED_FOR_DESIGN`.
