# TGCV — Transformational Capability / Intelligence Conceptual Specification 001

**Status:** CANDIDATE — CONCEPTUAL / NOT EXPERIMENTAL
**Scientific execution:** NOT AUTHORIZED
**Date:** 2026-09-20

## 1. Purpose
Define a candidate phenomenon for TGCV research without assuming that it exists as a new primitive, without defining it through value, and without treating TR-131 as proof of its existence.

## 2. Candidate phenomenon
Working concept: transformational capability/intelligence denotes the system-level capacity by which a system can discriminate among, select, coordinate, realize, adapt, or learn transformations from a set of transformations accessible under its current conditions.

This is a hypothesis label, not a settled construct.

## 3. Separation from T_acc
T_acc describes which transformations are accessible. The candidate construct concerns how accessible possibilities are handled by the system.

Conceptually: T_acc = possibility space; TCI = transformation-handling capacity.

The distinction must remain testable. If all observed variation in selection/realization can be represented adequately by T_acc and existing state/context variables, the candidate construct loses explanatory necessity.

## 4. Separation from value
The candidate construct must not be defined as the ability to create value. A system could display high transformational capability while producing low value under particular objectives, constraints, environments, or trajectories.

A value outcome must not be used retrospectively to define transformational capability.

## 5. Candidate dimensions
Research may investigate discrimination among accessible transformations; selection under competing alternatives; coordination; realization under constraints; adaptation of selection; learning from prior transformations; composition or sequencing; and maintenance or expansion of future transformational possibilities.

These are hypotheses for investigation, not an imposed ontology.

## 6. Relation to TR-131
TR-131 provides a motivating boundary condition: equal tested (S,C,T_acc) can coexist with different realization outcomes when the realization condition differs.

This does not establish TCI. It establishes that the tested accessibility representation is insufficient to explain the observed realization contrast.

The new construct must therefore be independently specified and subsequently tested.

## 7. Candidate process architecture
A provisional research architecture is:

S_t → T_acc,t → transformation handling → T_real,t → S_(t+1) → trajectory → outcome → value

The transformation-handling layer is the object of investigation.

No claim is made that this layer is a single operator, a primitive, or irreducible.

## 8. Falsification requirements
The candidate construct is weakened or unnecessary if T_acc plus existing state/context variables fully explains selection and realization; proposed TCI measures add no explanatory power beyond T_acc; apparent TCI effects disappear under controlled counterfactuals; the construct can only be defined using observed outcomes; or competing established constructs explain the same phenomena without requiring TCI.

## 9. Positive evidence requirements
Evidence would require more than different outcomes under different policies. A stronger demonstration would show that independently measured transformation-handling characteristics predict differences in realization or trajectory while controlling the accessible transformation space and relevant state/context variables.

A further step would test whether those characteristics alter subsequent outcomes or value without defining the construct from those outcomes.

## 10. Causal research questions
Q1 — Accessibility: Does a change in T_acc alter the set of realizable transformations?

Q2 — Handling: Given equivalent T_acc, do independently measured transformation-handling characteristics alter which transformations are realized?

Q3 — Trajectory: Do differences in transformation handling produce systematic differences in subsequent trajectories?

Q4 — Outcome: Do trajectory differences produce differences in domain outcomes?

Q5 — Value: Under specified value functions, do those outcome differences translate into differences in value?

No causal arrow is accepted in advance.

## 11. Competing explanations
Future tests should distinguish the candidate construct from ordinary policy differences, explicit decision rules, state/context differences, resource constraints, environmental information, stochastic selection, learning history, dynamic capabilities, affordance structures, search/optimization mechanisms, and other established mechanisms of adaptive selection and realization.

The programme should not claim novelty merely because established mechanisms are described using new terminology.

## 12. Transformational intelligence versus transformational capability
The terms remain deliberately separated at this stage.

Transformational capability may refer to an observable or organizational capacity to perform transformation-related activities.

Transformational intelligence may refer to a broader construct involving discrimination, selection, adaptation, learning, and coordination across a changing transformational possibility space.

No choice between the terms is made by this specification.

## 13. Research strategy
The next stage should be conceptual and comparative before experimental.

Required sequence: define the phenomenon independently; map adjacent established constructs; identify discriminating dimensions; derive competing models; identify observable implications; design falsification tests; only then construct experiments.

## 14. Core boundary
This specification does not modify TGCV Core. It does not establish TCI as a Core primitive, does not establish Pi as a Core primitive, and does not establish causal ΔT_acc → ΔValue.

## 15. Immediate next gate
**CONSTRUCT DIFFERENTIATION AND LITERATURE EVIDENCE GATE**

The next task is to determine whether the candidate phenomenon is genuinely distinguishable from existing constructs and whether prior evidence justifies a new TGCV construct rather than a relabelling.

No experiment is authorized at this stage.
