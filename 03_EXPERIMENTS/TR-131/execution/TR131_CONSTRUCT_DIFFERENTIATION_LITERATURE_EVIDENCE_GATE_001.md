# TGCV — Construct Differentiation and Literature Evidence Gate 001

**Status:** CLOSED — CONCEPTUAL DIFFERENTIATION REQUIRED
**Scientific execution:** NOT AUTHORIZED
**Date:** 2026-09-20

## 1. Gate result
Current literature does not support treating transformational capability or transformational intelligence as an unused term that TGCV can simply claim as novel.

Related constructs already exist across dynamic capabilities, transformational capabilities, adaptive systems, enterprise capability modelling, and transformation/organizational change literature.

Therefore the immediate research task is not to name a new construct, but to determine whether TGCV identifies a distinct analytical object.

## 2. Evidence reviewed
Relevant literature located includes:
- Koutsopoulos, Henkel & Stirna (2021): capability meta-models for dynamic business transformation, including transformational capabilities and runtime self-awareness/adaptability.
- Recent work on agile manufacturing and transformational capabilities treats transformational capabilities as intermediate mechanisms between agility/dynamic capabilities and sustainable performance.
- Dynamic capabilities literature explicitly addresses sensing, seizing and transforming, including selection, resource reconfiguration and transformation.
- Literature on transformative capacity also treats transformation as an emergent property involving agency, processes and context.
- The term transformational intelligence has prior uses in organizational change and other domains and therefore cannot be assumed to have a unique TGCV meaning.

## 3. Decisive distinction
The candidate TGCV construct should not be defined as generic organizational ability to transform.

The potentially distinctive TGCV object is narrower:
**the capacity/mechanism by which a system handles a currently accessible space of transformations, including discrimination, selection, realization, sequencing, adaptation or learning, and thereby generates subsequent trajectories.**

This is still a hypothesis and must be tested against established constructs.

## 4. Current differentiation hypothesis
A potentially differentiating feature is the explicit separation between:
`T_acc` = currently accessible transformation space

and:
`transformation handling` = the process/capacity governing which accessible transformations are selected and realized and how realization changes subsequent possibilities.

Existing dynamic-capability and transformation literatures often operate at organizational/resource/capability levels. TGCV's possible contribution would need to demonstrate a cross-domain analytical layer tied explicitly to transformation spaces and trajectories, rather than merely renaming dynamic capability.

## 5. Terminology decision
Do not canonize the term transformational intelligence yet.

The phrase has heterogeneous prior uses and creates an immediate construct-validity and novelty burden.

For the next research stage, use the neutral working term:
**transformation-handling capacity (THC)**

THC is a temporary research label only. It does not enter TGCV Core.

## 6. Falsification / differentiation test
A future TGCV construct is worth retaining only if it can demonstrate at least one property not captured by the competing constructs under the same analytical conditions:
1. representation of an explicit accessible transformation space;
2. discrimination among alternatives within that space;
3. realization/selection effects conditional on equivalent accessibility;
4. effects on subsequent transformation trajectories;
5. cross-domain operationalization independent of value outcomes.

Failure to establish these distinctions means TGCV should integrate the phenomenon into existing theory rather than introduce a new construct.

## 7. Value relationship
The literature reviewed is consistent with treating capability effects on performance/value as potentially indirect rather than assuming a direct transformation-capability → value relation.

This reinforces the current TGCV decision not to assume causal ΔT_acc → ΔValue.

Future work must test the sequence from accessibility to handling, trajectory, outcome and value separately.

## 8. Decision
Do not launch a new formal irreducibility ontology.
Do not run a new experiment yet.
Do not promote transformational intelligence to TGCV Core.
Do not claim novelty for transformational capability/intelligence.

Proceed to a **construct-differentiation matrix** comparing THC against dynamic capabilities, transformational capabilities, affordances, adaptability/self-adaptive mechanisms, search/selection, learning/evolution and related constructs.
