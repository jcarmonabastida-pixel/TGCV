## 1. Review question
Does the provisional Transformation-Handling Capacity (THC) hypothesis identify an analytical object sufficiently distinct from established constructs to justify further TGCV development?

## 2. Evidence reviewed
### Dynamic capabilities
Dynamic-capabilities research already frames organizational change through sensing, seizing and transforming/reconfiguring. The transforming/reconfiguring component explicitly concerns changing routines and resource configurations, so a generic claim that THC concerns the ability to transform would not be distinctive. citeturn0search48turn0search49

### Affordances and actualization
Affordance research already separates action possibilities from their actualization and effects. Strong et al. explicitly identify the process of actualizing an affordance's potential and emphasize its non-deterministic relation to organizational effects. Later work also distinguishes affordance existence, perception, actualization and effect. citeturn0search3turn0search2

This is the closest conceptual competitor to a TGCV separation between accessible possibilities and realized transformations.

### Self-adaptive systems
Self-adaptive-systems research already formalizes runtime adaptation through feedback/control loops such as MAPE-K, including explicit adaptation logic, planning and execution. citeturn0search0turn0search4

Recent work also studies learning-based runtime adaptation and decision mechanisms. citeturn0search15

### Search, optimization and learning
Search/optimization and learning approaches already model selection among candidate actions/solutions and adaptation from feedback. Therefore selection among transformations alone cannot establish novelty.

## 4. Decisive finding
The review does not establish that THC is a new construct.

The strongest potentially distinctive feature is narrower:
> TGCV may provide a cross-domain analytical decomposition in which a formally represented space of accessible transformations is distinguished from the mechanisms that select and realize transformations, with resulting trajectories represented explicitly and value treated as a downstream relation.

This is not equivalent to claiming that dynamic capabilities, affordances or self-adaptation do not already contain similar ideas. They clearly do. The burden is to show that TGCV's particular decomposition adds explanatory precision or transferability that those frameworks do not provide.

## 5. Closest competitor
The closest conceptual competitor is affordance actualization.

Affordance research explicitly asks how action possibilities become actualized and how actualization leads to effects. citeturn0search3turn0search13

Therefore the TGCV novelty question should not be whether TGCV distinguishes possibility from realization. That distinction already exists.

The sharper question is whether TGCV's explicit transformation-space representation and trajectory formalism provide a materially different and cross-domain explanatory unit from affordance/action-possibility plus actualization.

## 6. Stronger potential TGCV contribution
A defensible future contribution could potentially be methodological rather than ontological:

A common analytical representation of transformation spaces and their evolution across adaptive systems, capable of separating accessibility, realization and trajectory without defining the mechanism through value.

This would be stronger than claiming a new intelligence or capability construct prematurely.

## 7. Required evidence for novelty
Before introducing THC as a canonical construct, TGCV should demonstrate at least one of:
1. an explicit transformation-space formalism that is not merely a relabelling of affordance/action space;
2. a trajectory calculus that yields explanatory distinctions unavailable in the compared frameworks;
3. cross-domain transfer using the same representation and measurable quantities;
4. an empirical prediction conditional on equal transformation accessibility;
5. an explanatory decomposition that identifies mechanisms missed by existing frameworks.

Without such evidence, the appropriate outcome is integration with existing theory rather than construct creation.

## 8. Implication for transformational intelligence
The term Transformational Intelligence should remain non-canonical.

The review provides no basis for claiming that TGCV has discovered a distinct intelligence construct.

If future evidence supports the transformation-handling layer, the name should be selected only after construct validity and literature differentiation are established.

## 9. Implication for ΔT_acc → ΔValue
The review strengthens the decision not to treat ΔT_acc → ΔValue as the central causal hypothesis.

A more discriminating research chain is:
ΔT_acc → transformation handling → T_real → trajectory → outcome → value

Each arrow requires independent theoretical and empirical justification.

TR-131 provides evidence only for insufficiency of the tested (S,C,T_acc) representation for explaining realization differences; it does not establish the later arrows.

## 10. Decision
Do not create THC as a new TGCV Core construct yet.
Do not launch a new experiment yet.
Do not pursue formal irreducibility of Pi at this stage.

The next productive step is to determine whether the transformation-space / trajectory formalism itself provides a genuine cross-domain contribution independent of THC and independent of value.
