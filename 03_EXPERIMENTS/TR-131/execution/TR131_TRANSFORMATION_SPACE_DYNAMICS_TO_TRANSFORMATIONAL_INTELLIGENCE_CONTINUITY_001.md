## 1. Purpose
This record preserves and integrates the conceptual result developed after the corrected TR-131 V007 cross-domain analysis.
It does not replace prior TGCV manifests, governance records, construct-distinction reviews, or experimental freezes. It is an additive continuity record.
The immediate motivation is the corrected V007 observation that the relationship between a realized transformation and the subsequent accessibility space can be represented across VisitAll and PRISM without requiring a causal claim from accessibility change to value.

## 3. Empirical observation motivating the new line
The corrected V007 result shows two structurally different forms of transformation-space reconfiguration.
### PRISM
For all 16 PRISM transition records:
`A_t = 1`
`A_t1 = 1`
`G = 1`
`L = 1`
`P = 0`
`D = 0`
Thus the space preserves cardinality while replacing the accessible transformation identity. Examples include:
`{read} -> {retry}`
`{retry} -> {pick}`
`{done} -> {loop}`
The descriptive interpretation is identity turnover with cardinality conservation, not net expansion or net contraction.
### VisitAll
The 20 VisitAll records show turnover throughout, with most transitions preserving cardinality and four transitions changing cardinality from 4 to 3.
The two domains therefore expose different transformation-space dynamics while remaining representable through the common sequence:
`T_acc,t -> T_real,t -> S_t1 -> T_acc,t1`
This is the relevant cross-domain observation.

## 4. Shift in analytical focus
The corrected result reinforces the decision not to make Delta_T_acc -> Value the central causal hypothesis of TGCV.
The current research focus instead moves toward the dynamics of the transformation space itself.
The relevant object is Transformation-Space Dynamics, understood initially as the observable evolution of the set/structure of transformations accessible to a system as the system realizes transformations and enters subsequent states.
The analytical sequence is:
`T_acc,t -> T_real,t -> S_t1 -> T_acc,t1`
rather than:
`Delta_T_acc -> Value`

## 5. Proposed analytical layer: Transformation-Space Dynamics
Transformation-Space Dynamics is an analytical layer, not yet a new ontological primitive.
Candidate observable properties include:
- accessibility expansion and contraction;
- identity turnover;
- persistence/retention;
- appearance and disappearance of transformation identities;
- branching;
- reconfiguration;
- transformation-space trajectory;
- structural divergence between possible trajectories;
- changes in future accessibility following realized transformations.
These properties are to be treated as descriptive/analytical observables unless subsequent evidence warrants a stronger construct claim.

## 6. Proposed conceptual artefact: Transformation-Space Dynamics Analysis
A new conceptual artefact is proposed: **Transformation-Space Dynamics Analysis**.
Its purpose is to provide a structured analytical bridge between the state/transition representation already operationalised in TR-131 and the subsequent investigation of Transformational Intelligence.
The artefact should preserve, at minimum:
`S_t`
`T_acc,t`
`T_real,t`
`S_t1`
`T_acc,t1`
`Delta_T_acc,t`
and derived dynamic descriptors.
It should analyse how a system traverses and reconfigures its transformation space, rather than reducing the analysis to a scalar measure of accessibility or value.
This artefact is conceptual at this stage. Its creation does not authorize a Core modification.

## 7. Fundamental construct distinction
A central distinction is established:
`Transformation-Space Dynamics != Transformational Intelligence`
Transformation-Space Dynamics describes **what happens to the transformation space**.
Transformational Intelligence is a hypothesis about **a system capability in relation to that space**.
The distinction is deliberately maintained to avoid the ontological leap:
`observable reconfiguration -> new intelligence construct`
No such inference is currently authorized.

## 8. Formal bridge to Transformational Intelligence
The next conceptual research artefact should be a formal map:
`Transformation-Space Dynamics -> Transformational Intelligence`
The map is intended as the natural bridge between the TR-131/V007 empirical result and the emerging Transformational Intelligence research line.
The proposed sequence is:
`Transformation-Space Dynamics -> representation of transformation-space structure -> handling/navigation of transformations -> possible anticipatory/adaptive use of transformation-space information -> Transformational Intelligence hypothesis`
Each arrow must remain an explicit research question rather than an assumed causal or ontological relation.

## 9. Working hypothesis for Transformational Intelligence
The current preferred open hypothesis is:
> **Transformational Intelligence is a capacity of a system to represent, evaluate, select, anticipate, or adapt its behaviour as a function of the structure and evolution of its accessible space of transformations.**
This is deliberately a **hypothesis**, not an established construct.
The definition does not require value production.
Value therefore remains external to the definition of Transformational Intelligence and may subsequently be studied as an outcome or contextual consequence.

## 10. Separation from Value
The current architecture is:
`Transformation-Space Dynamics -> Transformational Intelligence (hypothesis) -> behaviour / transformation selection / adaptation -> outcomes -> value`
This is a research architecture, not a demonstrated causal chain.
In particular, current evidence does not establish Delta_T_acc -> Value nor Transformational Intelligence -> Value, nor that Transformational Intelligence is a superior explanation of value construction.

## 11. Ontological governance rule
The governing sequence remains:
**Evidence first -> conceptual differentiation second -> ontological review third -> Core modification only if warranted.**
Accordingly:
- Transformation-Space Dynamics is currently an analytical layer.
- Transformation-Space Dynamics Analysis is a proposed conceptual artefact.
- Transformational Intelligence remains a hypothesis/open construct.
- No new primitive is added to TGCV Core at this stage.
- Value remains separated from the definition of Transformational Intelligence.
- Negative results remain admissible.

## 12. Research direction
The immediate research direction is therefore not another attempt to establish a direct causal relationship between Delta_T_acc and value.
The next conceptual task is to formalise the map:
`Transformation-Space Dynamics -> Transformational Intelligence`
and determine which properties of transformation-space dynamics are necessary, sufficient, or merely correlated with the hypothesised capability.
The empirical question should subsequently become whether a system can **use information about the structure and evolution of its transformation space** to represent, evaluate, select, anticipate, or adapt transformations in a way that is distinguishable from ordinary transformation execution.
That question is intentionally left open.
