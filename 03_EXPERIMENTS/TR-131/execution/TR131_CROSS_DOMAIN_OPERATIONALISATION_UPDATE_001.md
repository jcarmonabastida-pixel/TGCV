## 2. Operational representation established by TR-131
The common cross-domain representation is:

    S_t -> T_acc,t -> T_real,t -> S_t1 -> T_acc,t1

with transformation-space change represented as added and removed identities:

    Delta_T_acc,t = (T_acc,t1 minus T_acc,t, T_acc,t minus T_acc,t1)

and descriptors A_t, A_t1, G_t, L_t, P_t, R_t and D_t. These are analytical properties of a transition between transformation spaces, not a universal scalar capability score.

## 6. Cross-domain operationalisation finding
The result is not that VisitAll and PRISM have equivalent transformation spaces. Their identities, semantics, cardinalities and domain mechanisms remain domain-specific.

The cross-domain finding is that both can be represented by the same analytical relation:

    T_acc,t -> T_real,t -> S_t1 -> T_acc,t1

and that the same abstract descriptors can characterise materially different transformation-space dynamics.

## 7. Bounded operationalisation claim
> **The evolution of an accessible transformation space can be operationally represented across at least two structurally distinct domains by a common transition-level representation linking source state, accessible transformations, realised transformation, successor state and successor accessibility, from which transformation-space reconfiguration descriptors can be mechanically derived.**

This claim is bounded to the audited domains and evidence and does not establish universal applicability.

## 8. Why this supports Transformation-Space Dynamics
The analytically relevant object is not merely |T_acc| but the relation between successive transformation spaces. Two transitions can preserve cardinality while replacing all transformation identities.

PRISM demonstrates this directly with patterns such as:

    {read} -> {retry}
    {retry} -> {pick}

Therefore transformation-space dynamics must retain identity and relational change rather than collapse the space into a scalar capacity measure.

## 9. Analytical-layer status
On the basis of TR-131/V007, Transformation-Space Dynamics can now be treated as a proposed analytical layer grounded in operational evidence, while remaining distinct from a new ontological primitive.

Candidate observables include identity turnover, persistence, additions and removals, net cardinality change, reconfiguration, branching where supported, trajectory structure, and future accessibility reconfiguration following realised transformations.

## 10. Distinction from Transformational Intelligence
This update does not establish Transformational Intelligence.

    Transformation-Space Dynamics = observable evolution of the transformation space
    Transformational Intelligence = hypothesised system capability in relation to that space

TI requires separate evidence that a system can represent, evaluate, select, anticipate or adapt behaviour as a function of transformation-space structure or evolution. TR-131/V007 does not provide that evidence.

## 11. Distinction from value
This update does not establish Delta_T_acc -> Value, Transformation-Space Dynamics -> Value, or Transformational Intelligence -> Value. Value remains an external outcome/context variable.
