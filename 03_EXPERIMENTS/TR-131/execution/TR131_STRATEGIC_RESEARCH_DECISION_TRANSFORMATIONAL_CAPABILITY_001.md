# TGCV — Strategic Research Decision: From ΔT_acc→Value to Transformational Capability

**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Status:** CANONICAL RESEARCH DECISION — CONCEPTUAL / NOT EXPERIMENTAL

## 1. Decision
The TGCV programme will not open, at this stage, a new explicit representation ontology whose primary purpose is to prove formal irreducibility of the realization operator Pi.

The formal irreducibility programme arising from TR-131 remains closed at:
**INCONCLUSIVE — REPRESENTATION CLASS NOT CLOSED.**

No new ontology will be constructed merely to make Pi irreducible.

## 2. Rationale
TR-131 established a representation insufficiency result:
(S,C,T_acc)_A = (S,C,T_acc)_B
while:
X_A != X_B
H_A != H_B

The result supports the conclusion that the tested representation (S,C,T_acc) is insufficient to explain realization differences.

It does not establish formal irreducibility of Pi, Pi as a TGCV Core primitive, causal sufficiency of ΔT_acc for value creation, empirical generality, or a causal relation ΔT_acc → ΔValue.

The subsequent formal irreducibility design audit showed that the frozen TGCV semantics do not provide a closed, independently justified representation class from which such an irreducibility proof could be conducted without introducing new theoretical assumptions.

The programme therefore rejects an outcome-driven ontology construction.

## 3. Strategic redirection
The next conceptual line of TGCV research will investigate whether its deeper object is not a direct causal relation ΔT_acc → ΔValue but a broader transformational process:

system state → transformational possibilities → transformation selection → realized transformations → trajectories → outcomes → value

This is a research hypothesis, not a settled TGCV architecture.

Working question:
> What system-level capacity explains how a system converts a space of accessible transformations into selected and realized transformations and subsequent trajectories, and how, if at all, does that capacity relate to value creation?

## 4. Provisional role of Pi
Pi remains a provisional explanatory construct for realization/selection.
It is not promoted to Core.
It is not declared irreducible.
It is not assumed to be ontologically fundamental.
Future work may replace, decompose, refine, or eliminate Pi depending on independent evidence.

## 5. New research direction
The programme will investigate a possible transformational capability / transformational intelligence layer.

The term transformational intelligence is initially treated as a research direction and hypothesis label, not as an established TGCV construct.

The research must determine whether the underlying phenomenon can be defined independently of its desired explanatory role and whether it provides measurable explanatory power beyond T_acc.

Candidate conceptual distinction:
- T_acc: what transformations are accessible under the current system description;
- transformational capability/intelligence: how the system can discriminate, select, coordinate, realize, adapt, or learn among accessible transformations;
- trajectory: the sequence of realized transformations and resulting states;
- value: an outcome-level construct whose relationship to the preceding layers remains to be established.

These are provisional distinctions only.

## 6. Causal stance
The programme will not assume that ΔT_acc → ΔValue is causal.
It will also not assume that the relation is absent.

The next research phase should explicitly distinguish:
1. changes in transformational possibility;
2. changes in realization/selection;
3. changes in trajectory;
4. changes in outcomes;
5. changes in value.

The purpose is to determine which relations, if any, are causally supported.

## 7. Relationship to TR-131
TR-131 remains valid and closed as a representation-insufficiency result.

Its scientific value is preserved as a boundary condition: accessible transformations alone do not necessarily determine which transformation is realized when other pre-realization conditions differ.

The result is therefore used to motivate the next question, not to pre-judge its answer.

## 8. Research discipline for the new line
The new line must avoid:
- building constructs solely to rescue a prior hypothesis;
- defining intelligence as successful value creation;
- defining capability as the ability to realize the observed outcome;
- encoding outcomes into state variables;
- circular definitions of selection or realization;
- treating correlation between accessibility and value as causal evidence;
- promoting provisional concepts directly into TGCV Core.

Any proposed construct must have independently stated semantics, observable implications, falsification conditions, and a clear separation between mechanism, trajectory and value.

## 9. Evidence status
A search of the canonical TGCV repository on 2026-09-20 found no existing TGCV artifact defining transformational intelligence, a formal transformational capability construct, or an existing representation ontology suitable for the proposed irreducibility proof.

Therefore this direction is a new research development, not a relabelling of an existing canonical construct.

## 10. Immediate next gate
The next gate is:
**TRANSFORMATIONAL CAPABILITY / INTELLIGENCE CONCEPTUAL SPECIFICATION**

This gate should define the candidate phenomenon independently of value and independently of the observed TR-131 result, identify competing interpretations, specify what would distinguish it from T_acc, and derive falsifiable research questions.

No experiment is authorized by this decision.

## 11. Governance boundary
This decision does not modify the frozen TR-131 package, TR-131 results or dispositions, TGCV Core, RMA, or Evidence→Claim Matrix, and does not authorize scientific execution.

It records the strategic transition from a blocked formal-irreducibility programme to a new conceptual research line.
