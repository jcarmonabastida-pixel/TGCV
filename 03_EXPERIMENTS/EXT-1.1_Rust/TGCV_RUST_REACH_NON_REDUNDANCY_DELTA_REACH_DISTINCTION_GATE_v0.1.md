# TGCV — Rust Reach Non-Redundancy / ΔReach Distinction Gate v0.1

**Status:** PASS — REACH NON-REDUNDANCY TEST CONTRACT FROZEN

## Purpose

Determine whether `Reach` contributes an analytically distinct object beyond `T_acc`, rather than merely renaming transformation membership.

## Locked architecture

`Core_ontological = S`

`T_acc,t = {(e_o,τ) | P_τ(e_o,t)=1}`

`ΔT_acc(t,t+1) = T_acc,t+1 ≄ T_acc,t`

`Reach` remains downstream from `T_acc`.

## Central question

The test must distinguish:

1. change in accessible transformations;
2. change in potential successor configurations;
3. change in the number/identity of reachable successors.

A simple equality of cardinalities is insufficient evidence of equivalence or non-equivalence.

## Non-redundancy constructions

### NR-1 — ΔT_acc ≠ 0, ΔReach = 0

A newly accessible transformation may generate a successor already generated by another accessible transformation. This establishes that `T_acc` and `Reach` are not identical objects.

### NR-2 — ΔT_acc ≠ 0, ΔReach ≠ 0

A newly accessible transformation may generate a successor configuration not previously available. This establishes an explicit downstream structural consequence.

### NR-3 — same T_acc cardinality, different Reach

Two T_acc sets may have equal cardinality while containing transformations producing different successor configurations. Therefore cardinality of T_acc is not sufficient to identify Reach.

### NR-4 — different T_acc, same Reach

Different transformation memberships may collapse onto the same successor configuration. Therefore Reach cannot generally identify T_acc.

## Required comparison objects

For each paired focal transition:

- `T_acc,t0`
- `T_acc,t1`
- `Reach¹_pot,t0`
- `Reach¹_pot,t1`
- transformation→successor mapping

The implementation must retain transformation identity separately from successor identity.

## Successor identity

A potential successor must be represented by the complete canonical configuration resulting from the single transformation under the frozen successor semantics.

It must not be represented solely by the transformation identity `(origin,target_package,target_version)`.

If the current implementation cannot construct configuration-level successor identity without importing unsupported resolver semantics, that limitation must be reported rather than silently bypassed.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- no Cargo execution;
- no resolver-selected version;
- no observed runtime transition;
- no outcome or value;
- no future activity;
- no predictive model;
- no post-hoc selection of examples;
- no inference from successor counts alone;
- no replacement of configuration identity by transformation identity.

## Falsifiers

**RN-F1:** every distinct T_acc membership necessarily maps one-to-one to a distinct successor configuration.

**RN-F2:** Reach can be reconstructed from transformation membership without configuration-level information.

**RN-F3:** successor identity cannot be defined without importing execution/resolver outcomes.

**RN-F4:** all temporal differences in Reach are mathematically identical to differences in T_acc under the frozen representation.

**RN-F5:** no valid non-redundant successor configuration can be constructed in the bounded Rust representation.

## Decision

**PASS — REACH NON-REDUNDANCY TEST CONTRACT FROZEN.**

The next implementation must test configuration-level successor identity and explicitly search for both redundant and non-redundant mappings. It must not treat the previously observed equality between T_acc and Reach counts as evidence of equivalence.
