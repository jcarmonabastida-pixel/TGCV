# TGCV — Current Research Architecture

## Central research object

Interactions that modify the set of transformations accessible to a system.

## Core analytical chain

**Interaction → modification of accessible transformations → altered future trajectory/possibilities → consequences for value construction.**

The architecture is intentionally domain-independent at its core. Application domains provide empirical instantiations and constraints rather than redefining the central object.

## Scientific proposition

TGCV proposes a cautious, falsifiable hypothesis that the phenomenon above is widespread but represented through fragmented constructs across fields such as adaptive/self-adaptive systems, adaptive dynamic systems, changeability, affordances, dynamic capabilities, self-X systems, runtime composition, learning and evolution.

The research task is not to claim that these fields are equivalent, but to determine whether a minimal common representation can account for the relevant transformations without erasing domain-specific mechanisms.

## Empirical programme

EXT-1.0 and EXT-1.1 are empirical components of the programme. EXT-1.1 is subject to an explicit identifiability/privacy gate before dataset acquisition/processing and before its results can become a frozen canonical state.

## Value construction

The practical programme derives a methodology from the ontological core: value is investigated in relation to how interactions alter future transformation possibilities and the trajectories that follow. This practical layer must remain traceable to the scientific core.
