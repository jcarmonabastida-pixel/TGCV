# Statement of Purpose — PhD

**Programme:** PhD in Complex Systems for Societal and Technological Transitions — IE University  
**Source:** IE_PhD_Candidatura_Final_Pack_REVISED_v4.zip (ChatGPT Library)

## Motivation

I am applying to IE’s PhD because I want to turn a research question that has emerged from more than 25 years of professional experience in technology, organisations and transformation into a rigorous and testable research programme.

## Research question

Across different professional contexts, I have repeatedly encountered a common problem: interactions can change not only a system’s current state but also what the system can subsequently do. This has led me to investigate whether a transversal analytical layer can explain how particular interactions modify the conditions of future transformations and how those modifications relate to subsequent trajectories and value creation.

## Scientific stance

The project is deliberately cautious. I do not present TGCV as an established theory or claim to have discovered a new phenomenon. Instead, I propose a falsifiable hypothesis and seek to determine whether a minimal transversal representation can capture a common mechanism and add explanatory value beyond existing accounts.

## Why IE

IE’s PhD in Complex Systems for Societal and Technological Transitions provides a strong fit because it combines complex-systems thinking with technological and societal applications and interdisciplinary research.

## Why a PhD now

Professional experience has given me extensive exposure to complex technological and organisational systems, but experience alone cannot establish whether the relationships under investigation are valid. Doctoral research is the appropriate transition from professional observation to formal theory-building, modelling and validation.

## Career plans

After the PhD, I intend to combine research with professional engagement in technology and organisational transformation, contributing to academic-practitioner dialogue and applied research.

## Expected contribution

The immediate goal is a scientifically defensible contribution to the understanding of interaction-driven changes in future transformation possibilities. A later practical goal is to translate validated findings into organisational frameworks, derived from the research rather than assumed in advance.

## Commitment

I would bring to IE an engineering and management background, extensive experience in complex technological environments, and a research agenda that has progressed from a broad theory of value toward a more fundamental and falsifiable problem concerning the evolution of future transformation possibilities.
