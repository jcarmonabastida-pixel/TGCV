The result supports the claim that the **specified computational operationalisation** of accessibility information can improve predictive performance in the tested environment, and that the observed improvement is not adequately explained by accessibility cardinality alone or by arbitrary permutation of accessibility structure.

## 3. What the result does not establish

The result does not by itself establish:

- universal validity of TGCV;
- domain-independent causal efficacy;
- empirical confirmation of every theoretical construct;
- superiority over all alternative representations;
- validity of any future operationalisation without independent testing.

## 4. Theoretical consequence

The empirical result strengthens the status of the TGCV hypothesis from a purely conceptual proposition to an **empirically supported, still provisional theoretical programme**.

The Core must not be retrospectively altered to fit the result. Any theoretical revision must be generated through the normal evidence → synthesis → hypothesis → validation process.
