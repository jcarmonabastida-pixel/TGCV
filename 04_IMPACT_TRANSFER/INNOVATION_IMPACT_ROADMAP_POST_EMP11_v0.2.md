# TGCV — Innovation & Impact Roadmap v0.2 (Post-EMP-1.1)

**Status:** Strategic roadmap — NOT FROZEN

## 1. Purpose

Translate validated or sufficiently supported scientific knowledge into potential practical impact without using transfer activity to inflate scientific claims.

## 2. Current scientific starting point

EMP-1.1 provides positive evidence for the predictive usefulness of a specified accessibility representation in the tested computational environment. The result is bounded: independent replication, cross-domain generalisation, trajectory effects and value creation remain open.

Therefore the roadmap distinguishes **scientific validation**, **technical maturation**, and **transfer readiness**.

## 3. Impact pathway

```text
Scientific question
      ↓
Evidence
      ↓
Theory
      ↓
Independent replication
      ↓
Boundary conditions
      ↓
Validated principles
      ↓
Reference architecture / methodology
      ↓
Demonstrators and pilots
      ↓
Adoption
      ↓
Practical value
```

No stage is considered automatically completed because the preceding stage exists.

## 4. Near-term priorities

### P1 — Independent replication
Design and execute an independently specified replication of the accessibility hypothesis.

**Gate:** pre-specified protocol and frozen operationalisation.

### P2 — External empirical validation
Test the relevant representation against an external dataset, domain or system where the semantics of transformations and accessibility can be independently justified.

**Gate:** reproducible evidence and explicit boundary conditions.

### P3 — Scientific consolidation
Update the Core, ontology, formal model and reference architecture only to the extent warranted by accumulated evidence.

**Gate:** traceable evidence-to-claim mapping.

### P4 — Technical maturation
Develop reusable implementation patterns and tooling around validated principles.

**Gate:** technical reproducibility and documented assumptions.

### P5 — Demonstration
Construct selected demonstrators or pilots in domains where changing future transformation possibilities is materially relevant.

**Gate:** explicit hypothesis, measurable outcome and separation between scientific and business claims.

## 5. Transfer channels

Potential channels include:

- research collaborations;
- industrial collaborations;
- doctoral/industrial PhD pathways;
- European research projects;
- innovation programmes;
- technology demonstrators;
- licensing or other exploitation mechanisms where appropriate.

These are **opportunity channels**, not commitments to particular organisations or institutions.

## 6. Programme-level principle

The Programme's ultimate purpose is to construct and deliver practical value, but practical value must emerge from validated knowledge. Industrial partners, universities and funders are therefore treated as possible participants in an evolving programme rather than as fixed destinations of the architecture.

## 7. Readiness levels

### Scientific readiness
Current: **SRL-2/3 — initial empirical support, replication required.**

### Technical readiness
Current: **early/provisional**; reusable architecture and methodology are being consolidated.

### Transfer readiness
Current: **exploratory**; no claim of validated industrial solution is made.

These labels are programme governance indicators, not external standard certifications.

## 8. Impact hypotheses

The programme will test whether knowledge of accessibility-changing interactions can improve:

- anticipation of future system possibilities;
- design of adaptive or generative systems;
- identification of strategically valuable interventions;
- technological roadmapping;
- decision-making under changing transformation spaces.

These are impact hypotheses, not established benefits.

## 9. Risks

**Risk 1 — Premature transfer.**
Mitigation: scientific evidence gates precede adoption claims.

**Risk 2 — Overfitting to one industrial use case.**
Mitigation: maintain domain-independent scientific core and test multiple contexts.

**Risk 3 — Confusing technical feasibility with value.**
Mitigation: define measurable value outcomes separately from architectural performance.

**Risk 4 — Partner dependency.**
Mitigation: maintain multiple possible routes to replication and impact.

**Risk 5 — Commercial pressure changing the theory.**
Mitigation: preserve the Programme Contract and scientific governance invariants.

## 10. Success indicators

- independent replication completed;
- boundary conditions documented;
- reusable principles identified;
- demonstrator hypotheses tested;
- measurable practical effects demonstrated where applicable;
- external collaborations established without compromising scientific independence;
- validated knowledge converted into reproducible methods and transferable artefacts.

## 11. Current decision

The Programme is **not yet in a scale-up/exploitation phase**. Its immediate strategic objective is to convert the positive first empirical signal into independently replicated, bounded and reusable scientific knowledge. Transfer should proceed in parallel only where it does not compromise that objective.
